# Devices and signed-in sessions (/docs/admin/look-after-it/devices-and-sessions)

This page shows your own devices and sessions. It is not a household list.

## ✍️ Steps [#️-steps]

1. Open **Settings**, **Data and privacy**, then **Signed-in devices**.
2. Under **Devices** you see paired robots, pods, TVs, phones, desktops and browsers, with the room and when each was last seen. A device that never connected says "never connected".
3. Tap **Remove** beside one, then **Yes, remove it**. It must be paired again to reconnect. **Keep it** cancels.
4. Under **Signed-in sessions**, tap **Sign out** beside another browser, then **Yes, sign it out**.

Your current session shows **This device** and has no sign-out button. Use **Log out** in the profile menu for that.

If nothing is paired, you see "No devices paired to your profile yet."
