# Find and share your files (/docs/user/everyday-use/find-and-share-your-files)

## ✍️ Steps [#️-steps]

1. In a chat, find a document MaiPai made. Tap the **More** button beside it.
2. Tap **Show in Library**. The **Library** opens.
3. Use **Search your Library**, **Whose** and **Kind** to find a file.
4. Tap **View details** on a row, or pick it under **Select a file**.

## 🤝 Share a file [#-share-a-file]

Under **Shared with**, pick a person or **The whole household**. Tap **Share**. To stop, tap **Unshare** beside the name.

You can only share files that are yours. A file shared with you shows under **Shared with me** in **Whose**.

## ⬇️ Keep a copy [#️-keep-a-copy]

In the file details, use the download button to save a copy.

If the Library is empty, it says "Nothing here yet. Stories, pictures and documents you make in chat are kept here."

## 🙋 Still need help? [#-still-need-help]

If a file you made is missing, ask the person who runs the house.
