# What never leaves (/docs/user/privacy-and-safety/what-never-leaves)

{/* generated-source: docs/user/privacy.md */}

These stay on your computer:

- Your conversations.
- Everything MaiPai remembers.
- Every profile in your household.
- Anything your family says. It is never used to train anything.

## 🚫 What MaiPai does not collect [#-what-maipai-does-not-collect]

- No usage stats.
- No crash reports.

## 🌐 Your browser [#-your-browser]

Your browser saves MaiPai's own screens so the app can open without internet. It never stores what you typed or what MaiPai remembers.

## 🕶️ Temporary chats [#️-temporary-chats]

An owner, admin or adult can mark a chat as temporary. Its turns are not added to history or memory. If you open it again, the old turns do not return. This covers MaiPai on this computer only.

## 💛 A child's hard moment [#-a-childs-hard-moment]

The adults get a note with the child's name only, never what was said. It cannot be turned off.

## ✍️ Check it [#️-check-it]

1. Tap **Privacy**.
2. Read **What leaves your house**. It names every exception.
