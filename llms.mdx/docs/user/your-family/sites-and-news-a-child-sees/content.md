# Sites and news a child sees (/docs/user/your-family/sites-and-news-a-child-sees)

This page is for owners and admins.

## ✍️ Steps [#️-steps]

1. Tap **Settings** and open the household settings for search.
2. Find **Blocked sites by person**.
3. Find the kind of site you want to change.
4. Tick or untick the box under **Kids**, **Teens** or **Adults**.
5. Your choice saves as you tick it.

Adult sites stay blocked for children and teens. Their boxes are greyed out and cannot be changed.

## 📰 News [#-news]

The news shelf has its own card where you choose which news sources show up. It sits beside the blocked sites card.

<Callout type="info">These lists are for the household. They do not read any chat.</Callout>
