## ✍️ Steps [#️-steps]

1. Open MaiPai Home in your web browser. Ask the person who runs the house for the address if you do not have it.
2. Pick your name from the list of people.
3. Under **Enter your PIN**, type your 4 digits. If you use a longer password, tap **Use a password instead**, type it in **Password**, then tap **Sign in**.
4. Tap **Chat** in the main menu.
5. Tap **New chat** if the page is not already empty.
6. Click the box that says **Send a message...** and type a question.
7. Press Enter, or tap the arrow button. The answer appears below your question.

<Shot id="chat-empty" />

<Callout type="info">Teens, children and guests may not need a PIN. Adults always do.</Callout>

## 🤔 If the first answer takes a while [#-if-the-first-answer-takes-a-while]

The first reply can be slow while MaiPai gets ready. You may see **Starting your AI…**. Wait a minute and it will answer.

## 🙋 Still need help? [#-still-need-help]

If your PIN is refused, see [If you cannot sign in](/docs/user/fix-a-problem/cannot-sign-in). If the first chat stays silent, see [If chat does not answer](/docs/user/fix-a-problem/chat-does-not-answer).
