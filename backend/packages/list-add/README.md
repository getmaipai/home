# Add to Shopping List

Ask MaiPai to add something to the household's shopping list.

## Try it

- "add milk to the shopping list"
- "add eggs to my shopping list"
- "put bread on the shopping list"

One shopping list for the whole household - everyone adds to the same
one.

## What it needs

Nothing to set up. Works as soon as it's installed.

## What it uses

Nothing leaves the house - your shopping list lives right here.

## Offline

Always works, even with no internet.
