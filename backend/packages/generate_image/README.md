# Make a Picture

Ask chat for a picture and it makes one on this computer. Nothing is sent
anywhere.

## Try it

- "make me a picture of a red fox in the snow"
- "draw a lighthouse at dusk"
- "design a logo for my bakery"

A card shows how far along it is, with a Stop button. When it is done the
picture appears in the chat. Tap Edit to change a part of it.

## Who can make pictures

Adults and teens can make pictures in a written chat on their own screen.
Children can make friendly cartoon pictures when the family has them enabled.
Spoken answers and shared screens never make pictures.

## What it needs

A picture model on this computer. An admin installs one in Settings, Pictures
and videos.

## Safety

Home blocks CSAM. The model decides whether to make other adult content and
may refuse under its own rules. Children and teens keep their age-specific
content filters. Home also applies age and surface access rules.

## Offline

Works with no internet once the model is installed.
