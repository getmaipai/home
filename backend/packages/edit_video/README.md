# Change a Video

Ask chat to change a video made here: make another take, remix it with a new look, camera move, sound or length, or make it again at Max. You can also continue a video from its last picture, cut a video, join your videos into one, or lay a spoken line or one of your songs over it. Every version is kept, so you can go back.

Adults can use this on their own written chat screen. Children, teens and guests cannot. A video model must be installed on this computer. Max needs an admin's OK. Nothing leaves your home.

Works offline after the video model is installed.
