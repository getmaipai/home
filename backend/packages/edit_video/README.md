# Change a Video

Ask chat to change a video made here: make another take, remix it with a new look, camera move, sound or length, or make it again at Max. You can also continue a video from its last picture, cut a video, join your videos into one, add a title card and rolling credits, reframe it, change its color and texture, apply a film look, add cinema bars, change its speed, add timed captions, or lay a spoken line or one of your songs over it. Ask for a transition when you join clips. Every version is kept, so you can go back.

You can also cut one or more videos to the beat of one of your songs. Choose how many beats each cut lasts and how much of the song to use.

Grade changes its color and texture.

Make a movie from a Production's finished shots.

Adults can use this on their own written chat screen. Children, teens and guests cannot. A video model must be installed on this computer. Max needs an admin's OK. Nothing leaves your home.

Works offline after the video model is installed.
