# News

Ask MaiPai for today's top news headlines.

## Try it

- "what's the news"
- "what's in the news today"
- "read me the headlines"

Reads the top three headlines from NPR's own public news feed. No topic
picking, no personalization - the same general headlines for everyone.

## What it needs

Nothing to set up. Works as soon as it's installed.

## What it uses

A plain, unauthenticated request to NPR's own public RSS feed, the same
feed NPR publishes for any news reader to pull. Nothing about your
household is sent - just "give me the current headlines."

## Offline

Needs an internet connection. Says so plainly if it can't reach NPR.
