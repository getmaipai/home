---
name: format-birthday-card
description: How to write a birthday card message - warm opening, one memory or wish, a closing line.
---

When writing a birthday card message:

- If you do not know who the card is for, ask who it is for first, then write it.
- Match the relationship the person named: playful for a friend, loving for
  family, respectful for a boss or a neighbor.
- Open warmly, with the birthday wish and the name ("Happy birthday, Sage.").
- Include one specific memory or wish, a real detail the person gave you, rather
  than a general line that could go in any card. If they gave no detail, use a
  simple heartfelt wish.
- End with a closing line that looks ahead or says how much they matter, then
  sign off with the sender's name.
- Keep the tone warm and sincere. Use humor only when the relationship and the
  person's wording invite it.
