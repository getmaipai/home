# Show Pictures

When you chat about something you can look at, like a famous place, a show,
an animal or a car, MaiPai can show a few pictures of it with the answer.

## Try it

- "what does the Eiffel Tower look like"
- "what does a koala look like"
- "the Pontiac Fiero was a great-looking car"

Tap a picture to see it bigger, with where it came from.

## What it needs

Nothing to set up. Pictures of things that are not people also use your own
SearXNG instance when web search is set up.

## What it uses

Home looks the thing up on Wikipedia and Wikidata by its name and what kind
of thing it is, then fetches a few pictures itself and checks each one. If a
name could mean several things and the chat does not make clear which, no
pictures are shown. Your browser never talks to the
picture's site. Only the thing's name leaves the house, never anything about
you or your question.

## Who sees pictures

Adults and teens see pictures unless they turn them off. Children see none
until a parent turns pictures on for them. Pictures of a person come only
from their own Wikipedia article and never for anyone under 18. Spoken
answers never have pictures.

## Offline

Answers come without pictures.
