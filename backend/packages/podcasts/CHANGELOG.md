# Changelog

All notable changes to the Podcasts package, in [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/) format.

## [0.2.12] - 2026-10-28

- The front page opens on a rotating Featured shows banner of six top shows; each opens its show page.

## [0.2.11] - 2026-10-28

- Made show details readable below the artwork and polished episode dates, descriptions and cover thumbnails.
- Removed the misleading follow-to-play episode overlay.

## [0.2.10] - 2026-10-28

- Added show detail pages and directory feed previews; preview episodes prompt the person to follow before playback.

## [0.2.9] - 2026-10-28

- Added cached teen-safe episode summaries and takeaways when a transcript is available.

## [0.2.8] - 2026-10-28

- Added private podcast snips with notes, plus a Snips shelf on Saved.
- Fixed dashboard subtitles and added search refinement to Search.

## [0.2.7] - 2026-10-28

- Made Podcasts home a dashboard with Up Next, new episodes, shows and a top shows shelf; moved search onto the home page and removed Find shows navigation.

## [0.2.5] - 2026-10-28

- Publisher transcripts are fetched through the podcast address guard; episodes without one can queue Stack transcription, and Transcript search finds phrases in stored transcripts and opens at the matching time.

## [0.2.2] - 2026-10-28

- The pages match the hub's own copy again (the cinema tone is gone from the page headers).

## [0.2.3] - 2026-10-28

- Added private listening stats and a year in review from each person's own podcast progress.

## [0.2.1] - 2026-10-28

- Filters: save a filter of episodes by unplayed or in progress, saved on the hub, length, shows and how recently released, and see what matches. A filter is private to the person who made it and only narrows what that person can already see.

## [0.2.0] - 2026-10-27

- Find shows: search Apple's podcast directory or browse its top list, and add a show with a tap (an adult admin adds; the page is off until the household turns on Find podcasts online). A child never sees it.

## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-17

- First version: Listen now, Shows, Episodes, Queue, Saved and Listen (the player with Add to queue, Save on the hub and Bookmark). Stream by default, save on request or by keep rules; a child sees only child-cleared episodes.

## [0.2.4] - 2026-10-28

- The saved-filter pickers share one data id, `filter-options` (scope `filters` or `shows`), so the manifest stays within its 16 data ids.

## [0.2.6] - 2026-10-28

- The Find page says where to turn on the podcast directory when nothing shows.
