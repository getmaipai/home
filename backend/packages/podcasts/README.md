# Podcasts

Listen to the shows your home follows, in a dark look like Apple Podcasts. New episodes play right away from the show's website. Press Save on the hub and a copy plays with the internet off.

## The Podcasts app

Open Podcasts from the app list. The tabs are Listen now, Shows, Episodes, Queue and Saved.

- Listen now has Up next, Continue listening, New episodes and your shows.
- Press a picture to play it. When you come back later, it starts where you stopped.
- Add to queue, Save on the hub and Bookmark are under the player.
- A child sees only the episodes the family cleared for children. An explicit episode never shows for a child.

## What it needs

A show added by an admin. Nothing else.

## What it uses

Home asks each show's website for its list of episodes, about once an hour, and asks only for what changed. No name, device or cookie goes with it. An episode you do not save plays straight from the show's website on your own screen.
