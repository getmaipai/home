# Household news feeds

The list of news feeds behind the "Household news" choice on the News shelf.

## What it gives you

Headlines from: NPR News, BBC News, PBS NewsHour headlines, NASA news releases.

It adds no questions to ask. It only feeds the News shelf, and the News
shelf feeds the "what's the news" answer.

## Turn it on or off

Open Settings, then News shelf. Switch the shelf on, then tick "Household news"
for each kind of person (children, teens, adults) who should see it. A
person only sees a list that is offered to their kind of person.

## What it uses

A plain request for each feed file, about every 30 minutes at most, and
only while nobody is using MaiPai. Your home's internet address goes with
it. No name, device or cookie does. MaiPai reads the feed file and never
opens the articles.

## Offline

Needs an internet connection to fetch. Headlines already fetched stay on
the shelf until they are old enough to be cleared.
