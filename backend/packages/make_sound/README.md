# Make a Sound

Ask chat to make a sound effect or an ambient sound from your words, such as rain on a tin roof or a door creaking.

Adults can use this on their own written chat screen. Children, teens and guests cannot. A music model must be installed on this computer. Nothing leaves your home. Home blocks CSAM; the model decides whether to make other adult content. Child and teen content filters remain in place. Uploaded audio cannot be used.

Works offline after the music model is installed.
