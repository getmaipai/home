# Lights On

Ask MaiPai to turn on a light.

## Try it

- "turn on the living room light"
- "turn on the kitchen light"
- "turn on the bedroom lights"

Say which room.

## What it needs

Home Assistant, connected under Settings > Integrations, with that
room's light already set up there.

## What it uses

Talks directly to your own Home Assistant instance on your own network.
Nothing leaves the house.

## Offline

Needs Home Assistant to be reachable on your network.
