# Make a Video From My Pictures

Attach pictures of people, objects and places, then ask for a short video that uses them: "Make a video of the girl in the red jacket in front of this building using my pictures."

Home builds one reference sheet from your pictures (people and objects cut out on white, places kept as they are), shows it in the chat before anything is rendered, then makes an 8 second video from the sheet at the Max level. If a picture is already a clean sheet of several separate panels, Home uses it as it is. The sheet stays with the video, so "same sheet, but make her walk away" makes a new video without redoing the cut-outs.

For a film or commercial of 10 seconds or more, use Make a Film instead.

This is for adults on their own written chat screen. Video must be on, Max must be installed and an admin must have allowed it. One video is made at a time. Nothing leaves your home.
