# Make a Film

Ask for a film, movie or short with several shots. Describe the story, scene or script in your own words. You can name a look, ask for 4 to 12 shots, and choose saved characters. Home plans it first. Check the plan and press Go to start the film, unless you asked Home to skip that check.
You can also ask for a faceless film: a narrated film of pictures with slow zoom and pan, with no character clips.

Films are for adults on their own written chat screen. Video must be on for this home. The film runs in the background while chat continues. Nothing leaves your home.

Works offline after the models are installed.
