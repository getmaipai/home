# Make a Band

Press Create a band like this while a song plays in Music. Home opens a chat, invents a new made-up band with the feel of that song, and makes its portrait, its logo and a first single.

The song only sets the feel: its genre, era and mood. The band's name, members, story and words are always new. Adults can use this on their own written chat screen. Children, teens and guests cannot. A music model, and a picture model for the portrait and logo, must be installed on this computer.

When the song matched MusicBrainz, Home reads that artist's public genre tags once (see the Music package). Nothing else leaves your home.
