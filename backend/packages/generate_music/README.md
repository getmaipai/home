# Make a Song

Ask chat to make a song or an instrumental from your words.

Adults can use this on their own written chat screen. Children, teens and guests cannot. A music model must be installed on this computer. Nothing leaves your home. Copying a real voice and sexual songs about children are refused. Uploaded audio cannot be used.

Works offline after the music model is installed.
