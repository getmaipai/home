# Make a Song

Ask chat to make a song or an instrumental from your words.

Adults can use this on their own written chat screen. Children, teens and guests cannot. A music model must be installed on this computer. Nothing leaves your home. Home blocks CSAM; the model decides whether to make other adult content, including voice imitation. Child and teen content filters remain in place. Uploaded audio cannot be used.

Works offline after the music model is installed.
