# Timers and alarms

Ask MaiPai to set a timer, or to wake you at a time.

## Try it

- "set a timer for ten minutes"
- "set a timer for an hour"
- "set a timer for 30 seconds"

Say a number and a unit: seconds, minutes, or hours.

## Alarms

- "wake me at 6:30"
- "set an alarm for 7 tomorrow called gym"
- "cancel my alarm at 6:30"
- "what alarms do I have"

Say the time you want. You can add a short name, like "gym". An alarm that
repeats ("on weekdays") shows up in the Clock app too. When it is time,
MaiPai sends you an in-app notice. A robot can also tell you if that notice
is turned on for robots. An alarm is yours alone, so MaiPai needs to know
who is asking before it sets one.

## What it needs

Nothing to set up. Works as soon as it's installed.

## What it uses

Nothing leaves the house - the timer runs right here.

## Offline

Always works, even with no internet.
