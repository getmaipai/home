# Make an Album

Ask for an album in chat, or press Make an album on a band's card. Home writes a title and a blurb, plans the songs, makes a cover and starts the first song. The album shows as a card with its songs, and you can change it in chat or on the card.

Adults can use this on their own written chat screen. Children, teens and guests cannot. A music model, and a picture model for the cover, must be installed on this computer. Nothing leaves your home.
