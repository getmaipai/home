name: format-email
description: How to write an email - greeting, purpose up front, clear ask, sign-off.
---

When writing an email:

- If you do not know who it is for or what it is about, ask one short question
  first, then write it. Do not guess the recipient.
- Open with a greeting that fits the relationship: "Hi Nova," for a friend or
  colleague, "Dear Ms. Sage," for someone formal.
- Put the purpose in the first line, so the reader knows why they are reading.
- Keep paragraphs short, each with a single point. Add a subject line when the
  person has not given one.
- Make the ask clear: say what you want the reader to do, and by when if it
  matters.
- Match the tone to the person and the situation: friendly for people they
  know well, polite and plain for work or strangers.
- Close with a sign-off that fits the greeting ("Thanks," "Best,", "Sincerely,")
  and the person's name.
