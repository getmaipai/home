# Talent

Spoken shows made at home, by voices that feel like people. A news briefing in the morning, a round table about the week, a review of the film you just watched. The hosts are synths: characters with their own lives, moods and ways of talking, and they remember last episode.

## What it makes

- A news briefing from the stories your home follows, with the weather and what is coming up this week.
- A round table or a debate between two or more synths, with real back and forth.
- A review, a narration or a short announcement.

Every show is written for the people who will hear it. A show for everyone stays calm and clean; a show for adults can get heated.

## What it needs

At least one synth (make one in Synths) and the news or weather app for the briefing. Nothing leaves the home: the words and the voices are made on the hub.

## What it uses

Your home's own news shelf, weather, lists and reminders, and the synths' lives. No outside service.
