# Convert

Ask MaiPai to convert between units: length, weight, temperature, volume.

## Try it

- "convert 5 miles to km"
- "convert 100 fahrenheit to celsius"
- "convert 2 cups to ml"

Say the number, the unit, then "to" or "in", then the unit you want.
Currency isn't a unit table, so "convert 5 dollars to euros" won't work -
that needs its own package with live exchange rates, not built yet.

## What it needs

Nothing to set up. Works as soon as it's installed.

## What it uses

Nothing leaves the house - every conversion happens right here.

## Offline

Always works, even with no internet.
