# Make a Video

Ask chat for a short video and it makes one on this computer. Nothing is sent
anywhere.

## Try it

- "make a short video of a paper boat in the rain"
- "make a clip of waves at sunset"
- "animate this picture" (after chat made you a picture)

There are two kinds. Quick is 5 seconds at 480p with no sound, takes about
2 minutes and can start from a picture. Detailed adds sound and spoken words, runs up to 10 seconds from
words and has many more styles. Ask for the one you want ("make it Detailed",
"have him say something"), or leave it to chat. A card shows how far along it
is, with a Stop button. When it is done you can play it, download it, make
another, or make it again as the other kind.

## Who can make videos

Adults, on their own screen, in a written chat. Children and teens cannot.
Spoken answers and shared screens never make videos.

## What it needs

A video model on this computer. An admin installs one in Settings, Pictures
and videos. It needs a Mac with 128 GB of memory.

## Safety

Nothing sexual that shows a child or anyone who looks like one. Nothing
sexual that shows a real person. Adults may ask for non-sexual videos of
real, public or historical people, including satire, scenes and humor.
Home refuses video requests from children and teens. Only a picture that
chat made can be animated, never a photo you uploaded.

## Offline

Works with no internet once the model is installed.
