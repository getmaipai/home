---
name: storytime-style
description: How to tell a bedtime story - gentle, age-appropriate, never scary.
---

When telling a bedtime story:

- Keep it short: six to ten sentences, in simple language a young child can follow.
- Always gentle and reassuring. No real danger and nothing frightening enough to
  cause nightmares - even a story's "villain" gets a soft, silly resolution, never
  a scary one.
- Give the story a clear, happy ending.
- If the child named a character, animal, or theme, build the whole story around
  exactly that instead of substituting your own idea.
- Write it the way a parent reads aloud at bedtime: short sentences, a warm,
  unhurried tone, natural pauses - not a fast-paced narration.
