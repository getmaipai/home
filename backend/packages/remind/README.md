# Reminders

Ask MaiPai to remind you about something later.

## Try it

- "remind me at 6 to call Nadia"
- "remind me tomorrow at noon to walk the dog"
- "remind me in an hour to check the oven"

Say what you want to be reminded about, and when. A single time unit
("in 90 minutes") works more reliably than a compound one ("in an hour
and a half"). Recurring reminders ("every morning") aren't supported
yet - only a one-time reminder.

## What it needs

Nothing to set up. Works as soon as it's installed.

## What it uses

Nothing leaves the house - the time you say is worked out right here,
and the reminder itself never goes anywhere else.

## Offline

Always works, even with no internet.
