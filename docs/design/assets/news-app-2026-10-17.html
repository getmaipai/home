River trail reopens after two years of repairs, with a new bridge and night lighting

Published today, 5:30·4 outlets covering this·6 min read
ListenSaveKey pointsOpen on site
Key points, written on this hub: the trail is open end to end; the bridge replaces the 1970s crossing; lighting is solar; night access ends at 11.

The river trail reopened on Friday morning after two years of repairs, the city said. The new bridge replaces the crossing closed in 2024, and the whole 11-kilometre route is lit at night for the first time.

Crews fitted 300 solar lamps along the path. The lights come on at dusk and switch off at 11, when the trail closes. The ferry terminal end gains a bike-repair stand and a water fountain.

Three parks connect through the route, and the city expects most people to reach it by the number 14 bus, which gains evening service next month.
