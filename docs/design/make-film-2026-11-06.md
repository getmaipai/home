## 1. What exists

- One piece at a time: pictures, clips from a picture, music, saved characters, Productions with a shot list, and the `edit_video` `film`
  change that joins a Production's finished shot clips with transitions, a title card and cast credits.
- Missing: the step before and between. Nobody plans the shots, makes the stills and clips for each shot with the same characters,
  scores it and puts it together. A person has to do each of those by hand in chat.

## 2. The feature

A chat tool `make_film` and a Create entry "Film". The person gives an idea, a script or a scene in words, and optionally a look (photoreal
film, cartoon, a named film grade), a length (4 to 12 shots, about 20 to 90 seconds), and saved characters or places to use. Home makes
the film and puts it in the Library and on a Production. Adults on their own written screen only (the same surface as `generate_video`).

The steps, each stored on the Production so a failed film can resume at the step it stopped on:

1. **Plan.** The household chat model writes a plan as JSON checked against a schema: title, cast (name, kind, a short locked description),
   shots (framing, still description, motion description, seconds, an optional spoken line, the transition into the next shot), a music
   brief and credits. A bad plan is repaired once, then refused with a plain line. The planner prompt carries the rules in section 4. The planner uses the engine's own JSON-schema output (rule 2); it does not hand-parse the reply, and the repair is one more schema-checked call.
2. **Cast.** Each cast member is a saved subject (reused when the person named one, made when new). A new subject gets a model sheet:
   one 16:9 picture with the character from front, three-quarter, side and back (PRODUCTION-VIDEO-02), and its locked description.
3. **Stills.** One picture per shot from the picture engine. For cartoon and illustrated looks the sheet is the base of a picture edit so
   the design holds across shots; for photoreal looks the locked description is repeated in every shot (section 4).
4. **Clips.** One clip per shot from its still through `generate_video`, one at a time, in the household's resource order (chat first, then
   pictures, then video), pausing while chat is working. The tier is the person's own limit.
5. **Music.** One instrumental score from the plan's brief through the music engine, after the clips so the two never wait on each other
   (STACK-GEN-WAIT-01).
6. **Assemble.** One `media.edit` job: transitions as planned, a film look and widescreen bars when asked, a title card, rolling credits
   and the music bed. The cartoon and photoreal samples used exactly this chain.
7. **Keep.** The finished film is a Library video with provenance; its file id is added to the Production's finished cuts. Every clip,
   still and the music stay on the Production, so any one shot can be redone and the film assembled again.

Status uses the existing media status surfaces (stage and percent on the card, "waiting for memory" shown calmly), not a new status UI.

## 4. Rules learned from the samples (these go into the planner prompt and the picture step)

1. Name what is there, never what is absent. Prompts that said "no other boats" made the model add boats. Describe the empty horizon
   ("a flat empty gray sea meeting a plain hazy sky") without the word for the forbidden thing.
2. Lock identity in words, every shot: colour, markings, clothing and "what is never true" in each shot's description. In photoreal
   looks a sheet used as the base made the model drift; the repeated sentence held.
3. Name the film stock look, not a grade: "1975 35mm, overcast, muted, heavy grain" matched an original film; a warm sepia grade did not.
4. Frame like the scene: a character facing the camera with the creature behind the shoulder, the dolly-zoom close-up, the retreating
   wide shot. The planner is given a short list of named shot types with their descriptions.
5. A person's name is a prompt, not a face. Home does not refuse a real person's name (rule 0); the model decides what it draws.
6. One engine job at a time per kind; music before or after the clips, never beside them.

## 5. Surfaces and access

- Tool `make_film` in a new package `make_film` (conditional offer, the `videosAllowed` gate). Rule 1 budget: it is offered only on a turn
  whose words ask for a film, a movie or a short with several shots (the model decides; no word list), and it takes the slot of the retired
  `make_storyboard`. Worst case on that turn: four video tools inside the cap of 16, the music set shrinks first. Its card is the kit
  `JobProgress` element (its `stages` prop draws Plan, Cast, Stills, Clips, Music, Assemble) and the finished film in the kit
  `VideoPlayer`; no Home `*Card` (rules 9 and 9b); a missing prop goes into `commons` first. Actions: Redo a shot, Change the
  music, Change the look, Make longer (more shots appended).
- Child and teen lanes: not offered (video is adult-only today). Nothing here loosens rule 0 or any age gate.
- Rule 10: every still, every clip and the assembled film pass the existing finished-item CSAM check before they are kept or shown; the
  check fails closed (a check that cannot run keeps the item hidden and the step marked failed).
- Privacy: every step runs on the hub; no outbound connection is added.
