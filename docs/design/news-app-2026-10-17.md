# The News app: a Today feed, channels, My Mix and a reader on the household's own shelf (2026-10-17)

Jesse's ask, 2026-10-17: a News app "modern like Apple News": a Today feed with a hero story and an editorial card hierarchy,
topic and source channels, following, My Mix personalization computed locally, saved stories, big type, image-forward cards,
a reader view with clean article text, read-aloud through the local TTS, summaries by the local model, notifications per
`docs/NOTIFICATIONS.md`, offline reading, Home widgets and TV tiles, phone first plus desktop and TV, dark mode. Everything
keeps the promise: the hub fetches only feeds the household chose, nothing tracks anyone, no third party personalizes, and
every picture comes through the hub. Design note only; no code. The functional reference is Loki Doki's News page
(archived, section 3); the architect verdict is at the end. Mockup: [assets/news-app-2026-10-17.html](assets/news-app-2026-10-17.html)
(opened and judged at 1280 and 390, light and dark; section 6).

## 2. Purpose and audience

News is one person's window on what the household's chosen sources published: "what happened today, what I follow, what I
saved," read in a clean view, listened to, or summarized, without leaving the house. It is not a web browser (the reader is
the destination; the publisher's site is one tap away for teens and adults), not chat (the `news` tool keeps answering "what's
the news"), and not a search engine (global Search finds shelf stories like everything else, rule S2).

| Viewer | Sees | Does not see |
|---|---|---|
| Adult | Today, My Mix, Following, Saved, every channel of the lists ticked for adults, the reader with "Open on site", Listen, Key points | Another person's follows, saves, reads or mix (NO-CROSS-USER-VISIBILITY-01) |
| Teen | The same, from the lists ticked for teens; items pass the teen floor at release; summaries written at the teen band | Nothing extra |
| Child (kid mode), **only if Jesse accepts decision 1 of section 21** | Today and Following from the Kids' lists only, chronological, the reader with the feed's own text or a cleaned fetch, Listen; Saved; every sentence through the child output gate | My Mix (nothing is inferred about a child), "Open on site" and every outbound link (MINOR-SEARCH-01h), Key points unless the summary passed the child floor. Until he rules, every page carries `min_role: teen` (section 7) and a child keeps getting news only through the model (MINOR-SEARCH-01g) |
| Shared screen (TV, pod) | Today from household data only (the `household_today` data id, which names no person), in a household with no child profile; a household with a child profile shows no News on a shared screen until Jesse rules on decision 1 (section 15) | Anything per person |

A guest gets no News app: the role ladder ranks `guest` below `child`, and a `teen` page floor excludes both. A guest's band
also resolves to adult when there is no age signal, so offering a guest the app would hand an unknown viewer the adult lists.

Rule 0 holds throughout: the app adds no gate and removes none. Every list a person reads comes from `releaseShelfItems`
for their band, the one choke point, and every string the app shows passes the same floor the `news` tool's answer does.

## 4. The product in one paragraph

News is a package app (`news`, `kind: plugin`, the same package that answers in chat) with six schema pages drawn by kit
parts: Today, My Mix, Following, Saved, Channel and Story. Its data comes from the shelf the hub already keeps, through the
band-gated release path, as story clusters once KS-06 lands and as items until then. The hub classifies each item into a fixed
topic set with the embed role, fetches a story's picture from the feed's own image address the first time a screen shows it
into a capped same-origin cache, writes Key points for the top stories with the local chat model on the idle queue, and ranks per person from that
person's own follows and reading, stored as records only they can see. The reader is the one reader every page gets; Listen
is the local TTS through the shell's player; notifications are two declared passive events; offline is the shelf itself.

## 5. Information architecture

```
/apps/news                 Today        (index; nav: section tab)
/apps/news/mix             My Mix       (nav; adults and teens; min_role teen)
/apps/news/following       Following    (nav; Topics and Sources tabs; Follow controls)
/apps/news/saved           Saved        (nav; adults and teens)
/apps/news/channel         Channel      (not nav; state.kind + state.key: one topic or one source)
/apps/news/story           Story        (not nav; state.id: the reader)
gear                       Settings     (contributes.settings_area: the shelf matrix and feed boxes for admins; My Mix,
                                         digest and reader choice for the person; S4)
```

Sections are the app's `nav: true` pages, drawn by the shell as in-app tabs on wide screens and in the phone drawer (S1, S2);
the app draws no chrome and puts nothing of its own in the shell's tab strip. Channel and Story take page state: the `navigate`
action carries `state` (an additive field, section 7) so a card can open `channel` with `{kind: "topic", key: "science"}` or
`story` with `{id}`. Global Search indexes stories (KS-06 tier L) and opens `story`. Breadcrumbs become the phone's back
button (UI.md).

Every page is built from these nodes and nothing else (the exact list, so the schema can be checked against it):

| Page | Nodes |
|---|---|
| Today, My Mix | `page`, `section`, `answer_blocks` (kind `story_feed`, with its `actions` map), `empty_state` |
| Following | `page`, `tabs` (Topics, Sources), two `list` nodes (bound to the `topics` and `sources` data ids) with `item_media_field` and `row_toggle` (Follow), `empty_state` |
| Channel | `page`, `picker` (the person's channels; a topic or source arrives in state from a card), `answer_blocks` (kind `story_feed`, layout `channel`, header toggle through `actions`) |
| Saved | `page`, `list` (bound to the `saved` data id) with `item_media_field`, `batch` (Remove), `empty_state` |
| Story | `page`, `answer_blocks` (kind `article`, with its `actions` map), `empty_state` |

`tabs`, `item_media_field`, `row_toggle`, the `actions` map on `answer_blocks` and `navigate.state` are the five additive schema
changes (section 7). The two block kinds are answer blocks, not nodes, so the Home widget draws the same `story_feed` block
through the same `answer_blocks` path (home-page section 7).

Topics are a fixed set in `spec/news/topics.json` (section 7): `world`, `local`, `business`, `technology`, `science`, `health`,
`sports`, `entertainment`, `environment`, `kids`. A source channel is a `Feed` (one per feed address). Following is per person.

### My Mix (`mix`)

The same layout as Today with the ranking of section 9 and a one-line explanation at the top: "Ranked from what you follow and
read on this hub. Nothing leaves the house." "Reset My Mix" is the block's page menu entry (the `reset_mix` action in the
`actions` map, a `confirm` then a `call`). Page `min_role: teen`, and a child never gets it even if decision 1 opens the
app to children; a teen or adult may turn it off (`news.my_mix`), which hides the tab.

### Following (`following`)

A `tabs` node with two peer views (UI.md): Topics and Sources. Each is a `list` node: `item_media_field` (a topic's lucide icon
name; a source's favicon through `favicons.ts`), label, subtitle "12 stories today · from 3 sources", and `row_toggle` (Follow
or Following, bound to the row's `followed` field, calling `follow`). Sources are the feeds visible to the person's band,
grouped by list ("Household news feeds", "Kids' news feeds", "Added by your home"). Tapping a row opens Channel.

### Channel (`channel`)

A `picker` of the person's channels (pre-set from the card's state), then the `story_feed` block in its `channel` layout,
whose header carries the name, the Following toggle (the `follow` action), "N sources" for a topic or the publisher and feed
address for a source, then one hero, featured, rows. A source channel for an adult also shows "Open site" in the header menu.

### Saved (`saved`)

Thumbnail rows, newest saved first, with a "Saved for offline" `Badge` once the body and picture are cached; the kit's batch
selection with Remove (UI.md "Batch actions"). Empty: "Stories you save stay here, with the text kept on this hub."

## 7. Spec records (spec first, `commons/spec`, additive)

One definition per thing. The three existing records are extended; three person-scoped records are new. Every record carries
`id`, `provenance` and the `hlc` clock stamp (platform principle 3).

**`Feed` (existing), additive:** `kind` (`rss`, `atom`, `json`; JSON Feed joins RSS and Atom), `site_url`, `icon_ref` (the favicon
cache key), `topics_hint[]` (a list package may say a feed is `science`), `list_id`, `alerts` (reserved for FEED-ALERTS-01).

**`NewsItem` (existing), additive:** `topics[]` (from the fixed set; empty when the classifier is unsure), `image_url` (the feed's
own picture address, from `media:content`, `media:thumbnail`, an image enclosure or the first picture in the feed's content,
never from a fetched page), `image_ref` (the cache key once a screen asked for it, null until then), `content_ref` (the
feed-carried or fetched `ArticleBlock` list in `knowledge.db`, null until needed), `content_source` (`feed`, `fetched`,
`archived`, `teaser`), `reading_s` (estimated from the text), `author`.

**`NewsStory` (existing), additive:** `lead_item_id` (the item the hero shows: the earliest from the publisher with the most
items, with a picture when any item has one), `topics[]` (the union of its items'), `image_ref`, `summary` (Key points text),
`summary_band` (`child`, `teen`, `adult`: the band it was written for), `summary_at`.

**`NewsInterest` (new, person scope):** `id` (`interest-`), `person_id`, `kind` (`topic` or `source`), `key` (a topic id or a
`feed-` id), `followed` (boolean, the person's own tap), `affinity` (0 to 1, computed, section 9), `inferred` (true when only
the affinity holds it), `updated_at`, `hlc`, `provenance`, `source` (`hub`). One row per person and channel. Never readable by
another person or an admin page (rule 6a); cleared by "Reset My Mix" (inferred rows) or Unfollow; a person's forget rule removes
them all.

**`NewsSave` (new, person scope):** `id` (`save-`), `person_id`, `item_id`, `story_id`, `canonical_url`, `title`, `publisher`,
`saved_at`, `offline` (true once `content_ref` and `image_ref` are cached), `hlc`, `provenance`. A saved item's content and
picture are exempt from the shelf prune while the save exists.

**`NewsRead` (new, person scope):** `id` (`read-`), `person_id`, `item_id`, `story_id`, `opened_at`, `read_s`, `finished`
(scrolled past 80 percent or Listen reached the end), `feedback` (`more`, `less`, null), `hlc`, `provenance`. Pruned at 90
days; feeds the affinity only; never shown anywhere but the person's own dimmed cards.

**`spec/news/topics.json` (new):** `{version, topics: [{id, label, description, bands: [...]}]}`; `kids` is offered to the
child band and `politics` is not a topic (it folds into `world` and `local`, section 21). The description is what the embed
role compares against.

**Settings keys (`spec/settings/keys.json`), new:** `news.my_mix` (person, boolean, default on; not offered to the child band),
`news.reader_open` (person, select `reader` or `site`, default `reader`; locked to `reader` for a child), `news.digest`
(person, boolean, default on for adults and teens, off for a child and set by a parent), `news.images` (household, boolean,
default on), `news.image_cache_mb` (household, number, default 512), `news.full_text_prefetch` (household, boolean, default
off; its help says the request budget in plain words, section 8). The existing shelf keys are unchanged.

## 8. Ingestion: pictures, text, topics, request budget

The poller is KS-05a's and does not change. The app adds one idle-queue step behind it and two foreground steps, each bounded,
each with a typed health event and no person in any log line. A "tick" below is one run of `runShelfTick` (every
`news.poll_interval_s`, 30 minutes by default, never under the 15-minute floor).

1. **Topics** (`news.shelf.topics`, after each poll): each new item's title and summary are embedded once with the `embed`
   role (the same 256-d int8 vectors KS-06 clusters with, so one embedding serves both) and compared with the topic
   descriptions; the top topic above the threshold, and a second when within 0.05 of it. A feed's `topics_hint` adds its topic.
   No word list reads the text (rule 1's spirit; a classifier here decides a shelf label, never a turn). Stored on the item.
2. **Pictures** (foreground, only while `news.images` is on): nothing is fetched until a person's screen shows the story. A
   card's picture is `GET /api/plugins/news/images/:item?w=480|1280`; on the first request for an item the hub fetches
   `image_url` once through `guardedFetch` (SSRF guard, 10-second timeout, 8 MB cap, one request per host at a time through the
   existing limiter, back-off on the first 429, the hub's honest user agent), resizes to 1280 px and 480 px wide (the kit's
   `AspectRatio` draws 16:9 and 4:3 crops from the same file), stores WebP under the data directory with the `answerImages`
   cache pattern, LRU to `news.image_cache_mb`, 14 days unused, and serves it with `max-age` and ETag; every later request is the
   cache. The publisher's address never appears in an `<img src>`. Budget: what one screen shows (a Today page asks for at most
   the hero, four featured, a shelf of up to eight and twenty rows), at a person's pace, which is the feeds plan's "pictures
   loaded through Home's image proxy only when someone opens the item" (its line 23) read as "when someone's screen shows it";
   no background step touches a picture host, so no exception is needed. A page is never fetched for its picture.
3. **Text** (on open, foreground): opening a story whose item has no `content_ref` runs the one reader: the feed's own content
   when it carried the article (NPR, NASA, Science News Explores do), otherwise one fetch of `canonical_url` through
   `retrieval/fetchCache.ts` (news host TTL 24 hours, `robots_ok` honoured) and the landed `extractFullArticle()` (called with
   `allowLinks` false for a child, `allowImages` from `news.images`, and a news link mode that keeps outbound links as plain
   `href`s instead of kiwix routes: one additive option, no second extractor), producing the `ArticleBlock` list;
   `contentQuality.ts`'s rules (ported) decide teaser versus article (400 characters, keep the better copy, remember a failure
   30 minutes, Try again bypasses). A paywalled page keeps its summary, marked "subscriber article"; a 404 or 410 for a teen or
   adult may use KS-07's Wayback availability fallback; a block or a bot challenge stops that class of traffic (quiet mode).
   Full-text prefetch in the background runs only while `news.full_text_prefetch` is on, under the owner's written exception of
   2026-09-24 (one fetch per item ever, one every 30 seconds and 150 a day per site, robots respected), and only for items on
   a person's followed channels or in the Today top set; the setting's help states those numbers.
4. **Saved** (at save time): the item's text (one fetch if needed) and picture are cached and pinned (`NewsSave.offline`).

Dedupe and clustering are KS-05's canonical-URL dedupe and KS-06's `cluster_story` and `fold_wire_copy`, unchanged. Until
KS-06 lands, every item is its own story with `publisher_count: 1` and the app draws items; the data routes return the same
shape either way, so the app does not change when KS-06 arrives.

Children: a child's items are only those from feeds whose `band` is `child` and that passed the ingest floor and the release
floor (unchanged). A child's fetched text passes the same cleaning as a child's search page (rule 7); a fetch that fails it
shows the feed's own text or nothing, never the raw page.

## 9. Ranking

One function on the hub, `rankStories(stories, person, mode)`, tested per band and per mode. Candidates are the stories whose
lead items `releaseShelfItems` released for the person's band in the last 48 hours.

- **Today** (`mode: today`): `score = freshness × coverage × picture × channel`, with `freshness = 2^(-age_h / 8)`,
  `coverage = 1 + log2(publisher_count)`, `picture = 1.15` when the story's item carries an `image_url` (an image-forward feed
  is the brief; a text-only story still wins when fresh and widely covered; the picture itself is fetched only when the card
  shows), `channel = 1.5` when the story's topic or source is followed. Hero: the top story with a picture. Featured: the next four with at most one per publisher and two per topic.
  Rows: the rest newest first. A child's Today uses the same function with `channel` from follows only and nothing inferred.
- **My Mix** (`mode: mix`, teens and adults): the same with `channel = 1 + affinity` for the story's topic and source, and
  `feedback`: a story whose topic or source got "Less like this" in the last 30 days scores `× 0.5`. Diversity caps stay, so
  one source never fills the page.
- **Affinity** (per `NewsInterest`): recomputed nightly and after each read from the person's own `NewsRead` rows with a
  30-day half-life: an open counts 1, a finish 2, a save 3, "More like this" 3, "Less like this" minus 3; normalized to 0 to 1
  across the person's channels. Explicit follows never move; "Reset My Mix" deletes the inferred rows and the reads' feedback.
- **Shared screens**: `mode: today` through `household_today` at the household band, no follows, no affinity; nothing in a
  household with a child profile until decision 1b (section 15).

No model call ranks anything; the numbers are measured in NEWS-05's bench (a held-out week of a seeded shelf, the top ten's
source and topic diversity, and a replay of a persona's reads) before the weights are called final.

## 10. The reader

The one reader, already landed for Reference, not a second one: `extractFullArticle()` turns the feed's HTML or the fetched
page into the `ArticleBlock` list (heading, paragraph, list, rows, table of plain cells, figure, note, plus the additive
`quote`), links are plain text for a child and ordinary links for teens and adults, figures come through the hub's image
route, and the kit `ArticleView` primitive draws it (section 16), for News and for the Reference page alike. The page is an
`answer_blocks` node bound to `POST /api/plugins/news/data/story`, which returns `{as_of, blocks: [article], degraded?}`
through `filterAnswerBlocks()`; the block's `speech[]` is the body split into chunks of at most 4,000 characters at paragraph
boundaries, photo credits and timestamps left out (ported from `useArticleNarration.ts`). Reading time is `reading_s`.
"Open on site" opens `open_url` with `rel="noopener noreferrer"` and `referrerpolicy="no-referrer"` (the citation chip's
rule), teens and adults only; a child's block has no `open_url`.

## 11. Key points and Listen

**Key points** are the story's `summary`: written by the local chat model (the `chat` role, no tools, thinking off, the person's
band's content ceiling in the instructions) from the feed's own text or the fetched blocks, never from a snippet, in the voice
of the band it is written for, three to five short lines. Written on the idle queue for the top 24 stories per band per tick
(`news.shelf.summaries`, one request in flight at a time, skipped while a chat turn is in flight, as the poller already does)
and on demand when a person taps Key points on a story that has none (one request, the person's band). Stored once per story
and band on `NewsStory.summary` with `summary_band`; a child's or teen's summary passes the output floor before it is stored
and again at release, and a story whose summary fails the child floor shows no Key points to a child. The line under it says
"Written on this hub from the article" so nobody mistakes it for the publisher's words. Rule 6: a failed summary shows nothing
and the story still opens.

**Listen** is `POST /api/tts` with each `speech[]` chunk in turn, played by the shell's player bar (the platform owns the
player, UI.md), with pause, resume, a "paragraph N of M" line and a resume point kept in the person's `NewsRead` row
(`read_s`), so it carries across devices and does not live in browser storage. Listen is the TV reader's default action.
For a child (if decision 1 opens the app), a headline, a source line, a kicker, a summary or an article sentence is spoken
or shown only after it passes the child output gate sentence by sentence, and that includes the kid-mode read-aloud on every
card ([kid-mode-app-voice-2026-10-17.md](kid-mode-app-voice-2026-10-17.md)): the speakable text of a card is built on the hub
from gated strings, never assembled by the client from ungated fields (the same `StreamGate` a spoken turn uses, rule 10), on top of the minor floor the
shelf already applied; the gate runs on the hub when the `story` route builds `blocks` and `speech[]` for a child, so no
client ever holds an ungated sentence (the kid-mode architect ruling of 2026-10-17). A sentence the gate drops is left out of
both the page and the audio; a story with nothing left shows the `Empty` line and no Listen.
