# Editing pictures in chat and in the editor (2026-10-17)

Owner request (Jesse, 2026-10-17): real picture editing, in chat and in the editor. "Remove person X from the picture", "add this person
to the picture", "make the sky not cloudy", and the like (another background, the colour of an object, add or remove an object, fix the
light). This note records what existed, what was measured, the method picked for each task and the safety rules. It builds on
[image-tools-2026-10-17.md](image-tools-2026-10-17.md), [image-engine-2026-10-17.md](image-engine-2026-10-17.md) and
[likeness-consent-2026-10-17.md](likeness-consent-2026-10-17.md); it changes none of [RULES.md](RULES.md).

## 1. What existed

| Piece | State |
|---|---|
| Remove background | Apple Vision foreground mask, IS-Net fallback (Stack, non-generative) |
| Erase | LaMa under a painted mask (Stack, non-generative) |
| Upscale, resize | Real-ESRGAN, Lanczos (Stack) |
| Editor **Comment** | A described change: Stack `edit` or `edit_masked` on FLUX.2 Klein 4B at bf16, one picture, optional pointed-at box, 4 steps, 5 to 8 s; the result is a preview the person applies as a new version (Original and Undo in the editor) |
| Chat | `generate_image` could only make new pictures; its description told the model to refuse edits and point at the Edit button |
| Safety | Age and surface gate, the two absolute lines, the likeness rules (a photo of a person is changed only for one consenting household adult), the finished-picture age check |

The gaps: no instruction edit from chat; no second picture (a person to add); no brush mask on Comment; no way to take out one chosen
person without touching the others.

## 2. Measured (Apple M5 Max, 128 GB, GPU lock held, one render at a time, synthetic pictures only)

Six cases on pictures generated by Klein itself (a street with two people, a red car under cloud, a portrait, an empty bench, a living room).
Outputs and contact sheets are in the author's scratchpad (`scratchpad/edit/out`, `sheet_klein.jpg`, `sheet_people*.jpg`); the scratchpad is
temporary, so the verdicts are recorded here.

| Case | Klein 4B instruction edit (bf16, 4 steps) | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| Remove the man (two people walking) | 5.8 s, 16 GB | Both people removed, scene kept. Not selective: the wrong result for "remove one person" |
| Make the sky clear | 5.4 s | Clean blue sky, houses and car kept; small drift in tree shapes |
| Change the car to yellow | 5.5 s | Excellent: only the car changed |
| Add the woman from a second picture to the bench | 10.1 s, 16.9 GB | She sits on the bench in the right scale, face, hair and sweater kept; the park is unchanged |
| Replace the wall with a beach | 8.0 s | Subject and hair kept, plausible beach |
| Fix an under-exposed photo | 5.6 s | Natural daylight; it also drew a few clouds |

| Case | Qwen-Image-Edit-2511, mflux q4, 20 steps | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| Remove the man | 199 s, 40.6 GB | Both people removed and the whole street redrawn (different composition) |
| Make the sky clear | 196 s | Clear sky, but the car and house were recomposed and enlarged |
| Change the car to yellow | 196 s | See the sheet: slower, no better |
| Add the woman | 347 s | Returned the portrait alone and ignored the scene |

Qwen (Apache-2.0, 29 GB of q4 weights) is 35 times slower, needs 40 GB, redraws the whole picture and failed the add-a-person case. Two of the six
cases (swap background, fix light) were not run on Qwen once the other four were decisive. **Pick: Klein 4B for every generative edit.** No other local
edit model with a proven Apple Silicon runtime was added: mflux also lists FLUX.1 Kontext (non-commercial licence) and others that fail the licence bar.

Removing one chosen person, measured on the street picture:

| Method | Time | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| Apple Vision person instance mask, one person, LaMa fill | find 0.5 s, erase 1.6 to 5.7 s (CPU) | Selective and exact: only the chosen person goes; chairs and the other person stay. The fill shows a faint smear on the cobbles (LaMa works at 512 px) |
| Same mask, Klein masked fill (the changed area only, composited back) | 2.6 s warm | Best: clean cobbles, the far-off figures back, only the chosen person gone. Used for pictures made here; an uploaded photo uses LaMa (draws nothing, no consent question) |

Vision's raw instance masks carry stray bits (a chair, a far-off figure), so the Stack keeps each person's main blob and what touches it and drops the rest.

## 3. Method per task

| Task | Method | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Remove a person or people | `kind: remove_people`: Vision finds and numbers the people, the household's picture-reading model picks the number(s) from a boxed copy of the picture (numbers only, never a name; "everyone" needs no reader); on a picture made here the picture model fills the outline (masked edit), on an uploaded photo (or if that fails) LaMa fills | Selective (Klein removes everyone); draws nothing, so it needs no consent question and works on any picture the person may open in the editor |
| Remove an object | Klein edit (words), or the editor's Erase brush for exact control | Klein fills complex scenes well |
| Clear sky, recolour, swap background, fix light, add an object | Klein edit, whole picture (or `edit_masked` when a box or brush is given: only that area can differ) | Best quality and speed measured |
| Add a person from a picture | Klein edit with up to two reference pictures | The only method that placed the person correctly |
| Remove background, upscale | The Stack's non-generative tools | Unchanged |

One chat tool, `generate_image`: `change` set means "change a picture I have" (`attached`, `attached_2`, `latest`); `kind` says remove_people,
remove_background, upscale or change; `who` names the people to remove by position or clothes; `reference` is the second picture;
`photo_subject` is the household adult a photo shows. The model decides that an edit is wanted and fills these; Home never reads the words to
decide (rule 1). In the editor, **Comment** now also takes a painted `mask` and a `reference_id`.

## 4. Safety (SAFETY.md, likeness-consent note; nothing weakened)

- The age and surface gate and the two absolute lines run first, on the instruction, exactly as for a new picture.
- A picture this home made is its own. A photo someone brought is changed in words only if it shows no one (checked by the household's own model) or exactly one household adult
  who agreed, named by the person, with a signed proof (`editSources.ts`, shared with Comment). A photo of a child, of anyone else, or of a person the model cannot count is refused.
- A reference photo of a person is a photo of a consenting household adult with its own proof bound to that photo, `audience: adult`, one person at a time. The Stack checks each proof
  again when the job starts, and a proof is used once.
- A photo of a real person is never changed into something sexual (`asksToUndress`, the existing rule).
- The finished picture goes through the finished-picture age check before it is stored or shown.
- Removing people (Vision plus LaMa) generates no person, so it is allowed on a photo that shows several people (the editor's Erase is already allowed on any picture).
- **Freezing the people (IMGEDIT-05, owner's ruling 2026-10-17):** a change that is not about a person (sky, colour, background, an object, the light) is allowed on a photo that shows people.
  The Stack's `people_mask` outlines every person; the picture model edits the whole picture; every pixel inside every outline is then put back from the original (a soft edge only outside),
  and the result is proved identical to the original inside the outlines (`freezePeople`: a pixel that differs is an error, never a picture). The model states in `affects_people` whether the
  change is about a person; an edit that is (their look, clothes or face, adding a person) goes through the likeness rules above and the finished-picture age check, as before. If the person finder
  finds no one although the household's model counted people, nothing is changed. Measured on the two-person street picture ("make the sky clear", Klein 4 steps, real Vision outlines):
  115,283 pixels inside the outlines, 115,277 of them changed by the raw edit, 0 differ after the freeze. Limit: the model redraws the background (it moved buildings and trees), so a seam can show where the original people meet the new background.
- The original is never touched. The result is the next version of the picture (image-edit provenance), so **Edit** shows Original and Undo. Temporary and Incognito chats keep versions in memory.
- No picture, mask or instruction is logged.

## 5. Status and progress

Edits are picture jobs: the same card, the kit `ImageGeneration` progress, Stop, and the Stack's per-step progress (rule S6). Home-run steps report their own phase ("Finding the people in the picture", "Removing them and filling in the background").
The result renders in the kit gallery with Edit, as shipped (rule 9).

## 6. Not built, and limits

- No brush or reference control in the kit editor: the API takes `mask` and `reference_id`; the control is a kit change in `commons` (row IMGEDIT-04).
- Qwen-Image-Edit-2511 is recorded as measured and not shipped.
- A model the Stack cannot take a second picture on (an older Stack) is refused with a plain line (`edit.references` is 0 or absent).
- The Vision person finder needs macOS 14 and the Swift tools; elsewhere the answer is "paint over the person with Erase".
- Klein can redraw small unrelated parts of a picture on a whole-picture edit (tree shapes after "clear the sky"); a box or brush limits it to that area bit for bit.
