# The Home page: what it shows, how packages reach it, who can change it (2026-10-17)

Jesse, live first run on the Mac Studio, 2026-10-17: "we need to revamp our home page, it's terrible." This note is the
research and design pass he asked for. It covers the page a signed-in person lands on at `/` (not the setup wizard): what it
shows, how a package puts content on it, whether people can customize it, and what other products do. Design note only; no
code. The architect gate applies (verdict at the end).

It extends [HOME-ALIVE-01](../plans/home-alive-01-2026-10-02.md) (accepted 2026-10-02, decisions in its section 9). That note
decided the blocks and the copy. It did not decide the registration contract beyond "one card per widget", said nothing about
customization, breakpoints, the TV, loading or the "models still downloading" state, and predates two owner rulings that change
it (NO-CROSS-USER-VISIBILITY-01 of 2026-10-07 and the `app` kind of 2026-10-16). Fifteen days on, only HOME-ALIVE-01a is built.

## 1. What exists today (from code, 2026-10-17)

The capture `docs/assets/screens/home-desktop-light.png` (generated 2026-10-16 against the seeded household) shows it at 1440:

| Block | Code | What the person sees | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| Setup count | `DashboardPage.tsx:50`, `useSetupChecklist` | "9 things left to finish setting up", one link (owner and admin) | Right data, wrong shape: a sentence with no steps |

Why it reads as terrible: nothing on it invites the person to do anything (no way to ask, no chats to pick up, no apps); the
three numbers are operator metrics; the one list is a log; three quarters of a wide screen is empty, which `UI.md` names as a
failure outright ("a wide screen left empty by a page designed phone-first"). The data path underneath is sound and stays.

Facts in our words; one source per product. Full notes were gathered by two research passes the same day.

| Linear ([default home](https://linear.app/docs/default-home-view)) | Active issues; Inbox as the feed | Issues and notifications flow in | Per user picks the home view from a fixed list | Inbox noise |

| Hearth ([review](https://wetried.it/compare/hearth-display-review-digital-calendar-organizer-vs-pura/)) | Shared calendar, per-person profiles and routines with kid icons | Parents build routines in the app | Parents only | Membership required, lag, glitches |
| DAKboard ([layouts](https://blog.dakboard.com/announcing-dakboard-custom-layouts/)) | Photos, calendar, weather, news, clock | Blocks from feeds and accounts | Admin, web editor: drag, resize, layer; custom screens need Premium | Oversized widget boxes, subscription pressure |

Five findings that matter for us:

1. **A sane default plus hide and reorder is the ask.** Freeform editors (YAML, Take control, Gridstack) are used by a few and
   resented by the rest. Notion's per-section "count, reorder, hide" and Linear's "pick your home view from a list" are the shapes
   people accept.
2. **Pushed content is the loudest complaint everywhere** (Echo Show ads, Fire TV banners, iOS Widget Suggestions, SmartThings
   suggestion cards). People accept what they chose or what their own household installed; never a vendor's card. MaiPai has no
   vendor feed, which is a promise worth keeping visible: nothing appears on Home that the household did not install.
3. **Per-person views are rare and wanted.** Apple Home and Echo Show share one screen for everyone; Home Assistant stores the
   default per device. "A different view for my kid and me" recurs. We already have identity per person and role, so per-person
   is the default here, not a feature.
4. **Every AI chat app opens on a greeting and the composer, never a dashboard**, with recents one tap away; hiding recents causes
   backlash. The composer first is right for a chat product. A family hub is not only chat, so the question is how much glance
   content sits beside it (section 5).
5. **Family hubs win on per-person identity** (a color per member, chores a child can tap) and lose on manual setup and
   subscriptions. The useful glance content is the day: date, weather, what is on, what is left to do.

## 3. Rulings since HOME-ALIVE-01 that change it

- **NO-CROSS-USER-VISIBILITY-01 (owner, 2026-10-07)**: "no user can see what another user does, period." HOME-ALIVE-01's admin
  child line ("chatted 2 hours ago") and the dashboard's `recent_activity` of children for an admin break it. Both are dropped
  here; `visibleTargetIds` in `dashboard.ts` returns only the viewer. The 2026-10-02 decision 2 (headlines off a child's Home)
  stands.
- **The `app` kind (2026-10-16)**: a package's rich content is answer blocks drawn by kit Elements, and Weather's hero is the
  `weather_card` Element. A Home card should draw the same blocks through the same route, not a second text-only shape.
- **Rule 6a (RULES.md)**: no page carries a person's id or text next to an activity. Home shows only the viewer's own chats and
  files.

## 4. Purpose and audience

Home is the household's front door for one person: "what can I do, and what is going on today," then out of the way. It is
not a status page (the dot and `/status` own health), not chat (Chat owns the conversation), not the library. Three jobs, in
this order: let the person ask; let them pick up what they were doing; show the day as the household's installed packages
see it. Everything a person sees is theirs or the household's, never another person's.

| Viewer | Sees | Does not see |
|---|---|---|
| Adult member | Greeting, hero, Ask, Pick up where you left off, Today, Your family | Admin strip, Getting started (admin items) |
| Owner or admin | Everything a member sees, plus the one-line admin strip and Getting started while items remain | Any other person's activity |
| Teen | As an adult member; Today filtered by `min_role` and band; starters from the teen's package set | Admin items; model or mode controls (the chat already has none) |
| Child | Greeting, hero (child-safe blocks only), Ask with the child's starters, Pick up (own chats), Today filtered to `min_role: child` | Your family's management links, Recently made unless a Library item is theirs, anything role-gated |
| Guest | Greeting, Ask, Today widgets with `min_role: guest` only | Pick up, family, library, settings |

Rule 0 holds: nothing here loosens a gate. Every Today card passes the package's `min_role`, the widget's own `min_role`
(HOME-ALIVE-01e0), the block's `min_band` and the output floor, in that order, server side (`listWidgets`, `filterAnswerBlocks`).
Home adds no gate of its own and removes none.

## 5. The chat-first question

Three options were weighed.

| Option | What `/` is | For | Against |
|---|---|---|---|
| A. Home is Chat | `/` redirects to `/chat`; recents in the thread sidebar; no Home app | What every AI product does; zero clutter; one screen to maintain | Loses the household glance (weather, the day, kids' Today); S1 names Home as an app and the brand mark's destination, so this is a rule change; a child's first screen becomes a composer with no picture of the day |
| B. Home is a dashboard with an ask box | HOME-ALIVE-01 as written: seven blocks, Today first, ask below | Already decided and filed; glance first | Seven blocks is the clutter Jesse objects to ("too much"); nothing bounds how many cards a household's packages add |
| C. Ask first, a bounded glance beside it (recommended) | Greeting and Ask at the top, exactly as Chat's empty state; one hero; two short shelves; a block budget | Matches finding 4 and finding 1; keeps the family glance; a hard count keeps it calm as packages grow; a child's screen is the same shape with their content | Two places to type (Home and Chat); answered by making Home's box the same kit Element that hands off to Chat with the text unsent |

**Recommendation: C.** Option B's blocks survive, re-ordered and budgeted. Ask moves above Today (this reverses Jesse's
2026-10-02 decision 3, "Today first", on the evidence in finding 4 and his 2026-10-17 words; see Decisions). Option A is
measured, not dropped: it is the fallback if C still feels like too much after the first capture, and it needs S1 amended.

## 6. The page, block by block (option C)

Default order for an adult member. A block with nothing to show is left out. The whole page never shows more than six
blocks and never more than four Today cards without the person asking for more.

1. **Greeting** (kept). "Good morning, Juniper." and "Here is your household today."
2. **Admin strip** (owner and admin; HOME-ALIVE-01b, one line): "Looking after your hub" with Repairs and Updates as links
   (System status stays in the rail's profile menu, rule S2; the strip never draws a second copy). Shown always for admins, per
   decision 1 of 2026-10-02. Members never see it.
3. **Ask** (HOME-ALIVE-01h). The box is the kit `InputGroup` with a `Button` (HOME-ALIVE-01 section 6, unchanged); the three
   starters are kit `Button`s under it. Home is a non-chat surface, so it uses the kit's primitive set (rule 9b), not Chat's
   Elements. A starter or a typed line opens
   `/chat?ask=<text>` with the text in the composer, unsent. Starters come from the person's package set (`routing.examples[0]`,
   already filtered by `min_role`). While the engine is installing or no engine is set up, the box stays and its placeholder
   reads the install strip's own line ("Home can chat now. Better AI is still downloading"), with starters disabled exactly as
   Chat disables them (`useEngineDownReason`). No second wording.
4. **Hero** (one card, the first place a package's content appears). The highest-priority eligible widget that declares
   `hero: true` (section 7); Weather's `weather_card` when the Weather package is installed and the person has a place. With no
   hero widget installed, the hero is the day: date, and the household's events once a calendar package exists. The hero is the
   only card drawn large.
5. **Pick up where you left off** (HOME-ALIVE-01c). Your last three chats, title and relative time, "All chats". Own chats only.
6. **Today** (HOME-ALIVE-01e). Up to four cards from `GET /api/widgets` in priority order, the hero excluded; a fifth and later
   sit behind "More" (a disclosure, the kit's `Collapsible`). Each card is a kit `Card` whose body is the widget's blocks drawn
   by `answer_blocks`, or its `items` as kit `Item` rows when the widget has no `data`. A card whose data route fails or returns
   `degraded` with no cached data drops out quietly (rule 6: a failed tool never fails the page); with cached data it shows the
   data and "as of 7:10".
7. **Your family** (HOME-ALIVE-01d). Avatars and first names, "4 people", linking to each profile. No activity line for anyone
   (section 3). "Just you so far." with "Add someone" for admins.
8. **Getting started** (HOME-ALIVE-01g; owner and admin while items remain). One card from the existing setup checklist with
   its eleven items, a tick per done item, mark done and skip, replacing the "9 things left" sentence. Gone when empty.

Recently made (HOME-ALIVE-01f) leaves the default page: it is one tap away in Library and its row counts against the
budget. It stays filed as a block a person may turn on (section 9).

Empty and loading:

- **First paint**: skeletons hold the layout (kit `Skeleton` inside `AsyncState`); no spinner over the page.
- **Day one** (no chats, no files, one person): greeting, Ask, hero (the day), Getting started for the owner. No zero anywhere.
- **Models still downloading**: Ask shows the strip's line as its placeholder; Today cards still load (package data needs no
  model); the hero loads. **No engine at all** (`engine_failed` or none set up): the same, plus the install strip's own Try again
  stays where the shell already mounts it. Home adds no second status line.
- **Offline**: Today cards serve from the package cache within `stale_ok_s`; past that they drop out; the page says nothing
  about the network (the dot and `/status` own it).
- **Incognito on**: Pick up and Your family hide; Ask stays and opens a temporary chat; Today shows only widgets whose package
  is not `incognito: "blocked"`.

## 7. The registration contract (spec first, additive)

One definition: a package puts content on Home by declaring a widget. `contributes.widgets[]` gains five optional fields in
`commons/spec/schemas/manifest.schema.json`; existing widgets keep working unchanged.

| Field | Type | Default | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| `min_role` | role enum | the package's `min_role` | Lowest role that may see this card (HOME-ALIVE-01e0, unchanged) |
| `priority` | integer 0 to 100 | 50 | Higher sorts earlier within Today; ties by install time, then `id` |
| `hero` | boolean | false | May fill the hero slot. The eligible hero with the highest `priority` wins; the rest are ordinary Today cards |
| `data` | string | none | The id of a `contributes.data[]` entry (app-kind note). When set, the card draws that route's answer blocks through `answer_blocks`; when absent, the existing `{items}` shape |
| `surfaces` | list of `home`, `tv`, `robot` | `["home"]` | Where the card may appear. `tv` and `robot` are shared screens: a card there must carry only household data (it is filtered to widgets whose `data` or `inputs` name no person) |

What does not change: `id`, `title`, `size`, `refresh_s`, `inputs`; `GET /api/widgets` (it returns the new fields and, when
`data` is set, the data route path); the data route and its cache; the output floor on every string. A widget is a pointer to
data plus placement; it never names a route, a layout, a color or a size in pixels. The kit draws it (rule 9).

Ordering, in one function `orderHomeCards(widgets, person, surface)` on the hub, tested per role and band: filter by role, band,
enabled status, `surfaces` and Incognito; pick the hero; sort the rest by `priority`, install time, `id`; apply the person's
hidden list (section 9); cut at four plus "More".

## 8. Breakpoints

One page, four surfaces, the kit's breakpoints and density budgets; Home writes grid placement on its own containers only.

- **Phone (under 640)**: the round menu button (S1); one column: greeting; Ask (box, then the three starters as pills that wrap);
  hero full width; Pick up as three rows; Today cards stacked, two visible and "More"; Your family as a horizontally scrolling
  avatar row. Nothing is fixed to the bottom.
- **Tablet (640 to 960)**: two columns. Left: greeting, Ask, Pick up. Right: hero, then Today cards two across. Your family spans
  both at the foot. Admin strip spans both under the greeting.
- **Desktop (960 and up)**: three columns on the kit's 12-column grid. Row one: greeting (span 8) and the admin strip (span 4,
  admins). Row two: Ask (span 8) and the hero (span 4). Row three: Today cards three across (span 4 each), the fourth wrapping, then
  "More". Row four: Pick up (span 6) and Your family (span 6). Getting started (admins) sits at the end of row two's left column
  while items remain. At 1440x900 the first three rows fill the viewport; no row is a lone card on a wide canvas.
- **TV (Go, input mode)**: one focused row, the kit's TV density. The hero large at the left, then the Today cards that declare
  `tv` in `surfaces`, in priority order. No Ask box (the TV asks by voice and the remote), no Pick up, no Your family: these are
  per-person, and the TV is a shared screen. The same `GET /api/widgets` with `surface=tv`.

## 9. Customization: the smallest useful version first

Finding 1 says hide and reorder are what people use. We ship hide first, per person, and nothing that needs an editor.

**Ships with the page (one setting):**

- Every Today card and the two shelves carry the kit `DropdownMenu` placed in the `Card`'s existing `CardAction` slot, with one
  entry, "Hide from Home". Hidden ids go in a new person-scope key `ui.home_hidden` (JSON list of `<package>/<widget id>` or a
  core block id: `pick_up`, `family`, `recently_made`), declared in `commons/spec/settings/keys.json` beside `ui.pinned_apps` and
  shaped like it: an `expert`-level `text` key the generic renderer already draws (rule S4), normally written by the menu, not by
  hand. A "Show again" row per hidden card waits for a `list` selector in the settings key schema and its kit renderer row;
  until then "Show again" is clearing the key. A hidden hero falls back to the next eligible hero.
- A child may hide a card too; hiding never shows more. An admin's override of what a child sees is the existing `min_role` and
  capability allocation, not a Home setting.

**Waits (filed, not built until Jesse asks):**

- Reorder (drag on desktop, up and down on a phone) writing `ui.home_order`.
- Household defaults set by an admin (`home.default_hidden`, household scope), applied under a person's own list.
- Choosing the hero when two packages offer one.
- Turning Recently made on.

No YAML, no edit mode, no layout editor. A setting that resets or vanishes is the trust-breaker finding 5 names, so both keys
are ordinary person settings in the one store, carried by backups.

## 10. Rule check

- **Rule 0** (age and surface gates outrank everything): every card is filtered server side by package `min_role`, widget
  `min_role`, block `min_band` and the output floor; the TV surface shows household data only. No conflict.
- **Rule 5** (glance surfaces keep their own limits): a Today card shows the widget's own summary, already bounded by the
  package and the output floor. No conflict.
- **Rule 6** (a failed tool never fails the answer): a failed card drops out or shows cached data with "as of". No conflict.
- **Rule 6a** (no person's activity on an admin page): the child activity line and children's `recent_activity` are removed.
  Measured conflict in the accepted HOME-ALIVE-01 note; this note supersedes those two lines.
- **Rule 9 and 9b** (kit parts as they ship, no overrides, no wrappers; non-chat surfaces use the kit's primitive set): every
  block is a kit part (`InputGroup`, `Button`, `Card` with its `CardAction` slot, `DropdownMenu`, `Item`, `Avatar`, `Collapsible`,
  `Skeleton`, the `weather_card` Element and the other answer blocks through `answer-block.tsx`). Grid placement classes sit on
  Home's own container `div`s, not on a kit part. The card menu fills the slot `Card` already exposes; no new part. No conflict.
- **S1 and S2**: Home stays an app on the rail and the brand mark's destination; the phone keeps the drawer and the menu button.
  The admin strip links Repairs and Updates only; System status stays in the profile menu. Option A would need S1 amended and is
  not recommended. No conflict in C.
- **S4 and S5**: `ui.home_hidden` is a registry `text` key drawn by the generic renderer with spec copy, like `ui.pinned_apps`;
  Home writes no settings layout. No conflict.
- **Platform principle 4** (one definition): the widget stays the one way onto Home; `data` points at the app-kind's
  `contributes.data[]` rather than a second data shape; the setup checklist is the one source of Getting started.

## 11. Reuse check

Looked at first: `InputGroup`, `Button`, `Card` (with `CardAction`), `DropdownMenu`, `Item`, `Avatar`, `Collapsible` and
`Skeleton` from the vendored shadcndashboard set; `answer-block.tsx` and the `weather_card` Element for package
content; the existing widget routes and package cache; `usePinnedApps` as the pattern for `ui.home_hidden`; the setup checklist
route for Getting started. Considered and rejected: a drag-and-drop board (Gridstack, as Homarr) because finding 1 shows hide
and a fixed order satisfy most households and a board is the editor they resent; a per-package React widget because a native
client cannot render it (the 2026-09-06 re-decision); the kit's pre-shell `primitives/WidgetCard.tsx` because it retires under the
2026-09-21 rule. Hand-built code is limited to `orderHomeCards()` and the page's grid containers, because no shipped part orders
cards by role and priority.
