# Videos from the person's pictures (REFVIDEO-01, REFVIDEO-FIRSTFRAME-01)

The `make_reference_video` tool (package `backend/packages/make_reference_video`) makes a short video from pictures a person attached in chat: people, objects and places. A film or commercial of 10 seconds or more stays `make_film`.

## Two routes, chosen in code before any work (REFVIDEO-FIRSTFRAME-01)

Measured 2026-11-12 (8 clips of two real adults, SFace cosine to the photo 0.02 to 0.22 against about 0.36 for the same person): the LTX 2.5 Ingredients adapter keeps hair, glasses and clothes from a reference sheet but not a real person's face. It works for objects, places and invented or cartoon characters. A face in the clip's FIRST FRAME does stay (image-to-video conditions frame 0 at full strength). So `videos/referenceFirstFrame.ts` `decideReferenceRoute` runs at the tool call, before the job starts:

- The face finder finds a face in a picture the vision model reads as a photographed person (`role actor`, `medium photograph`): **first-picture route**. Pictures with no face anywhere skip the vision read and take the **sheet route** (everything below this section). A face in a cartoon or an object picture is the sheet route too.
- People in the pictures and the face models not installed: a refusal sentence (`face_models_missing`, `FACE_MODELS_LINE`: an admin installs "Telling people apart" in Settings, AI and models). Never a silent downgrade to the sheet route.
- A picture that already is a reference sheet and also holds a real face is not handled specially; it is read like any other picture.

### First-picture route

The card's steps are "Creating the first picture" and "Creating the video" (`FIRST_FRAME_STEP_NAMES`, chosen by `ReferenceProgress.route`); the job phase is `making from the first picture`. Same job machinery as the sheet route: one `edit` job, one at a time, Stop in both steps, the "waiting for free room" line, restart ends the job with the first picture kept, the recipe on `VideoRequest.reference` (`route: "first_frame"`, `sheetFileIds[0]` is the first picture, `prompt` is the paragraph alone, `firstPicture`, `faceMatch`).

1. **Step 1, `buildFirstPicture(pictures, { scene, actor, onStep, signal })`** returns `{ png, how, notes, people, description }`. One actor picture and nothing else: the photo itself fitted to 1024x576 on a soft copy of itself (`how: "photo"`, nothing redrawn). Otherwise each person is cut out alone with `films/sheetMaker.ts` `cutOutPeople` (the sheet's own face split and junk-only cleanup; no close-ups, no drawn views), at most two people. The place picture (set, building) is the plate the edit model works on; the people (their cut-outs on white) and the objects are its reference pictures (the edit takes two). With no place, the first person's own photo is the picture that is edited. The edit is `films/engines.ts` `submitComposition` (Stack `/v1/images/edits`), whose instruction says to keep references exactly as they look and never asks for a redrawn face.
2. **Face gate, per person:** the best SFace cosine between their own photo's face (from their cut-out) and any face in the composed still must be at least `FACE_GATE` 0.5. If the edit fails or any person fails the gate, the first picture is a plain Sharp paste of each person's own cut-out over the plate (`how: "pasted"`) and a note says so; objects are then left to the video's words. The scores and the people's face vectors are saved in `recipe.firstPicture`.
3. **Step 2, `runFirstFrameClip`:** an ordinary image-to-video submit (`submitVideo`, picture start, `image_source: "uploaded"`) from the saved first picture. `planFirstFrameClip` picks the level the same way a clip from one attached photo does: Max with sound when the person holds it and the Stack reports `ltx25max.image_to_video`, else the best allowed level. Length is 6 s by default (4 to 8 asked, the level's ceiling lowers it). The prompt is the person's request rewritten as one paragraph about what happens, ending with `FIRST_FRAME_SENTENCE` (photorealistic, one continuous scene) and the no-dialogue sentence. The job's request takes the real size, level and tier (`EditHooks.request`).
4. **After the clip,** `measureClipFaces` samples six frames (`extractKeyframes` with a count), takes each person's nearest face in each frame (SFace cosine to their photo) and stores `recipe.faceMatch`. A median under `CLIP_FACE_LOW` 0.35 adds `LOOK_ALIKE_LINE` to the card's notice (`clip.note`), with any step 1 notes. It never blocks the clip.
5. **Another take and new words:** `anotherReferenceTake` and `reuse_video` re-run step 2 only from the saved first picture (new seed, or new paragraph). No cut-outs, no edit, no gate. The route needs a ready video level, not Max or the adapter.

Face code, the sheet maker and the Stack picture edit are dynamic imports (VISION-02c: `tests/turnMachine/pictureParts.test.ts` fails if the chat picture path reaches them). `referenceFirstFrame.ts` therefore defines its own `SheetPicture` type instead of importing the sheet maker's.

**Real run:** `buildFirstPicture` is the entry for step 1 against the live Stack's image edit with a temporary data dir (`MAIPAI_DATA_DIR`; the face models must be in its `models/robot-assets`); the test seam `__setFirstFrameToolsForTests` replaces the face finder, cut-out tool and edit. Tests: `tests/referenceFirstFrame.test.ts` (fake faces by colour, fake edit and Stack).

## Flow

1. **Offer.** `referenceVideoAllowed` (toolGates.ts): the film floor (adult, own written chat screen, video on) and pictures attached, or an earlier reference video in the chat (for "same sheet, but ..."). The policy node refuses a forged call.
2. **Refusals before any work** (`videos/referenceVideo.ts`, one plain line each): no pictures, a child or teen, a spoken or temporary chat, Max not ready or needing an admin, the adapter reported missing, a video already being made, a refused prompt.
3. **Sheets.** Each picture (at most 6) is checked by the local vision model (`videos/referenceSheetCheck.ts`). A picture is a ready sheet only when the model answers `is_sheet` true and names at least two panels. A ready sheet is fitted to 768x448 (scaled to fit, padded with its own corner colour, nothing cropped) and its panels become the "Reference sheet: ..." text in the sheet maker's format. Up to two sheets go to the Stack; pictures that are not sheets are read (`pictures/roles.ts`) and built into one more sheet by `films/sheetMaker.ts` (`drawViews: true`). A failed check counts as "not a sheet"; a failed sheet step is a plain refusal.
4. **Prompt.** The sheet text, a blank line, then "Generated video: " and the person's request rewritten as one paragraph around the sheet panels (`completeBackground`, with the person's own words as the fallback). It always ends with the full-frame sentence and stays within 2000 characters.
5. **Job.** Sheets are stored as kept files of the person. One `edit` job (`edit.change = "ingredients"`, phase "making from the sheet") posts multipart `POST /v1/videos/ingredients` (`sheet` x1-2, `prompt`, `seconds`, `seed`, `audience`, `timeout_ms`), polls `getVideoJob` and reads `fetchClip`, like a restyle. It is stoppable, age-checked when finished, and shown on the turn's video card. While it renders the card shows the first sheet as the preview picture (`item.sheets`).
6. **Errors.** `ic_lora_missing` (409) and the other codes map to plain sentences (`INGREDIENTS_FAILURE_COPY`); a plain-sentence 400 from the Stack is shown as it is.

## The two parts it needs are listed (REFVIDEO-PARTS-01)

Settings, AI and models and the setup wizard list both parts like every other model (`shared/aiChoices.ts` jobs `faces` and `ingredients`; the words are in `lib/aiCopy.ts`, the pins and sizes beside their records). **Face finder and matcher** (`lib/visionAssets.ts`: the YuNet detector and the SFace embedder, 229,738 + 38,696,353 bytes, MIT and Apache-2.0). There is one store and one definition: the robot asset store (`lib/robotAssets.ts`, manifest ids `yunet-2026may` and `sface-2021dec` in `spec/assets/robot-assets.json`, folder `models/robot-assets`). The robot's asset route, the browser route `/api/vision/model/:file`, `likeness/faces.ts` and the sheet maker all read those same two files; names, URLs, sizes, checksums and licences come from the manifest only. Readiness is both files present (`faceAssetPath`: the robot store, else a copy an older Home left in `vision-models/`, read only as a fallback and never deleted by an upgrade); the switch runs `ensureVisionAssets` (which calls `ensureRobotAssetIds`: the robot download path, one job row per file, sha256 and size checked, nothing fetched if a copy exists) or `removeVisionAssets` (removes the robot-store copies and any old vision-folder copies; a robot that asks later makes the hub fetch them again) through `POST /api/ai/choices/faces`; the wizard's Install starts it when the row is ticked (`beginInstall`). The sheet maker never downloads it on its own. **Video-from-pictures adapter** (`lib/videos/ingredientsAdapter.ts`: `ltx-2.5-22b-ic-lora-ingredients-0.9`, 1,308,787,472 bytes, LTX-2.x Community License, gated on Hugging Face): readiness is the Stack's `GET /stack/v1/video/loras` listing by that exact name; install is one `POST /stack/v1/video/loras/install` (Hugging Face file link, the person's token, all-ages, base `ltx-2.5`, licence acknowledged) after the household licence acknowledgement covers the LTX licence; progress is the Stack job's percent. It is an engine part, so `loras/sync.ts` never turns it into a Styles record. The wizard lists it blocked ("add it after setup") because a fresh install has no token yet. `ic_lora_missing` points at "Settings, AI and models". Tests: `aiChoicesRoutes.test.ts`, `shared/aiChoices.test.ts`, `planChoices.test.ts`, `loras03.test.ts`.

## Keeping the sheet with the video

`VideoRequest.reference` (a `ReferenceRecipe`) holds the sheet file ids, the full prompt, the "Generated video" paragraph, the seed, the panel list (name, view, slot, sheet, source picture ids) and which sheets were provided as they were. `getReferenceVideoRecipe(actor, jobId)` returns a copy.

- **Another take** (`POST /api/videos/jobs/{id}/another`, and `anotherReferenceTake`) re-renders from the saved sheets and prompt with a new seed. No cut-outs, no drawn views.
- **New words, same sheet.** Tool arguments `reuse_video` (a video id, or `latest`) with `request`.
- **Cleanup.** A sheet is a companion of its video's clip (`libraryTrash.companionsOf`): Trash and purge take it along only when no other video that is still being made or still has its clip uses it (`videos/referenceSheets.ts`). A kept sheet never protects its clip from the storage rules. A sheet of a video that failed or was stopped stays in the Library until the person deletes it.

## One look sheet per cast member (CREATOR-06)

`lib/castLook.ts` `captureLook(actor, ref)` builds a cast member's reference sheet once, through `films/sheetMaker.ts` (the one builder), from the member's pictures: a synth's rest picture and approved sheet views, a saved subject's reference pictures, or photos the person supplies for a household member (`POST /api/cast/{id}/look` with `photo_file_ids`). The sheet is stored as the subject's sheet reference; its file provenance carries a source key (`lib/castLookKey.ts`), so the same pictures reuse the stored sheet and changed pictures rebuild it (`look.sheet_stale` on the cast list shows it). A made-up character with no picture gets one portrait from the picture engine first; a real person never does. A household member who is under 18 is never captured. Features read the sheet through `resolveCast` (`look.sheet_file_id`); `POST /api/media/subjects/{id}/sheet` calls the same function.

## Whole-clip quality check (CREATOR-10)

The local Stack's `media.check` decodes the clip once and returns frame luma, difference, sharpness and optional camera flow. Home applies the sole threshold table, `CLIP_RULES` in `backend/src/lib/videos/frameCheck.ts`: black luma under 12 for 0.3 s; frozen difference under 0.35 for 1.0 s; a jump over max(28, 8 times median difference); soft sharpness under 0.35 of median for 0.5 s (serious at 1.5 s, with median above 20); camera motion below 8 motion units per second when a move was requested; and loop seam SSIM under 0.9 when supplied. Checks are advisory and never say a clip is fine.
