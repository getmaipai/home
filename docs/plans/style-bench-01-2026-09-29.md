# STYLE-BENCH-01: the fidelity bench, the gate before any adapter reaches a turn

## Why

`STYLE-TRAIN-01` (landed `1e3ff6dd`) produced four real trained
companion voice adapters (pal, tutor, buddy, default), converted to
GGUF and verified loadable. This item is the gate that decides whether
any of them is good enough to actually ship: prove each adapter carries
its companion's voice without moving the substance, the cost, or tool
calling. Read `docs/BACKLOG.md`'s `STYLE-BENCH-01` row in full (search
for it) - it is fully specified: every arm, every recorded metric, every
pass-bar number, in exact words. This work order summarizes it; the
BACKLOG row is the actual spec, read it directly rather than relying on
this summary for anything precise.

Also read, in `docs/dev.md`: `VOICE-CLASS-01`'s design pass (why a
document-shaped typed reply's body trains at near-zero loss and the
signal lives in the frame), and the `STYLE-CORPUS-01b`/`02`/
`STYLE-TRAIN-01` closed sections (the real numbers this bench will be
measuring against, including the per-companion drop rates that never
cleared 20% - that finding stays STYLE-CORPUS-02's/TRAIN-01's own, not
something this item re-litigates; this item measures the trained
adapters as they are).

## Steps

Follow the BACKLOG row's spec exactly. In short:

1. Build `style-adapter.ts` on the spike's runner, live on tier 1
   against a hand-spawned engine on a spare port (never the shared 8787
   hub or 8788 engine - the spike's own operator pattern).
2. Five arms, three seeded runs each: A floor (identity only, no
   adapter), B shipped (real `contextToMessages()`, no adapter), C
   adapter (B's prompt byte-identical, `lora: [{id, scale: 1.0}]`), D
   vector (informational: `llama-cvector-generator` on the companion's
   own `examples`), E strength (informational: scale 0.5 and 1.5).
3. Record per row/arm exactly the metric list the row names: predicted
   tokens, headings/lists, lowercase, self-description check,
   contraction count, marker count (tokens in `examples` absent from
   `default`'s), the five written fact answers, decode tokens/sec,
   `cached_tokens`, the persona judge's relative score (trend only).
4. Run `scripts/bench/tool-calling.ts` at 10 repeats with the adapter
   on.
5. Apply the shape-aware reading from `VOICE-CLASS-01` (2026-09-29,
   already in `dev.md`): classify every written row by arm B's own
   reply using the corpus builder's `replyShape` (document-shaped:
   heading, list marker, or more than two paragraphs); the substance
   checks read the whole reply on every row; the voice checks (marker
   count, contractions) read the frame (`splitFrame`'s opener/closer)
   on a document-shaped row and the whole reply on a conversational
   one. Do not move the bar's numbers for this - the row is explicit
   that a companion is not failed for a contraction the bare model
   wrote inside a body the adapter was trained to copy, and gets no
   voice credit inside that body either.
6. Apply the pass bar exactly as the row states it, per companion, all
   on arm C: tokens within 0.85x-1.15x of B on at least 18 of 22
   written rows with B's structure kept; 0 lowercase; 0
   self-description; the five facts right; formal companion (the
   Tutor) zero contractions on all 52 rows; casual companions a
   contraction on at least 24 of 30 spoken turns; a companion with
   `filler_density: frequent` a marker on at least 18 of 30 spoken and
   11 of 22 written; no companion shows another companion's marker;
   every companion's written marker count above B's; turns 21-30 hold
   the voice checks within two turns of turns 1-10; the tool bench
   holds every positive and negative of the no-adapter run; decode at
   or above 0.9x of B; the one-slot companion-switch prefix
   re-evaluation recorded as a number.
