# HOME-ALIVE-01: a Home page for a family (design note, 2026-10-02)

Docs only. Nothing here is built. Jesse reads it before any item below is cut into `docs/BACKLOG.md`.
Inputs: `docs/design/home-pages-2026-09-20.md` ("The dashboard"), `docs/dev/reference-screens-review-2026-10-01.md`
(Screen D), `docs/plans/status-page-2026-09-30.md`, and Jesse's ruling of 2026-10-01: the first screen should feel
alive for a family, not like an operator's panel; the status dot and `/status` already carry health; Family is
already a first-class area. This note does not add a second health surface.

## 1. What Home shows today

Route `/` renders `DashboardPage.tsx` from one read, `GET /api/dashboard` (`backend/src/lib/dashboard.ts`).

| Block | Code | What a parent sees | What is wrong for a family |
|---|---|---|---|
| Greeting | `Greeting.tsx:32-34` | "Good morning, Juniper" and "Stay informed with today's activity" | The second line is the template's own, and it says nothing |
| People | `PeopleCountCard.tsx:9` | "People 3" | A bare count; no faces, no names, no way to the Family page |
| Updates | `UpdatesCard.tsx:10` | "Updates: Up to date", shown to every person | A member cannot act on it, and the bell already tells admins |
| Repairs | `RepairsCard.tsx:12` (admins) | "Repairs 1" | Fine for an admin, but it is a full card on a family's first screen |
| Engines | `EnginesCard.tsx:24` (admins) | "Engines: No Stack" | Two of our own words; "No Stack" reads as a fault on a working hub |
| Chart | `TurnsPerDayChart.tsx:34` | "Turns per day", a flat line at zero for 30 days | "Turns" is our word; a new household sees a month of zeros |
| Table | `RecentActivityTable.tsx:32-51` | Person, Where ("chat", "voice"), When; or "Nothing yet" | A log, not something to do; a raw date format; "Where" is our word |

Three words a parent would not understand: turns, engines, Stack. Nothing on the page invites a person to do
anything, and on day one it shows "3", "0" and a flat line. The data underneath is sound: the backend already
scopes activity to the viewer plus their children, never a teen or another adult (`dashboard.ts:48`).

## 2. The member view (an adult who is not an admin)

Top to bottom. Every word below is proposed copy. A block with nothing to show is left out, never shown empty.

1. **Greeting.** "Good morning, Juniper." with the sun or moon icon as today. Second line: "Here is your household
   today." (the line `home-pages-2026-09-20.md` already chose).
2. **Getting started.** Only while a step is left (section 5).
3. **Today.** One small card per home-screen widget the installed packages declare, in the package's own title:
   "Today" (the date and what is special about it), "Weather", "Shopping List". Each card shows the items its
   package returns; a card links where its item links.
4. **Ask.** One box, placeholder "Ask anything, or tell MaiPai what you need", button "Ask". It opens Chat with the
   words already typed in the box, ready to send. Under it, up to three starter buttons, the same real examples
   the empty chat uses (each installed package's first routing example), for example "What's the weather
   tomorrow?".
5. **Pick up where you left off.** Your last three chats: the chat's title and "2 hours ago". Link: "All chats".
6. **Recently made.** Your last four Library items: the name and "made yesterday". Link: "See Library".
7. **Your family.** Each person's avatar and first name, linking to their profile. Header: "Your family", "4 people".

No turns, engines, Stack, models or tokens. No counts that are zero on day one.

## 3. Children and teens

A child sees a restricted product by default, and Home follows that without a second rule of its own:

| Block | Child | Teen | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ask and starters | Shown; opens the child's own chat, which already has no mode or model control | As a member | Starters come from `GET /api/plugins`, already filtered by the package's `min_role` (`routes/plugins.ts:18`) |
| Getting started | Two steps only: "Ask your first question", "Make something" | As a member, minus "Add your family" | A child cannot add people; nothing teaches a child to share facts about themselves |
| Today | Only widgets the child's role may see | As a member | `lib/widgets.ts:68` already applies `min_role` |
| Chats, Recently made | Their own only | Their own only | The routes already scope to the signed-in person |
| Your family | Names and avatars only | Names and avatars only | Nobody but an admin sees another person's activity, and never a teen's |

Teens' activity never reaches an admin's Home: `visibleTargetIds` (`dashboard.ts:48-52`) returns the admin plus
children only. This note keeps that wall exactly as it is.

## 4. The admin view (owner or admin)

Everything a member sees, plus two small additions:

- **One strip** under the greeting, one line high: "Looking after your hub" then three links: "Nothing to fix" or
  "2 things to fix" (to `/repairs`), "Up to date" or "An update is ready" (to `/updates`), "How everything is
  running" (to `/status`). It does not repeat health: the dot and `/status` own that.
- **A line under each child in "Your family"**: "chatted 2 hours ago" or "no chats yet", from the activity rows the
  backend already scopes to the admin's children. Adults and teens get no line.

The strip appears exactly when `repairs_open` is present on the wire, the page's existing admin signal
(`DashboardPage.tsx:92`), so no client-side role check is added.

## 5. A brand-new household

On day one the page shows the greeting, the ask box, Getting started and Today (Today needs no history). Getting
started replaces every zero. Each step is ticked from data that already exists, so nothing new is stored:

| # | Step (title, then one line) | Links to | Done when | Who |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | "Ask your first question." Try the weather, a recipe, or help with homework. | `/chat` | You have one chat (`GET /api/conversations`) | Everyone |
| 2 | "Add your family." Each person gets their own space, with limits for children. | `/users` ("Add someone") | More than one person (`people_count`) | Owner, admin |
| 3 | "Tell MaiPai about yourself." Say something like "I'm allergic to peanuts" and it will remember. | `/chat` | You have one memory (`GET /api/memory?scope=self`) | Adults, teens |
| 4 | "Make something." Ask for a bedtime story or a short document. It is kept in your Library. | `/chat` | You have one Library item (`GET /api/files`) | Everyone |

Header: "Getting started", "1 of 4 done". A done step stays in the list with a tick; when every step is done the
block is gone for good, and the blocks it stood in for (chats, Library) have filled in. As the house gets used: Pick up where you left off
appears after the first chat, Recently made after the first file, the child lines after a child's first chat.

## 7. Cut list

## 9. Decisions, made by Jesse on 2026-10-02

Nothing is built. Items 01a to 01h and 01e0 are filed in docs/BACKLOG.md.

1. **Admin strip: always, or only when something needs you?**
   Jesse took the recommendation: always, one line, because it is the admin's way back to Updates and Repairs since those left the rail.
2. **News headlines on a child's Home.**
   Jesse took the recommendation: keep headlines off a child's Home through a per-widget `min_role` in the manifest spec (spec first, a small commons item before 01e), not through a Home-side rule. The news package is `min_role: child`, so its headlines widget would show to children (a child can already ask for news in chat).
3. **Order: Today above the ask box, or below it.**
   Jesse took the recommendation: keep Today first, as written; the starters still sit above the fold on a laptop. Section 2 follows what you said before (glanceable state first, then shortcuts and search). The reference review argued a person should be invited to do something first.
