---
title: If voice does not work
description: What to try when MaiPai cannot hear you or will not speak.
---

## 🎤 It cannot hear you

1. Tap the microphone button (**Voice input**).
2. When your browser asks to use the microphone, choose to allow it.
3. If you said no before, open your browser's site settings for MaiPai Home and allow the microphone there.
4. Say your question, then tap **Stop dictation**.

## 🔊 It will not speak

1. Look for **Read aloud** under a reply. If you do not see it, your house has no voice set up yet.
2. Turn up your device volume.
3. Tap **Read aloud** again.

## 👂 The wake word

Wake word listening is an early feature. It may be off, and it needs the microphone allowed.
