---
title: If voice does not work
description: What to try when MaiPai cannot hear you or will not speak.
---

## 🎤 It cannot hear you

1. Tap the microphone button (**Voice input**).
2. When your browser asks to use the microphone, choose to allow it.
3. If you said no before, Home shows a help box when you start a voice conversation. It names your browser and the exact switch to change, and also covers the Mac's own microphone switch. Change it, then tap **Try again**. You can also open your browser's site settings for MaiPai Home and allow the microphone there.
4. Browsers only allow the microphone on `localhost` or an `https` address. If the help box says the address is not secure, open Home from one of those.
5. Say your question, then tap **Stop dictation**.

## 🔊 It will not speak

1. Look for **Read aloud** under a reply. If you do not see it, your house has no voice set up yet.
2. Turn up your device volume.
3. Tap **Read aloud** again.

## 👂 The wake word

1. Wake word listening is for owners, admins and adults only, and it is off until someone turns it on.
2. Look for the microphone button that says **Turn on wake word listening** in the message box. If it is missing, wake word is not set up on this house's Home, and no screen in Home sets it up. Ask the person who runs the house.
3. The only wake word is "Hey Jarvis". Allow the microphone in your browser, and keep the MaiPai page open.
4. A red bar that says "Listening for Hey Jarvis" shows while it is on. Tap **Turn off wake word listening** to stop it.
