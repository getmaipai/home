---
title: If chat does not answer
description: What to try when you send a message and nothing comes back.
---

## ✍️ Steps

1. Wait a minute. The first answer after a restart can be slow, and you may see **Starting your AI…**.
2. If an error shows, tap **Retry**.
3. Reload the page and send the message again.
4. Open **Home** and look at the **Repairs** card. A number means something needs fixing.
5. Open **Repairs**. Each row says what is wrong and how to fix it.

<Shot id="repairs" />

6. If a repair needs an admin, tell the person who runs the house.

Admins also see **Error details** on a failed answer. Send those to whoever helps with the house.

## Chat was paused by an owner or admin

While chat is paused, you cannot send a message. Adults and teens see the pause notice and any note the owner or admin wrote. Children see "Chat is resting. Ask a grown-up."

An owner or admin can pause chat for the whole family:

1. Open **Settings**, then **Home settings**.
2. Open **Engines and AI**.
3. Fill in **Message shown while chat is paused** if you want to add a short note.
4. Turn on **Pause chat for everyone**.

Turn the same switch off to let chat answer again. Timers, alarms and emergency lines keep working during the pause.

## The AI is restarting

Home tries to restart an AI engine that crashes. Wait for chat to be ready, then send your message again.

Owners and admins get a notification when an engine recovers. If the AI service does not answer for two minutes, they get a restart notice and a warning in **Repairs**. After six minutes, the repair says "The AI could not restart itself: open Repairs". Ask an owner or admin to open it and follow the steps. The repair clears when the service answers again.

## Why is my picture, video or song slow?

If the card says "Paused while chat is working", the job is waiting while someone chats or talks to Home. It carries on by itself after the reply. The wait is not counted against the job. You do not need to do anything.

"Waiting for memory to free up" means another big job holds the memory. Wait for it to finish. Home makes one big job at a time, so your job may wait for another picture, video or song.

## 🔁 Try a fresh start

Tap **New chat** and ask something short. If that works, the old chat may be the problem.
