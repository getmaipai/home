---
title: Sites and news a child sees
description: Turn site types and news sources on or off for kids, teens and adults.
---

This page is for owners and admins.

## ✍️ Steps

1. Tap **Settings**, then **Search and web**.
2. Find the **Blocked sites** card.
3. Find the kind of site you want to change: adult, gambling, scam, phishing, malware or drugs.
4. Tick or untick the box under **Kids**, **Teens** or **Adults**.
5. Your choice saves as you tick it.

Adult sites stay blocked for children and teens. Their boxes are greyed out and cannot be changed.

## 📰 News

The **News shelf** card sits on the same page. It has a row for each news list and the same **Kids**, **Teens** and **Adults** boxes. A child only reads lists made for children. A dash means the list is not offered to that group.

<Callout type="info">These lists are for the household. They do not read any chat.</Callout>
