This page is for owners and admins.

<Shot id="child-topics" />

## ✍️ Steps

1. Tap **Family**, then the child's card. Their **Profile** opens.
2. On **Overview** you see shared files and the child's details.
3. Tap **Memories** to see what MaiPai remembers about them.
4. Tap **Limits** to set daily time limits and storage. The time limits are saved but not applied yet.
5. Tap **Topics** to choose how MaiPai answers them about sensitive subjects.

Only owners and admins see **Limits** and **Topics**.

## 💛 When a child has a hard moment

If a conversation seems to weigh on a child, every adult gets a notice: "A check-in may help." It names the child but never says what was said. It cannot be turned off. Talk to the child, kindly and in person.

## 🔐 Teens are different

A teen's profile, memories and settings are private. A parent gets no check-in view of a teen.

## Cartoon pictures and videos

Only an owner or admin can change these controls:

- Open **Settings**, **Home settings**, **Pictures, videos and music**. **Let kids make cartoon pictures and videos** starts on. Children can make cartoons as soon as a picture model is installed.
- On that same page, **Cartoon pictures a child can make each day** starts at 20. Changing one of the child's own cartoons counts as one picture. **Cartoon videos a child can make each day** starts at 5. Set either limit to 0 for none.
- Open **People**, choose the child, then open **Limits**. **This child can make cartoon pictures and videos** starts on while the home switch is on. Turn it off to stop cartoons for that child. Only owners and admins see **Limits**.
