This page is for owners and admins.

## ✍️ Steps

1. Tap **Family**, then the child's card.
2. Tap the **Topics** tab.
3. Find the **Sensitive subjects** group.
4. Choose a level for each subject.
5. Your choice saves as you make it.

Only owners and admins can change these. The child cannot.

## 🛡️ What never changes

Some things stay the same for every child:

- Feelings and safety come first. MaiPai points the child to a trusted grown-up.
- MaiPai never helps with bullying, secrets, or sharing where they live.
- It takes no side on religion or politics.
- If a child is in danger, it points to a grown-up and the emergency number.

## 🔐 Teens

There is no per-teen control. Household settings in **Settings**, **Privacy** apply to every teen. A teen's own settings are private.
