## ✍️ Steps

1. Tap **Family** in the main menu.
2. Stay on the **People** tab. Each person has one card with their name and role.
3. Tap a card to open that person's profile.

<Shot id="people" />

## 🏷️ The roles

A role is a label that sets what a person may do: **Owner**, **Admin**, **Adult**, **Teen**, **Child** or **Guest**. See [What each role can do](/docs/user/your-family/what-each-role-can-do).

## 🐾 Other tabs

Family may also show **Pets**. Owners and admins also see **Bots**.

## 🔐 What you can open

You can always open your own profile. Owners and admins can also open a child's profile. Nobody can see a teen's or another adult's settings or memories, not even an admin. A teen's card in **Family** does not open for an admin, and typing the teen's address shows a message that the profile cannot be opened.

## ➕ Adding someone

Only an admin can add a person. Admins, see [Add people and parental controls](/docs/admin/people-and-safety/add-people-and-parental-controls).
