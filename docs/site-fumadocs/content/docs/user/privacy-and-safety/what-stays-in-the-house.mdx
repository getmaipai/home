---
title: What stays in the house
description: Your chats, memories and profiles stay on your own computer.
---

Your conversations, memories and family profiles stay on the computer in your house. MaiPai does not run a server that your family's information passes through. Nothing reports back about how you use it.

## ✍️ See for yourself

1. Tap **Privacy** in the main menu.
2. Read the answers to "Can someone outside see what we say to MaiPai?" and "Can someone reach into your house?"
3. Open **What leaves your house** to see every connection.

For each connection you see **When** it happens, **What it sends**, **Who gets it** and **How long they keep it**. Anything not listed does not happen.

## 📨 What can leave

A feature can send the small piece it needs, such as your search words or the place for a weather check. A web search sends the words you searched, never your name or address. Children's and teens' searches never use the Wikipedia fallback.

## 🕶️ Temporary chats

Adults can mark a chat as temporary. It is not saved to history or memory.

## 🙋 Still need help?

Not sure why a connection is listed? Ask the person who runs the house. Admins, see [Privacy audit](/docs/admin/look-after-it/privacy-audit).
