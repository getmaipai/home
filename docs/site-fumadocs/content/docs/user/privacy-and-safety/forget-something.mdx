---
title: Forget something
description: Delete a chat or a memory, or erase everything about a child.
---

Forgetting cannot be undone.

<Shot id="memory" />

## 💬 Delete a chat

1. Open the chat.
2. Tap the menu at the top, **Conversation actions**.
3. Tap **Delete**.

## 🧠 Forget a memory

1. Tap **Family**, then your own card.
2. Tap **Memories**.
3. Tap the archive-box button beside a memory. Or tap **Select memories**, tick some, and tap **Forget selected**.
4. To remove all of them, tap **Clear all**, then **Yes, forget all**. Choose **Keep them** to stop.

## 👶 Erase everything about a child

Owners and admins only:

1. Open the child's profile and tap **Memories**.
2. Tap **Forget everything about** and the child's name.
3. Confirm with **Yes, forget everything**.

Before you erase, tap **Export** and the child's name first if you want a copy.

## 🔐 Teens and adults

Only that person can forget their own memories. An admin cannot.
