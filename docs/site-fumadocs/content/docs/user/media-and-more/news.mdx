title: Read the news
description: Read stories from the feeds your home chose, follow topics and sources, and save stories.
---

**News** reads the same news shelf MaiPai uses when you ask for headlines. An admin chooses which feeds are on it.

## ✍️ Steps

1. Open **News** from your apps.
2. Pick **Today**, a topic or a source you follow in the sidebar. On a phone the choices are in the menu drawer.
3. Open a story to read it.
4. Save a story with its save button. Find it later under **Saved**.
5. Choose **Follow** on a topic or source to keep it under **Following**.

## 🔎 Find a story

Type in **Search News** and press Enter. Tap a chip to see only **Saved only**, **Today** or one topic. Home remembers your last five searches. **Clear** removes them. Only you see them.

## 🎧 Under a headline

- **Listen** reads the story in your hub's voice.
- **Key points** shows a short summary written on your hub.
- **Discuss** opens a chat about the story. It is for teens and adults.

## 🧠 My Mix

Teens and adults have **My Mix**. It picks stories from what you open and save. Open its menu and choose **Reset My Mix** to start over. Children get stories from the Kids' lists, and their reading is never used to learn interests. Guests do not get News.

## 🔒 Privacy

Your follows, saves and reading history stay on the hub. Opening a story may ask its publisher for the full text. See [What stays in the house](/docs/user/privacy-and-safety/what-stays-in-the-house).

<Callout type="info">An owner or admin chooses the news lists for each group. See [Sites and news a child sees](/docs/user/your-family/sites-and-news-a-child-sees).</Callout>
