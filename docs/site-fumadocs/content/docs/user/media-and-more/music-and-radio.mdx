title: Play music and radio

---

The **Music** app plays songs saved in your home and songs made at home. **Radio** is inside it.

## 🎵 Play a song

1. Open **Music** from the apps list or **Media**.
2. Pick **Listen now**, **Songs**, **Albums**, **Artists** or **Playlists**. Type in **Search Music** to find one.
3. Open an album, artist or playlist and press **Play**, **Shuffle** or **Start radio**.
4. Press **Like** to keep a song in **Liked**. Press **Add to playlist** to save it on your newest playlist.

The small player at the bottom keeps playing while you look around. Tap it to go back to **Now playing**. The lyrics light up as the song plays.

## 📻 Listen to the radio

1. In **Music**, open **Radio**.
2. Pick a station under **Your stations**, or one from a row such as **Popular now** or **Jazz**.
3. Press **Play**.

Type in **Search stations** and press **Add** to save one. Teens and adults can press **Record** on a saved station to record 15 minutes. The recording shows under **Recordings** on that station's page.

Children play only the stations a parent saved.

## 🛠️ Make your own music (adults)

Open **Create** in the left column. Choose **Song**, **Band**, **Album**, **Style**, **Sound** or **Voice**. You can also ask in chat. See [Songs](/docs/user/everyday-use/make-songs).

**Studio**, **Stems** and **Remix and edit** change a song you already have.

## 🔒 Who decides what

An admin adds the music folder in **Settings**, **Storage**. To let your home look for stations online, an owner or admin opens **Settings**, **Pictures, videos and music**, the **Music** tab, **Online radio and podcasts**, and turns on **Find radio stations online**. The station then sees your home's internet address.

Children and teens never see songs marked explicit.

<Callout type="info">The Music DJ that speaks between songs is off for children and teens until a parent turns it on. Find it in **Settings**, **Chat and AI**, **Personalization**, **Music**.</Callout>
