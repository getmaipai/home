title: Watch shows and films
description: Browse the shows and films on your hub, play the next episode, and keep a watchlist.
---

**Shows and Films** plays files on your home hub. It does not look up posters or details online.

## ✍️ Steps

1. Open **Shows and Films** from your apps.
2. Press **Play** on the big picture, or tap a poster for more info.
3. For a show, choose a season, then tap an episode.
4. Watch. A bar shows how much you have seen. A glowing edge marks the episode to watch next.

**Play** starts the next unfinished episode, or resumes your place.

## 📋 Keep a watchlist

On a title's page, choose **My list** to save it, or **Mark finished** when you are done. Playing a saved title moves it to **Watching**.

Open **Watchlist**. Use **Status** to show **Want to watch**, **Watching** or **Completed**, or pick **All**. Your list and your place belong to you.

## ▶️ While you watch

- **Skip intro** and **Skip credits** appear when the file has those chapters.
- At the end of an episode, the player can offer the next one.
- Saved files play with the internet off while your hub is within reach.

## 👶 For children

A child sees only titles cleared for them. The row says **Keep watching**. The player never starts the next episode by itself.

## ➕ No titles?

Ask a grown-up to add a library folder in **Settings**, **Storage**.
