title: Pick or make a companion
description: Choose how your assistant sounds, set a default, and make your own companion.
---

A companion is how your assistant sounds. Some are plain and short. Some are cheerful and ask questions.

## ✍️ Pick one

1. Open **Companions** from All apps.
2. On **Yours**, open a companion. Choose **Discover** to browse more and **Add** to put one in your list.
3. Press **Play a sample** to hear its voice.
4. Press **Chat with** to start a chat, or open the card's menu and choose **Set as my default**.

In a chat, the companion picker in the header changes it for that chat only. Type **@** and a name to ask another companion one question.

## 👀 Who sees which

Each card says who it is for. A companion for everyone adapts to a child or an adult. Teen and adult companions need that age or older. You only see the ones that fit you.

## 🎨 Make your own

Teens and adults can. Children ask an owner or admin.

1. Choose **New companion**.
2. **Create Character.** Describe it or use **Upload Photo**, pick a style, then **Generate Character**. Choose **Approve & Continue**.
3. **Animate Character.** Choose **Generate Required**, then **Continue**.
4. **Customize Companion.** Pick a voice and personality, write a backstory, then **Save Companion**.

Home saves a draft if you close early. To change a companion you made, use the menu and choose **Edit**. One that comes with Home needs **Make a copy**.

<Callout type="info">A companion changes the style of an answer. It never makes an answer less safe for a child.</Callout>

## 🔧 Owners and admins

On a companion you made, **Audience** sets who can use it. An owner or admin can turn companions off for a person. Robots use companions too: open the robot in **Settings**, **Apps and devices**, **Devices**.

## 🙋 Still need help?

If Companions says it is turned off, ask an owner or admin. See also [Still stuck](/docs/user/fix-a-problem/still-stuck).
