A synth can be based on a real person outside your home, such as an author, an athlete or a leader from history. It takes that person's real name and history. It does not make anything up about them.

## ✍️ Make one

Only an adult can. This works for people who are alive and for people who are not.

1. Open **Synths** from All apps and choose **New synth**.
2. Under **Based on**, choose **A real person**. The choices are **Nobody**, **A household member** and **A real person**. You only see the last one if you are an adult.
3. Type the name in **Enter a real person's name**.
4. Follow the steps, then save.

The card always says "Based on" and the name. You can give the synth another display name.

## 🔎 How Home learns about them

Right after you save, Home researches the person. It reads news on your News shelf and runs a web search for their name. From what it finds, it keeps:

- up to five dated events, each with where it came from
- how the person talks, and short phrases they are known for, each with a source
- up to six topics with their stated view, each with a source

Home then checks again about once a week. The check runs only when web search is on. There is a daily limit for the whole house.

Only the person's name leaves your home, as a search, the same as when you search for them yourself. Nothing about your family goes with it.

If the person dies, the synth stays. The death becomes one more dated fact.

A copy of a real-person synth does not research again. It reads the research of the one it came from.

## 👨‍👩‍👧 Who may talk with one

Adults can use them. For a child or teen, a parent or admin sets **Synths based on real people** in that person's settings:

- **Off.** None are shown.
- **Ask.** Each one needs a parent to say yes first. A yes lasts for that synth. A child starts on Ask and cannot be set to All.
- **All.** Every one is allowed. Teens only.

**Real-person synths a parent allowed** lists the yeses. A parent can remove one there.

If the real person is under 18, Home knows it and applies the child safety check to everything about that synth.

## 🏠 A person in your house

Choose **A household member** to base a synth on someone at home. That person can turn off **Synths can be based on me**. If it is off, Home says so and makes nothing. For a child or teen, a guardian makes this choice.

<Callout type="info">A synth changes the style of an answer. It never makes an answer less safe for a child.</Callout>
