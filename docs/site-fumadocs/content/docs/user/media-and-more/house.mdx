title: Control your house

The **House** page shows your rooms and the devices in them. It needs Home Assistant, the free smart-home hub, to be connected first.

## 🔌 Connect Home Assistant (owner or admin)

1. Open **Settings**, then **House**.
2. Under **Connection**, enter the Home Assistant address and its access token. The same boxes are under **Settings**, **Apps and devices**, **Integrations**.
3. Press **Test connection**.

Home says "Connected" and how many rooms and devices it found. Until then the House page says "Home Assistant is not set up yet" and offers **Open Settings**.

## 🧑‍🤝‍🧑 Choose who can do what

On **Settings**, **House**, **Who can control what** lists each room and device. For each person choose **None**, **See** or **Control**. Owners are always allowed. Locks are **Locked by rule** for children, and a teen needs a choice on that very lock.

## 🏠 Use the House page

Open **House**. Each room shows how many devices are on.

- A light has a switch and a brightness control.
- A scene has an activate button.
- A lock asks you to confirm.
- A switch, fan or blind turns on and off, or opens and closes.

You only see the devices shared with you. If none are, the page says "No devices are shared with you yet."

## 💬 Ask in chat

Say "Turn on the kitchen lights" or "Lock the front door." MaiPai acts only on devices you may control and tells you what it did. Locking doors needs a teen or older with **Control** on the lock. After an action, **Undo** appears.

## 🙋 Still need help?

If a device says it did not respond, try again. Otherwise see [Still stuck](/docs/user/fix-a-problem/still-stuck).
