title: Watch videos
description: Open the Videos app, play a video from your hub, pick up where you stopped, and save videos for later.
---

The **Videos** app plays the videos saved on your hub. It works with the internet off, and it stays dark so pictures look their best.

## ✍️ Steps

1. Open **Videos** from the apps list or your favorites.
2. Press a picture on the **Videos** page, or type in **Search Videos**.
3. Press the big play button.
4. Tap the left or right side twice to jump 15 seconds. Press and hold for double speed.
5. Press **Watch later** to save it, or **Like** to mark it.

When you come back, the video starts where you stopped. **Start over** is beside the time.

## 🧭 Around the app

- **Continue watching** and **Made at home** are rows on the first page.
- **Library** shows every video by channel.
- **Watch later** and **History** are your own lists. Nobody else sees them.
- Leave a video while it plays and a small bar at the bottom keeps it going. Press **Close** to stop it.
- When a video ends, the next one starts after a short countdown. You can stop the countdown.

## 🎥 Make videos

Grown-ups also see **Create**, **Studio** and **My clips** in the left column. To ask chat for a video, see [Make short videos](/docs/user/everyday-use/make-short-videos).

## 👶 For children

A child sees the videos the family has cleared for children. If you cannot find one, ask a grown-up.

## ➕ Add videos

An admin adds a folder of videos in **Settings**, **Storage**. Home checks the folder on a schedule.
