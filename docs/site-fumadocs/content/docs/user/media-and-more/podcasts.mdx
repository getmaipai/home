title: Listen to podcasts

---

The **Podcasts** app plays the shows your home follows. A new episode plays right away from the show's own website. A saved episode plays with the internet off.

## ✍️ Steps

1. Open **Podcasts** from the apps list or your favorites.
2. Press a picture under **Up Next**, **New episodes** or **Your shows**.
3. Press the big play button.
4. Use the speed button, the moon button (sleep timer) or the chapter button if the show has chapters.

Come back later and the episode starts where you stopped.

## 📌 Keep an episode

Under the player:

- **Add to queue** puts it on your **Queue** page.
- **Save on the hub** keeps a copy for the internet-off days. Only grown-ups can save one. Pressing it again moves the copy to Trash for 30 days.
- **Bookmark** marks the place you are at. Your bookmarks are on the **Saved** page.

**Filters** saves a list, such as unplayed and under 30 minutes. **Search transcripts** in **Episodes** finds words said in an episode.

## ➕ Add a show

Only a grown-up with an admin account can add or remove a show. Type in **Search podcasts**, then press **Follow**. You can also paste a feed address that starts with `https://`, or import an OPML file from another podcast app.

## 🔒 Online lookups

An owner or admin opens **Settings**, **Pictures, videos and music**, the **Music** tab, **Online radio and podcasts**. **Find podcasts online** lets adults search a public list. **Let teens find podcasts** is off until you turn it on. Children never can.

## 👶 For children

A child sees only the shows a grown-up chose and episodes marked clean.
