---
title: Use the Library and Trash
description: Find, add, share and delete the pictures, videos, songs and files in your home, and bring deleted things back.
---

The **Library** holds every picture, video, song and file you can see in Home. Open it from the folder icon in the left rail.

## ✍️ Find and open a file

1. Pick a section in the column: **All files**, **Pictures**, **Videos**, **Songs** or **Documents**.
2. Type in **Search your Library**, or use **Mine**, **Only me** and **Newest** to narrow the list.
3. Pick a file to open it big. Press the **i** button for details, and **Esc** to close.

## ➕ Add and share

- Pick **Upload**, or drag a file onto the page. Files can be up to 500 MB. A child sees **Ask to add a file**.
- Open a file and pick **Share**. Turn on a person or **Everyone in the home**. Turn it off to stop sharing. **Send** uses your device's own share sheet.

A child cannot share. A picture marked adults only is never shown to a teen or a child.

## 🗑️ Delete and bring back

1. Open a file and pick **Delete**. It moves to **Trash**, and **Undo** appears.
2. Open **Trash** in the Library. Trash keeps it for 30 days.
3. Press **Restore** to bring it back, or **Delete forever** and confirm.

You can also reach Trash in **Settings**, **Your data and apps**, **Data and privacy**, **Storage**, then **Trash**. It holds deleted media and playlists too. Lists and memories do not use this Trash.

**Keep** stops Home from ever removing a file to make room. Files you add yourself are always kept.

## 🔄 Many files at once

Pick **Select** (or press **S**), tick the files, then use **Share**, **Keep** or **Delete**.

## 📊 Your storage

The bar shows how much of your space you used. An admin can open **Everyone's storage**. See [Storage](/docs/admin/look-after-it/storage).

## 🙋 Still need help?

If a file is missing, ask the person who runs the house. To find files made in chat, see [Find and share your files](/docs/user/everyday-use/find-and-share-your-files).
