---
title: Read books and listen to audiobooks
description: Read books and PDFs, listen to audiobooks, and pick up where you stopped, on your own hub.
---

The **Books** app opens the books and audiobooks saved on your hub. It works with the internet off.

## 📖 Read a book

1. Open **Books** from the apps list.
2. Press a cover under **EPUB and PDF** or **Continue reading**.
3. Press the play button next to **Read**.
4. Press the arrows, or the left and right keys, to turn the page.
5. Press **Close** to leave. Home saves your place on every page turn.

Open the book again on any screen in your home and it opens on the page where you stopped.

## 🎧 Listen to an audiobook

1. Press a cover under **Audiobooks**.
2. Press the play button next to **Listen**.
3. Use back 15 seconds, forward 15 seconds and the speed button. **Chapters** lists every chapter.

Leave the player and the sound keeps playing in a small bar at the bottom. Press the moon button for a **Sleep timer**: 15, 30, 45 or 60 minutes.

## 🔗 Read and listen to the same book

When a book and an audiobook share a title and author and sit in the same folder, Home links them. The book's page shows both **Read** and **Listen**. Each keeps its own place.

## ➕ Add books

An admin adds book folders in **Settings**, **Storage**.

## 👶 For children

A child sees only books marked for children, from the folders a grown-up chose.

<Callout type="info">Reading aloud, bookmarks, notes and a font size choice are not built yet.</Callout>
