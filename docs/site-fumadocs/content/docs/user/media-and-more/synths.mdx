A synth is an AI being. It has a name, a personality, a voice and a life that carries on from day to day. You give a synth a job.

## 🎭 Two jobs

- **Companion.** Your assistant in chat. Press **Make this my companion** on a synth and it answers you from then on. In a chat, the picker in the header changes it for that chat only. Type **@** and a name to ask another one a question.
- **Cast.** A part in a spoken show, such as a news briefing, a round table or a film review. A host remembers last week and can mention your birthday or the weather.

One synth can do both.

## ✍️ Find one

1. Open **Synths** from All apps.
2. On **Yours**, open a card. Choose **Discover** to browse more and **Add** to put one in your list.
3. Press **Play a sample** to hear its voice.
4. Press **Chat with** to start a chat, or open the card's menu and choose **Make this my companion**.

Each card says who it is for and whose it is. Some are yours. Some are based on another synth, and the card says which. You only see the ones that fit you.

## 🎨 Make your own

Teens and adults can. Children ask an owner or admin. Choose **New companion**, then follow the three steps: look, animation, and voice and personality. Home saves a draft if you close early.

A synth that comes with Home cannot be changed. Choose **Make a copy**, then edit your copy. To go back to how a synth started, choose **Start fresh from the base**. To let the household use one you made, choose **Share with the household**.

## 🔧 Owners and admins

On a synth you made, **Audience** sets who can use it. An owner or admin can turn synths off for a person. Robots use synths too, but only ones the whole household can use. A parent decides whether children and teens can use a synth based on a real person.

<Callout type="info">A synth changes the style of an answer. It never makes an answer less safe for a child.</Callout>

## 🙋 Still need help?

If Synths says it is turned off, ask an owner or admin. See also [Pick or make a companion](/docs/user/media-and-more/companions) and [Still stuck](/docs/user/fix-a-problem/still-stuck).
