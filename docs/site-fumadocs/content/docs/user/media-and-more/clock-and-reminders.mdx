You can work by hand in the **Clock** and **Reminders** apps, or type or say it in chat.

## ⏰ Set an alarm

1. Open **Clock**, then the **Alarms** tab.
2. Press the plus button.
3. Under **Time**, choose when it rings. Under **Repeat**, tap the days, or leave them empty for one time.
4. Add a **Label**, **Sound** and **Snooze** time if you like.
5. Press **Save**.

Tap an alarm to change it. **Skip next** skips one ring. An alarm set by an adult or a teen rings even in quiet hours. A child's alarm waits until quiet hours end.

## ⏱️ Timers, world clocks and the stopwatch

- **Timers**: press the plus button under **Quick timers**, type a **Name** and **Minutes**, then press **Start**.
- **World clocks**: press **Add a place** and pick a city. It works without the internet.
- **Stopwatch**: press **Start**, **Lap** and **Stop**.

## 💬 Say it in chat

- "Add milk to the shopping list." "What's on my shopping list?"
- "Remind me tomorrow at noon to walk the dog." "Snooze that reminder."
- "Set an alarm for 7." "Wake me at 6:30 on weekdays."
- "Set a timer for 10 minutes."

After a reminder or timer, **Undo** shows for 10 minutes. You must be signed in as yourself, or MaiPai will not set an alarm.

MaiPai asks first before it deletes or clears a list, or changes more than 5 items at once.

## 🔒 Who can do what

Children and teens can add to the home shopping list, tick items off, and run their own lists, reminders and timers. A parent does the rest.

## 🎚️ How much MaiPai does on its own

An owner or admin opens **Settings**, **AI and models**, **Advanced**. The **Reminder actions**, **Timer actions** and **List actions** rows each take **Do at once**, **Ask first** or **Off**. Your own alarm choices are in **Settings**, **Clock**.
