title: What MaiPai Home is

MaiPai Home puts the AI apps people usually pay for under one roof. Chat, music, pictures, videos, podcasts, news, books and your smart home all run on a computer in your house. They share one memory, one Library and one set of family profiles. Your chats, memories and family details stay there.

## 🧩 What it does, and what it replaces

- **Chat.** Ask questions, talk out loud, and see where answers came from. It remembers what helps you, and you can see or erase any memory. This is what you would do in ChatGPT.
- **Songs and albums.** Describe a song and get a track with lyrics. Make a whole album, split a song into parts, or remix it. This is what you would do in Suno.
- **Pictures.** Make a picture, try variations, or edit one by describing the change. This is what you would do in Midjourney.
- **Videos and films.** Start from words or a picture, then trim, join and add voices and songs. This is what you would do in Runway.
- **Music, radio and podcasts.** Play your music, tune in radio, follow podcasts, or have a daily show spoken for you. This is what you would do in Spotify and a podcast app.
- **Shows, films and books.** Watch your saved videos and read books or listen to audiobooks. Each picks up where you stopped.
- **News and Weather.** Read the news you chose and see weather for your places.
- **Library.** Every picture, video, song and document in one place, with sharing and a trash you can restore from. This is what you would do in Google Photos.
- **Reminders and your house.** Set reminders, lists, alarms and timers, and control lights and locks with Home Assistant.
- **Synths.** AI beings with a name, a voice and a life. A synth can be your companion in chat or play a part in a show. See [Synths](/docs/user/media-and-more/synths).

## 👨‍👩‍👧 Who uses it

Everyone in the house gets their own profile. A grown-up runs the house and decides what children can see. Teens and adults keep their own chats private.

## 🔒 What stays private

Your chats and memories are not sent to MaiPai or anyone else. A few features, like a web search, send only the small piece they need. The Privacy page lists each one. See [What stays in the house](/docs/user/privacy-and-safety/what-stays-in-the-house).
