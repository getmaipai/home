The left rail runs down the side on a computer. On a phone, the app name and actions sit in the top bar, and the app's sections are in a bar at the bottom of the screen.

## 🧭 The left rail

Six places are always there:

- **Home**: a greeting, an ask box and a quick look at your day.
- **Search**: find chats, people and pages.
- **Chat**: talk to MaiPai. Your past chats are listed here too.
- **Media**: one place for Videos, Shows and Films, Music, Podcasts and Books. See [Media and more](/docs/user/media-and-more/music-and-radio).
- **Library**: the files you and the family have added.
- **Family**: one card for each person. Tap a card to open that person's profile.

Under those sit your favorite apps, and the **All apps** grid button at the bottom opens every app you can use. See [Find and favorite your apps](/docs/user/everyday-use/find-and-favorite-your-apps).

Your own picture or initial sits at the bottom of the rail. Tap it to open your profile menu. It holds **Notifications**, **System status**, **Incognito**, **Settings** and **Help**, and the way to sign out.

## 🔔 The bell

The bell shows new notices. See [See your notifications](/docs/user/everyday-use/see-your-notifications).

## ✍️ Steps to try it

1. Tap **Chat** and start a new chat.
2. Tap **Family** and tap your own card.
3. Tap your picture, then **Settings**, then **Your data and apps** to see what MaiPai remembers about you.
4. Look at the pages in **Settings** to see what you can change.

<Callout type="info">Some settings show a lock. A lock means an admin controls that one.</Callout>
