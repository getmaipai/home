The main menu runs down the left side on a computer. On a phone, the app name and actions sit in the top bar, and the app's sections are in a bar at the bottom of the screen.

## 🧭 The main menu

- **Home**: counts for people, updates and repairs, and recent activity.
- **Chat**: talk to MaiPai. Your past chats are listed here too.
- **Library**: the files you and the family have added.
- **Family**: one card for each person. Tap a card to open that person's profile, where memories live.
- **News**: headlines and stories from the news shelf.
- **Music**: music saved in your home and songs made in chat.
- **Companions**: choose a companion for a chat or add one to your list.

Your own picture or initial sits at the bottom of the menu. Tap it to open your profile menu. It holds **Notifications**, **Incognito**, **Settings** and **Help**, and the way to sign out. Adults also see household settings under **Settings**. To see what stays in the house, read [What stays in the house](/docs/user/privacy-and-safety/what-stays-in-the-house).

The search box at the top finds chats, people and pages.

## 🔔 The bell

The bell shows new notices. See [See your notifications](/docs/user/everyday-use/see-your-notifications).

## ✍️ Steps to try it

1. Tap **Chat** and start a new chat.
2. Tap **Family** and tap your own card.
3. Tap **Memories** to see what MaiPai remembers about you.
4. Tap **Settings** to see what you can change.

<Callout type="info">Some settings show a lock. A lock means an admin controls that one.</Callout>
