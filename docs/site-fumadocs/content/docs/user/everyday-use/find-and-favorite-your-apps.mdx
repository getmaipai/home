---
title: Find and favorite your apps
description: Open the All apps page, search it, and choose which apps sit in the left rail.
---

Every app you can open is on one page. Press the grid button at the bottom of the left rail. Its name is **All apps**.

## Find an app

1. Open **All apps**. The search box is ready, so type the app name.
2. The list shortens as you type. Home looks in each app's name, its short description, and its topic.
3. Press the down arrow to move onto the apps. Use the arrow keys to move around and Enter to open one.
4. If nothing matches, Home says so. Press **Clear search** to see everything again.

With nothing typed, your **Favorites** come first. The other apps follow, grouped by topic. The apps that come with Home are last, under **Built in**.

## Choose your favorites

Pin an app from the left rail to keep it there. Open the app's **Settings** and choose **Sidebar** to turn its pin on or off. Your pinned apps sit in the left rail under Chat, Library and Family, so one tap opens them.

The rail holds six pinned apps. Remove one pin to free a spot.

Your favorites are yours. Nobody else in the house sees them.

## Which apps you see

**All apps** shows the apps your role can open. Apps that cannot be used in Incognito are hidden while Incognito is on. If an app is missing, leave Incognito and check again. Ask an owner or admin if it is still missing.

## Something not working?

| What you see | Why | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| An app is not on the page | Your role cannot open it, or it is turned off | Ask a grown-up who runs Home |
| A star will not turn on | You already have six favorites | Remove one favorite first |
| The left rail has no grid button | No apps are installed yet | Install an app, then look again |
