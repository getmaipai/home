---
title: Polish a video
description: Give a video a film look, widescreen bars, blends between clips and a sound that follows the picture.
---

You do all of this in chat, on a video you made. These changes are for adults. Home keeps your old video and makes a new one next to it.

## 🎨 Film looks

Say "give it a noir look". Home picks from eight looks:

- 70s color
- Noir
- Teal and orange
- Bleach bypass
- Technicolor
- Faded vintage
- Cool thriller
- Night

You can ask for a softer or stronger look. Strength runs from 0.2 to 1. You can also set a look for a whole film when you ask for it.

There is no look picker yet. That is Planned. For now, ask in words.

## 🎬 Widescreen bars

Say "add widescreen bars". Choose **2.39:1**, **2:1**, **1.85:1** or **1.33:1**.

## 🖌️ Style cards

A style card changes how a new clip is drawn, such as a clay look. Styles are installed by an owner or admin. If one is on, you can pick it in **Create**, and Home may suggest one as you type. See [Make short videos](/docs/user/everyday-use/make-short-videos#add-a-style-for-videos).

## 🔀 Blends between clips

When you ask to join clips, you can ask for a blend instead of a straight cut. Home has 20 blends:

- fade, dissolve, speckle, pixelate
- wipe left, right, up or down
- slide left, right, up or down
- circle open, circle close, radial
- smooth left, smooth right
- fade to black, fade to white
- zoom in

Each clip has to be longer than twice the blend. A film plan can also use a plain cut.

## 🔊 Sound that follows the picture

<Callout type="warn">Preview. This is built, but it is not on the live hub yet, and nobody has heard the result on a surround system.</Callout>

On a finished video card in chat, open **More**, then choose **Spatial sound** or **Spatial sound with 5.1 file**. A voice then comes from the side where the speaker is. Wear headphones to hear it.

1. Pick a video that has its own sound.
2. Choose **Spatial sound**. Home makes a new stereo clip.
3. For a surround file, choose **Spatial sound with 5.1 file** instead. The 5.1 file is its own video in your Library, named "Spatial sound 5.1".
4. The 5.1 file plays on a surround system or an Apple TV. Other players play it as stereo.

Surround comes from the clip's own sound. Home moves sound that is already there. It does not add any. Adding a 5.1 sound file of your own to a stereo clip is Planned and does not work yet.

If a clip has no sound, Home says so and makes nothing. If the sound already moves with the picture, Home leaves it as it is.
