---

## ✍️ Steps

1. Tap the bell, or open your profile menu and tap **Notifications**. A small list of new notices opens.
2. Tap **Dismiss** beside a notice to clear it, or **Dismiss all** to clear the list.
3. Tap **View history** to see older ones.
4. On the history page, tap **Select**, choose some, then tap **Dismiss selected**. Or tap **Clear all**.

<Shot id="notifications" />

History goes back 30 days. A notice you cleared shows a **Dismissed** badge. One you opened shows **Read**. If nothing came in, you see "Nothing in the last 30 days."

## 👨‍👩‍👧 If you are a parent

You also get a note when a child had a conversation that seemed to weigh on them. It carries the child's name only, never what was said. It cannot be turned off.

## 📨 Notices elsewhere

An admin can also send notices to Telegram. See [Notifications and Telegram](/docs/admin/connect-and-extend/notifications-and-telegram).

## 🙋 Still need help?

If the bell stays empty and you expected a notice, ask the person who runs the house.
