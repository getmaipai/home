MaiPai remembers things that help it answer you, such as a favorite food. You can see them and remove them.

## ✍️ Steps to see them

1. Tap your picture at the bottom of the left rail, then **Settings**.
2. Open **Your data and apps**.
3. The **Data and privacy** tab lists what MaiPai remembered from your conversations.

<Shot id="memory" />

If it says "Nothing remembered yet.", there is nothing stored.

## 🗑️ Forget one

Tap the archive-box button beside a memory. It is removed from what MaiPai uses.

## 🗑️ Forget several

1. Tap **Select memories**.
2. Tick the ones to remove.
3. Tap **Forget selected**, then confirm. Or tap **Clear all** to remove every one.

Forgetting cannot be undone. Choose **Keep them** if you change your mind.

## 👥 Who may hear a memory

Adults can choose who a memory may be shared with: everyone, teens and adults, or adults only.
