---
title: Pictures
description: Ask chat to make a picture, stop it, change it, and choose who can make pictures and which styles are on.
---

Home can make pictures on your own computer. Nothing is sent to anyone.

## Ask in chat

Type "make me a picture of a red fox in the snow" or "draw a lighthouse at dusk". Home answers in a few words and shows a card that says how far along the picture is and how many seconds it has taken. Press **Stop** on the card to cancel.

You can ask for up to 4 pictures at once ("give me three options") and for a shape: square (the default), portrait, landscape, wide or tall ("a tall poster"). Say "another one", "one more" or "the same again" to make a new picture. If you leave the page, the picture keeps going.

When it is done, the picture appears in the chat. Tap it to see it bigger. A picture takes about four seconds on a fast computer and longer on a slower one.

## Change a picture

- **Edit.** Press **Edit** on the picture. In the editor, choose **Comment**, click the part you want to change (or drag a box), and say what you want, like "make the sky pink". Home shows the new picture as a preview. Press **Apply** to keep it as a new version, or discard it. Only the part you pointed at changes.
- **The editor tools.** Press **Edit** on a picture to open it in the editor: **Remove background** (keeps the subject), **Erase** (paint over something and Home fills the hole), **Upscale** (2x or 4x, bigger and sharper), **Resize**, **Markup** (draw or write on it) and **Comment** (above). The first four run on your computer in a few seconds and need no picture model. A change you apply becomes a new version, and the original stays.
- **Make a variation.** Open the **More** menu under the reply and choose **Make a variation**. You get another picture from the same words.
- **Try again.** If a picture did not work, the card says why and has a **Try again** button.

## Who can make pictures

Adults can, on their own screen, in a written chat. Children and teens cannot. Spoken answers, the TV and other shared screens never make pictures. A guest cannot.

Two things are never made, whatever the settings: anything sexual that shows a child or someone who looks like one, and a picture of a real person who has not agreed to it. If you ask, Home says it will not make that and what to change. Home checks finished pictures and videos and will not show anything that may show a child in a sexual situation. If that happens, the picture or video is deleted and an owner or admin gets a note without the picture. The check uses the chat model on this computer, so nothing leaves it. If Home cannot check a picture or video for an adult-only request, it does not show it; ask again in a minute.

## Show a picture to Home

This is different from making a picture. Attach one of your own pictures with the **+** button and ask about it. Home reads it on your own computer and sends it nowhere. Adults and teens have this on by default. It is off for children until a parent turns on **Send photos in chat** for them, and a parent can turn it off for a teen. Home does not say who is in a photo.

## Choose how it works (owner and admin)

Open **Settings**, **Home settings**, **Pictures and videos**.

- **Adults can make pictures.** Turn it off to stop all picture making.
- **Adult-only styles.** Choose whether styles marked adults only can be used.
- **Picture model.** Shows the model that makes pictures. If none is installed, press **Install the picture model**. It downloads once.
- **Styles.** A style gives pictures a look, like pixel art. Each has a switch, a note on who can use it, its licence and its size. Remove one to delete its file.

### Add a style

1. Open **Add a style**.
2. Give it a name and paste a Hugging Face link to the file, or choose a file from this computer.
3. Say what the licence is and tick the box to say you read it.
4. Say if it is fine for all ages. If you are not sure, only adults can use it.
5. Tick the box to say it is not a likeness of a real person.
6. Press **Add style**. Check the fingerprint and licence it shows, then press **Turn it on**.

The picture engine checks the file and refuses styles of real people and anything that puts a child in an adult scene. It cannot catch everything, so only add styles you trust.

## Something not working?

| What you see | Why | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Home says it cannot make pictures for you | You are a child, a teen or a guest, or you are on a shared screen or a spoken chat | Ask an adult, or use your own written chat |
| "No picture model is installed" | The model is not on this computer | An owner or admin presses **Install the picture model** in Settings, Pictures and videos |
| "The picture engine isn't answering", or Home says it cannot reach the engine | The AI engine is restarting or stopped | Wait a minute and ask again. If it stays, open **Repairs** |
| "Waiting for memory to free up" | A video or another big job holds the memory | Wait. It starts by itself |
| "Something went wrong while making it" | The job failed | Press **Try again**, or change the words |
| The reply says it is coming but no picture shows yet | The picture started and the reply did not finish | Wait a minute. The card fills in |
| You asked chat to change a picture and got a new one | The picture tool cannot change an existing picture | Press **Edit** on the picture instead |
| A style file is refused | Over 120 MB, or it names a real person or a child in an adult scene | Use a smaller file or a different style |
| No **Edit** button | The picture is not in this chat | Open it from **Files** |

## Private chats

In an Incognito or temporary chat, the picture lives only in memory and is gone when the chat ends.
