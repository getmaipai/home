## ✍️ Steps

1. Open **Settings**, then **Profile and sign-in**.
2. Find the **Language** card.
3. Open the list and pick **English (United States)**, **English (United Kingdom)** or **Español**.

The screen changes at once. You do not sign out or reload.

<Shot id="profile-language" />

**Same as the home** is the first choice. It follows whatever language the owner or admin set for the home.

When you pick **Español** or **English (United Kingdom)**, a note says part of the text was translated by your home's own AI and may read oddly.

<Shot id="profile-language-es" />

## 🔤 What changes

Menus, buttons, settings, messages from Home, the bell and Telegram notices, and dates, times, numbers and units. The sign-in page uses the home's language, because Home does not know who you are until you sign in.

## 🇬🇧 What stays in English

- What you type, and what the AI writes back. The AI answers in the language you write to it in.
- Lines written for children and teens, safety and crisis messages, refusals, fixed voice lines and robot speech, until a grown-up has read and approved the translation.
- Names, such as MaiPai and Home.

A line with no translation yet shows in English. Some parts of the screen, such as the search box and the profile menu, are still in English while the translation is finished.

## 🙋 If a line reads wrong

Home's own AI wrote the Spanish on this computer. Nothing was sent to another company. Write down the screen and the line, and tell the person who runs your home. They can fix it for everyone.
