title: Find and share your files
description: See the stories, pictures and documents you made in chat, and share them.
---

## ✍️ Steps

1. In a chat, find a document or picture MaiPai made. Tap the **More** button beside it.
2. Tap **Show in Library**. The **Library** opens.
3. Type in **Search your Library**, or pick a section such as **Pictures** or **Documents**. **Mine** shows only what you added or made.
4. Pick a file to open it big. The **i** button shows details, including an **Origin** link back to the chat.

## 🤝 Share a file

Open the file and pick **Share**. Turn on a person, or **Everyone in the home**. Turn it off again to stop sharing. They lose access right away.

You can only share files that are yours. A file shared with you shows under **Shared with me**. A child cannot share.

## ⬇️ Keep a copy

Open the file and use **Download** to save a copy. **Send** uses your device's own share sheet.

If the Library is empty, it says "Nothing here yet. Stories, pictures and documents you make in chat are kept here."

For adding files, deleting, Trash and more, see [Use the Library and Trash](/docs/user/media-and-more/library-and-trash).

## 🙋 Still need help?

If a file you made is missing, ask the person who runs the house.
