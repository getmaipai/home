title: Ask the assistant to do things

Chat can act, not only answer. It acts only for things you are allowed to use, and it tells you what it did.

## 🏠 Home devices

Chat uses the same House tool as the House page. Say "turn on the kitchen lights", "set the fan to 40 percent" or "close the living room blinds". It can find, turn on and off, dim, change color, set speed, open and close, lock and unlock, start a scene, and set a temperature. Setup and who may control what are on [Control your house](/docs/user/media-and-more/house).

## ✅ The confirm step

For some things, chat asks first. Locks, alarms, garage doors and some scenes are the usual ones. The card says what it will do, for example "Please confirm turn on for 2 devices." Nothing happens until you confirm. A child needs a parent for anything sensitive.

An owner can turn Home controls off for the whole house. Chat then says they are turned off.

## ↩️ Undo

After an action, the card shows **Undo**. Press it to put things back. Undo never opens or unlocks a sensitive item. It only makes things safer, such as locking again.

## ⏰ Schedules

Adults can say "turn on the porch lights every day at 4pm" or "turn off the lamp at 10:30 tonight". Home can schedule turn on, turn off, brightness and scenes. Ask "what is scheduled" to list yours, and ask to cancel one. A plain reminder is not a schedule. "Remind me" uses the Reminders app.

## 🔌 Restart Home Assistant

Only an owner can ask chat to restart Home Assistant, one of its add-ons, or its host. Chat always asks first and says it cannot be undone. With more than one home connected, say which one.

If an Uptime Kuma server is connected, an adult can ask "is anything down right now?" Chat reads the status. It cannot change anything there. Other connector types are named in the code, but chat cannot use them yet.
