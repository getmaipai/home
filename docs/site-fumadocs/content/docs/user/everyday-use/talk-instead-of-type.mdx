## ✍️ Steps to talk

1. Open a chat.
2. Tap the microphone button (**Voice input**) in the message box.
3. If your browser asks to use the microphone, choose to allow it. MaiPai cannot hear you until you do. Safari asks every time unless you choose **Allow** in **Safari**, **Settings**, **Websites**, **Microphone**.
4. Say your question.
5. Tap **Stop dictation** when you finish. Your words appear in the box.
6. Check them, then send the message.

In Safari, choose your Home address in that list and set it to **Allow**. You can also open **Safari**, **Settings for this site**, **Microphone**, **Allow**. Home shows this tip once per visit when Safari is still on Ask.

## 🔊 Hear an answer

Under a reply, tap **Read aloud**. Tap **Stop reading** to quiet it. In the menu at the top, called **Conversation actions**, **Read replies aloud** reads every reply. You see it only when your house has a voice set up.

## Choose a voice

Open **Settings**, then **Voice and robot**, then the **Voice** tab. The **Voice for you** list starts with **Default voice**. It uses the voice built into the current speech engine. Tap **Test** to hear it. Your pick is saved when you choose it.

If your saved voice is no longer offered, Home uses the default voice and shows a note. Choose another voice from the list if you want. A child needs an owner or admin to choose for them.

### Change how fast it talks

Open **Settings**, **Voice and robot**, **Voice**. **Spoken reply speed** sets how fast MaiPai talks when it reads to you. 1 is normal. 1.25 is a little faster. 0.75 is slower. Fast speech also sounds a little higher. The setting is yours alone.

### One sound at a time

Only one thing plays at a time. If a podcast or a video is playing and MaiPai starts to read a reply, the podcast pauses. If you start a podcast or a video while MaiPai is reading, MaiPai goes quiet.

### Sleep timer

The players in Home's media apps, such as Podcasts and Books, have a **Sleep timer**. Pick 15, 30, 45 or 60 minutes, or the end of the chapter or episode. When the time is up, the sound stops, so you can fall asleep to a book. Podcast, audiobook and video speed are in the same place as your spoken reply speed, in **Settings**, **Voice and robot**, **Voice**.

## 🎙️ Wake word

On a computer where the wake word files are installed, owners, admins and adults can turn on wake word listening so they do not have to tap. Teens, children and guests do not get it. It is off until you turn it on.

1. Open **Settings**, then **Voice and robot**, then the **This device** tab. The tab shows up only when the wake word files are installed.
2. Turn on **Wake word**.
3. Allow the microphone if your browser asks.
4. Say "Hey Jarvis". That is the only wake word today, and you cannot change it. The voice conversation opens.
5. To stop, tap **Turn off wake word listening** in the bar that says "Listening for Hey Jarvis".

The setting belongs to this device, not to you. Listening is paused while a voice conversation is open and stops when you close the page. Home stops listening by itself if nobody speaks for a few seconds.

<Callout type="info">The microphone works only while the page is open and you have allowed it.</Callout>

## 🛠️ Something not working?

- **A help box opens when you start a conversation.** The browser or the Mac blocks the microphone. Follow the box for your browser, then tap **Try again**.
- **The conversation button is missing.** Speech in or out is not installed yet. Wait for the downloads, or look in **Repairs**.
- **No laughs or sighs.** An owner or admin sets **Natural speech sounds** to **Light** or **Natural** in **Settings**, **AI and models**, **Advanced**. Only the Chatterbox voice makes them, and children and teens never hear them.
