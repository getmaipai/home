---
title: Make a short film
description: Ask chat for a short film, check the plan, press Go, then redo a shot or change the music.
---

A film is a few shots joined with music. Home plans it, draws each shot, turns the shots into clips, adds music and puts it all together. Adults can make films on their own screen, in a written chat. Teens, children, guests, spoken answers and shared screens cannot.

## ✍️ Make one

1. Open a chat.
2. Type what you want. For example: "make a 40 second film of a paper boat in the rain". You can also name a length, a number of shots, a look, or saved characters.
3. Read the plan on the card. It shows a short summary and one line for each shot.
4. Press **Go**.
5. Watch the card. It moves through **Plan**, **Cast**, **Stills**, **Clips**, **Music** and **Assemble**.

The film plays on the card. If you say you want to skip the check, Home starts without waiting for **Go**.

A film takes a while. Home makes one picture or clip at a time. It waits whenever chat is busy.

## 🎞️ Films with no faces

Ask for a "faceless film" or a film from a topic. Home writes a script and reads it in a household voice. The pictures move slowly, with captions and music. No characters move.

## 🔁 Change a film

Open the **More** menu on a finished film.

- **Redo a shot 1**, **Redo a shot 2** and so on make that one shot again. The rest stays. The menu lists the first six shots.
- **Change the music** makes new music and puts it under the film.

If a film stops early, the card shows **Resume**. It picks up at the step that stopped. This also works after the hub restarts.

For a look, widescreen bars or the sound, see [Polish a video](/docs/user/everyday-use/polish-a-video).

## 📁 Where a film is kept

Every film is saved on a Production. A Production is a private folder for one film or show. It holds the title, the summary, the cast, the places and props, the shots with their pictures and clips, and the finished cuts.

- You own the Productions you make. Other people see one only if you share it with them by name. Guests see none.
- There is no Productions page yet. That is Planned. Today you reach a film from the chat card and from your Library.
- Redo a shot works because the Production keeps every picture and clip.
- A show Home makes for you, such as a news briefing, is kept the same way.

<Callout type="info">Every picture, clip and finished film passes the same safety check as other videos. If the check cannot run, Home keeps the film hidden.</Callout>

## 🙋 Still need help?

If chat does not offer a film, video may be off for you. See [Make short videos](/docs/user/everyday-use/make-short-videos#who-can-make-videos) or [Still stuck](/docs/user/fix-a-problem/still-stuck).
