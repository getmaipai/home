By default Home works only on your home Wi-Fi. To use it from anywhere, Home uses **Tailscale**, a free app that links your family's devices privately. Conversations go straight between the device and your Home computer. Tailscale sees device names and addresses, not what you say.

Home never turns this on by itself. Only an owner or admin can.

## ✅ Before you start

1. Install Tailscale on the Home computer from tailscale.com/download.
2. Sign in to Tailscale with the account your family's devices will use.
3. Open Tailscale's DNS settings and turn on HTTPS certificates.

Pick a plain computer name first. Turning this on publishes the computer's Tailscale name in public certificate logs, so do not use a family name. Headscale is not supported by this flow.

## ✍️ Turn it on

1. Open **Settings**, then **Advanced**. On the **Network and access** tab, press **Set up away access**. The setup checklist has the same button.
2. Step **Install and sign in**: press **Get Tailscale** if it is missing, then sign in. Home checks again by itself.
3. Step **Turn on secure addresses**: press **Open DNS settings** and turn on HTTPS certificates in Tailscale.
4. Press **Turn on away access**.
5. Step **Scan the code**: Home shows your Home address and a QR code.

If Tailscale is not signed in or HTTPS certificates are off, the step stays open until you fix that in Tailscale.

## 📱 Add a phone or tablet

1. Install Tailscale on the device.
2. Sign in with the same account.
3. Scan the QR code, or open the address.

The address works only on devices signed in to your Tailscale account.

## 🔧 Related settings

| Setting | Default | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **Away access through Tailscale** | Off | Set when you turn this on. It is on **Advanced**, **Network and access**. |
| **Who can reach Home** | LAN | Loopback keeps Home on this computer. LAN lets devices on your home network connect. Needs a restart. |
| **Home network port** | 3000 | The port Home listens on. Changing it changes the address devices use. |
| **Reach the engine computer when away from home** | Off | For a separate engine computer. See [Use another computer for the AI](/docs/admin/install-and-set-up/use-another-computer-for-the-ai). |

## 🔐 Privacy

Tailscale and the certificate authority are the two outside services involved. Both appear on the [privacy page](/docs/admin/look-after-it/privacy-audit), and only after you opt in.

The first-run setup wizard no longer has an away step. Finish away access here, or from the setup checklist.
