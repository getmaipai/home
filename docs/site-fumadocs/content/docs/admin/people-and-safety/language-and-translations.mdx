This page is for owners and admins.

## 🌍 Set the home's language

Open **Settings**, then **Household and people**. In the **Household** card, find **Language and region** and pick **en-US**, **en-GB** or **es**. It is the language people start with, and the language of the sign-in page. Everyone who never picked their own follows it. Changing it moves them all.

Each person picks their own in **Settings**, **Profile and sign-in**, **Language**. Children and teens can pick too. It makes no content, so it is not an age setting, and a parent does not see it as a limit.

## 🤖 Who wrote the Spanish

Your home's own AI wrote it on this computer. Nothing was sent anywhere else. Every line it wrote is marked `needs-review` in the catalog until a person has read it. `en-GB` holds only the words that differ from US English.

## 🔒 What stays in English until someone reviews it

Lines for children and teens, safety and crisis messages, refusals and consent screens, fixed voice lines, robot speech and the search-is-down line. The AI never writes these. A person writes and approves them. Until then they show in English, even for a person who picked Spanish. Telegram notices for children and teens are English too.

## ✏️ Fix a wrong line

Someone tells you a line reads wrong. The fix is a change to the Spanish catalog file `frontend/src/locales/es/messages.po`, then a release. The steps are in the developer page [The language stack](/docs/developers/reference/language-stack). Settings has no screen for editing a translation yet.

## 🙋 Something not working?

- A screen is in English after someone picked Spanish: the line has no approved Spanish yet. Nothing is broken.
- The sign-in page is in the wrong language: it uses **Language and region**, not a person's choice.
