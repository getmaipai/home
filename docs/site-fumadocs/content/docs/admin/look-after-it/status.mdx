---
title: Check that Home is working
description: Read the Status page to see which apps and parts of Home are working, and what a problem means.
---

## ✍️ Open it

Open your profile menu and choose **System status**. Anyone can open it.

## 🚦 Read the top

The banner says **All good** when everything runs. If something is wrong, it names what is affected. An owner or admin can post a note at the top, and everyone sees it.

## 📱 Read the apps

Each app shows **Working**, **Degraded**, **Waiting for internet** or **Not working**, with a bar for the last 90 days. Open an app to see what it needs, sorted into **Needs attention**, **Working** and **No recent use**.

**Degraded** means the app works with less than it should have. **Waiting for internet** means it needs the internet and cannot reach it yet.

## 🔧 Owners and admins see more

- **Engine computer** shows **Working**, **Connecting**, **Slow to answer**, **Reconnecting** or **Not reachable**. It appears when you use another computer for the AI.
- **Health checks** shows the last test. Press **Run checks now** to test again.
- **This Mac's self-check** shows on a Mac. It says whether the Mac can look up its own account, start a service and run the graphics check, and whether it is only watching or may restart the Mac.
- **Parts** lists the parts behind the apps as **Fine**, **Slow**, **Down** or **Maintenance**. Engine rows have **Stop** or **Start** and **Restart** buttons.
- **Recent problems** lists what went wrong lately.

## 🗓️ Plan maintenance

1. Press **Schedule maintenance**.
2. Pick the parts and set the times.
3. Press **Schedule**.

Everyone in the home sees the plan. Press **Cancel** on a plan to stop it.

## 🙋 Still need help?

If something is down, open [Repairs](/docs/admin/look-after-it/repairs) or see [Troubleshooting and support report](/docs/admin/look-after-it/troubleshooting-and-support-report).
