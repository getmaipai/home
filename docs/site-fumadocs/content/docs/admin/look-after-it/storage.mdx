## 📊 Look at the usage

Open **Settings**, then **Your data and apps**. On the **Data and privacy** tab, choose **Storage**. You can also open it from **Backups, storage and updates**.

Each person sees their own row: the space used, their limit and the kind of file that uses the most. A child sees only their own row.

Owners and admins also see a **Household total** bar, one row for every person, and a row for files shared by people who are no longer here. Those files stay, and the household keeps them.

Press **Trash** to bring back or delete for good what was deleted. See [Use the Library and Trash](/docs/user/media-and-more/library-and-trash).

## ✍️ Set the limits

1. Open **Settings**, then **Backups, storage and updates**.
2. On the **Storage and backups** tab, find the **Limits** card.
3. Set **Household storage cap**, the most all the files in the house may use. Setup fills it in from the disk. Zero means no cap.
4. Set **Default per-person storage cap**. New people start at 20 GB.

Both numbers are in bytes.

## 🧹 Clear saved online data

The **Saved online data** card lists copies of weather, news and other lookups Home fetched. Your chats, memories and news shelf stay.

1. Find the source and press **Clear** on its row, or press **Clear all**.
2. Confirm.

Home fetches the data again the next time someone asks.
