# Make a short film

A film is several shots joined with music. Home plans it, draws each shot, turns the shots into clips, adds music and puts it together. Adults can make films on their own screen, in a written chat. Spoken answers, shared screens, teens, children and guests cannot.

## Make one

1. Open a chat.
2. Type what you want, for example "make a 40 second film of a paper boat in the rain". You can say how long it should be, how many shots, the look, and who is in it.
3. Read the plan. The card shows a short summary and one line for each shot.
4. Press **Go**.
5. Watch the card. It moves through **Plan**, **Cast**, **Stills**, **Clips**, **Music** and **Assemble**.

The film then plays on the card. Home saves it, so you can find it again.

A film takes a while. It makes one picture or clip at a time, and it pauses while chat is busy. Press **Stop** on the card to cancel.

## Films with no faces

Ask for a "faceless film" or a film from a topic. Home writes a script and speaks it in a household voice. The pictures move slowly, with captions and music. No character clips are made.

## Change a film

Under a finished film:

- **Redo a shot** and the shot number makes that shot again. The rest stays.
- **Change the music** makes new music and puts it under the film.

If a film stops before the end, the card has a **Resume** button. It picks up at the step that stopped.

## Rules

Every picture, clip and the film itself pass the same safety check as other videos. If the check cannot run, the film is held back. See [Videos](videos.md#who-can-make-videos).
