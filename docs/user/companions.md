# Companions

A companion is how your assistant sounds. Some are plain and short. Some are cheerful and ask you questions.

Open **Companions** from All apps. Choose **Discover** to browse companions you can add. Choose **Add** on one you like to put it in your list, or **Remove** to take it out.

Tessa is the built-in companion for anyone who has not picked one. She is available to children, teens and adults.

## Pick one

Each card shows the companion's name, one line about it, and who it is for:

- A **companion for everyone** adapts to who it is talking to. It uses simple words and gentle topics for a child, and plain talk for an adult. Most companions are like this.
- A **teen companion** or an **adult companion** is made for that audience. It is limited to people and robots at that level or older.

You only see the companions that fit you. A child sees companions for everyone. A teen also sees teen companions. An adult sees all of them.

Tap a card to read more. You will see how it talks, things it might say, and its voice. Press **Play a sample** to hear the voice.
Press **Set as my default** to use it in your chats.

Open one of your chats and choose the companion in the chat header to use it for that chat. The choice stays with that chat. Type **@** followed by another companion's name to ask that companion one question. The chat's own companion stays the same.

On a companion's page, you can see your chats with it and start a new chat.

## Make your own

Teens and adults can make a companion. Children cannot. If you are a child, ask an owner or an admin.

1. Open **Companions** and choose **New companion**.
2. **Create Character.** Describe your companion, or choose **Upload Photo**. Pick a style, such as Cartoon or Anime, then choose **Generate Character**. If you do not like the picture, make another. Choose **Approve & Continue** when you do.
3. **Animate Character.** Home makes the moving versions of your picture, such as idle, talking and sad. The ones marked **Required** must be done before you go on. Choose **Generate Required** to make them. You can look at each one and redo any you do not like. Then choose **Continue**.
4. **Customize Companion.** Pick a voice and a personality. Write a short backstory, or choose **Generate** for a suggestion. You can edit it. Choose **Save Companion**.

Home saves your work as a draft while you go. If you close the window early, Home asks if you want to keep the draft or discard it.

## Change a companion

On a companion's card, open the menu and choose **Edit**. It opens on the same three screens, starting from the current picture. Choose **Make a copy** to start from a companion you like.

## Examples

Names and companions in this guide are examples. What you can pick depends on your age and what the owner has added.

## A companion never loosens the rules

What a person may see and hear is set by their own controls, or by the controls of the robot they talk to. A companion can change the style of an answer. It cannot make an answer less safe for a child.

## If Companions says it is turned off

An owner or admin can turn companions off for a person. Ask one of them to turn them back on.

## For owners and admins

- The gallery has a **Robots** list. It shows which companion each robot uses. Choose **Companion settings** to change a robot's companion and controls.
- A companion's page also lists the people and robots that use it.
- On a companion's page you can pick the voice that companion uses. Leave it on Home's default voice to follow the voice you chose for Home.
