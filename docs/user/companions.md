# Synths

A synth is an AI character with a name, a voice and a personality. Some are plain and short. Some are cheerful and ask you questions. The one you pick to talk to every day is **your companion**.

Open **Synths** from All apps. The **Yours** tab lists the synths you use. Choose **Discover** to browse synths you can add. Choose **Add** on one you like to put it in your list. To take one out, open the menu on its card and choose **Remove from Yours**. Use **Search synths** to find one by name.

Home comes with these synths: Tessa, MaiPai, Nova, Sprout, Riff, Pal, Rivet, The Tutor and Buddy. Tessa is the one for anyone who has not picked another. She is available to children, teens and adults.

## Pick one

Each card shows the synth's name, one line about it, and who it is for. A card says **No chats yet** until you talk to it, then it shows when you last chatted:

- A **synth for everyone** adapts to who it is talking to. It uses simple words and gentle topics for a child, and plain talk for an adult. Most synths are like this.
- A **teen synth** or an **adult synth** is made for that audience. It is limited to people and robots at that level or older.

You only see the synths that fit you. A child sees synths for everyone. A teen also sees teen synths. An adult sees all of them.

Choose **View page** on a card to open the synth's page. The **About** tab shows how it talks, things it might say, and its voice. Press **Play a sample** to hear the voice. The **Activity** and **Tasks** tabs show what it has been doing for you. Press **Chat with** the synth to start a chat.
To make it your companion for all your chats, open the menu on its card and choose **Make this my companion**. Your companion's card says **Your companion**.

Open one of your chats and choose the synth in the chat header to use it for that chat. The choice stays with that chat. Type **@** followed by another synth's name to ask that synth one question. The chat's own synth stays the same.


## Make your own

Teens and adults can make a synth. Children cannot. If you are a child, ask an owner or an admin.

1. Open **Synths** and choose **New synth**.
2. **Create Character.** Describe your synth, or choose **Upload Photo**. Pick a style, such as Cartoon or Anime, then choose **Generate Character**. If you do not like the picture, make another. Choose **Approve & Continue** when you do.
3. **Animate Character.** Home makes the moving versions of your picture, such as idle, talking and sad. The ones marked **Required** must be done before you go on. Choose **Generate Required** to make them. You can look at each one and redo any you do not like. Then choose **Continue**.
4. **Customize Synth.** Pick a voice and a personality. Write a short backstory, or choose **Generate** for a suggestion. You can edit it. Choose **Save Synth**.

Home saves your work as a draft while you go. If you close the window early, Home asks if you want to keep the draft or discard it.

## Change a synth

On a synth you made, open the menu on its card and choose **Edit**. It opens on the same three screens, starting from the current picture. A synth that comes with Home cannot be changed. Choose **Make a copy** (or **Customize a copy** on its page) and edit the copy. Your own synth can also be set **Back to draft** or **Mark ready** from the same menu, and **Delete** removes it for the whole home.

## Examples

Names and synths in this guide are examples. What you can pick depends on your age and what the owner has added.

## A synth never loosens the rules

What a person may see and hear is set by their own controls, or by the controls of the robot they talk to. A synth can change the style of an answer. It cannot make an answer less safe for a child.

## If Synths says it is turned off

An owner or admin can turn synths off for a person. Ask one of them to turn them back on.

## For owners and admins

- On a synth you made, the menu has **Audience**, where you choose who can use it. Before it is for everyone, you check each picture and the personality.
- Robots have a companion too. Open the robot under **Settings**, **Apps and devices**, **Devices** to change its companion and controls.
- A synth's page also lists the people and robots that use it.
- On a synth's page you can pick the voice that synth uses. Leave it on Home's default voice to follow the voice you chose for Home.
