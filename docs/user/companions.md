# Companions

A companion is how your assistant sounds. Some are plain and short. Some are cheerful and ask you questions.

Open **Companions** from All apps. You can pin it to the side bar there.

## Pick one

Each card shows the companion's name, one line about it, and who it is for:

- A **child companion** is for everyone, including kids.
- A **teen companion** is for teens and adults.
- An **adult companion** is for adults only.

You only see the companions that fit you. A child sees child companions. A teen sees child and teen companions. An adult sees all of them.

Tap a card to read more. You will see how it talks, things it might say, and its voice. Press **Play a sample** to hear the voice.
Press **Set as my default** to use it in your chats.

## If Companions says it is turned off

An owner or admin can turn companions off for a person. Ask one of them to turn them back on.

## For owners and admins

The gallery has a **Robots** list. It shows which companion each robot uses. Choose **Companion settings** to change a robot's
companion and controls. A companion's page also lists the people and robots that use it.
