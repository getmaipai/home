# Synths

A synth is an AI character with a name, a voice, and a way of speaking.

## Your companion

1. Open **Synths**.
2. Open a synth's menu.
3. Choose **Make this my companion**.

The card now says **Your companion**. Home uses this synth for new chats.

## Chat

1. Open a synth's page.
2. Choose **Chat** to start or reopen a chat with that synth.

## Credits

Open **Credits** on a synth's page to see shows, films, or music made with that synth.

## Life

Open **Life** on a synth's page to read its dated life notes. If you can edit the synth, add a note with its date. Choose **Forget** to remove a note.

## Based on

When you make a synth, choose **Nobody**, **A household member**, or **A real person**. Add a name when you choose a real person. Only an adult can make a synth based on a real person.

## Style pins

Open a synth you made and choose **Edit**. In **Style**, turn on a pin to keep a dial or example easy to find when you edit the synth again. An example may show where its words came from.
