# Keep copies on this device

You can keep a video, song or audiobook on a device so it plays when Home is out of reach.

1. A grown-up opens Settings, Devices, and turns on "Can keep copies on this device" for that device.
2. On the device, choose Keep on this device for the title.

Things to know:

- Chrome and Edge keep copies in the browser. On an iPhone or iPad, use the MaiPai Go app. The app says so if you try in Safari.
- If the device is full, Home says so. Remove some copies and try again.
- Files that need conversion are not ready to keep yet. Home says "not ready".
- If a device is removed, or its permission is turned off, it cannot get new copies.
- Signing out of a browser removes the copies in it. Signing in again after your session ran out keeps them. Settings, Devices has "Remove all offline copies".
- Kids and teens can keep copies only on a device a grown-up turned it on for. They cannot turn it on themselves.
- Robots, TVs and pods cannot keep copies.
- Copies belong to the person who is signed in. Someone else signing in on the same browser does not see them.
- Home checks your copies when the app opens and when you are back online, and removes any that were deleted or changed.
- A device you have not used for six months has to be paired again.


Settings, Devices lists the copies in this browser with their kind, such as Media. "Remove all offline copies" clears them
for every kind. It leaves the files saved on Home alone. You see only your own copies in this browser.

Media works with this feature today. News stories, reference pages and saved chats do not have device copies yet.
