---
title: Watch videos
description: Open the Videos app, play a video from your hub, pick up where you stopped, and save videos for later.
---

The **Videos** app plays the videos that are saved on your hub. It works like YouTube, in a dark look. Nothing here comes from the internet, so it plays with the internet off.

## Open it

Open **Videos** from the apps list or from your favorites. At the top you see four tabs: **Home**, **Library**, **Watch later** and **History**.

## Home

- A big picture at the top shows a few videos Home picked. Press **Play** to watch one, or **Watch later** to keep it for another day.
- **Continue watching** shows videos you started and did not finish. A thin bar under each picture shows how far you got.
- **Recently added** shows the newest videos. **More from your most watched** shows other videos from the place you watch most.
- **Watch again** shows videos you finished.
- **Everything in your library** lists all your videos.

A badge on a picture says **Saved** when the video is stored on your hub.

## Play a video

Press a picture. The video opens with its title. Press the big play button.

- Tap the left or right side twice to jump 15 seconds.
- Press and hold to play at double speed. Let go to go back to normal.
- The speed button picks a slower or faster speed. Home remembers it for next time (Settings, Voice).
- If the video has chapters, the chapter button lets you jump to one.

When you leave a video and come back, it starts where you stopped. For two seconds it says "Resumed from" and the time, with **Start over** beside it.

Under the video, **Up next** shows what plays after. When a video ends, the next one starts after a short countdown. You can stop the countdown.

## Library

**Library** shows every video by channel. A channel is the folder a video lives in. Use the **Channel** menu to look at one channel.

## Watch later and History

Press **Watch later** on the Home picture to save a video. Press it again to take the video off the list. **Watch later** shows your saved videos in the order you saved them. **History** shows what you watched, newest first. Only you see your own lists.

## If you are a child

You see the videos your family has cleared for children. You do not see the big picture or the lists of new videos, so nothing surprising shows up. **Everything in your library** and **Library** show all your videos. If you cannot find a video, ask a grown-up.

## Add videos

A grown-up with an admin account adds a folder of videos in **Settings, Home, Storage**. Home looks in the folder on a schedule and the videos show up in the app.
