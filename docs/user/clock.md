# Use the Clock app

The Clock app has four tabs: **Alarms**, **Timers**, **World clocks** and **Stopwatch**. Open **Clock** from the apps list. You can also ask MaiPai in chat. See [Lists, reminders and alarms](lists-reminders-alarms.md).

## Set an alarm

1. Open **Clock**, then the **Alarms** tab.
2. Press the plus button.
3. Under **Time**, choose when the alarm rings.
4. Under **Repeat**, tap the days it should ring. Leave them empty for one time.
5. Add a **Label**, pick a **Sound** and set a **Snooze** time if you like.
6. Press **Save**.

You will know it worked when the alarm shows in the list. Tap an alarm to change it. Choose **Skip next** to skip only the next ring. Choose **Delete alarm** to remove it, then confirm.

An alarm set by an adult or a teen rings even in quiet hours. A child's alarm waits until quiet hours end.

## Start a timer

1. Open the **Timers** tab.
2. Press the plus button under **Quick timers**.
3. Type a **Name** and the **Minutes**, then press **Save**.
4. Press **Start** on the timer.

You can **Rename** or **Delete** a quick timer from its **More** menu. Press **Reset** to start over. Timers you start in chat show under **Other running timers**.

## See the time in other cities

1. Open the **World clocks** tab.
2. Press **Add a place**.
3. Type a city in **Search cities** and pick it.

Each place shows its time and whether it is day or night there. Press **Remove** to take a place off. The list works without the internet, because Home looks cities up in data it keeps on this computer.

## Use the stopwatch

Open the **Stopwatch** tab and press **Start**. Press **Lap** to mark a lap. Press **Stop** to pause, then **Resume** or **Reset**.

## Alarm settings

Your alarm choices are also in **Settings**, then **Clock**.
