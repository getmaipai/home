# Templates

A template is a video idea you can start again: the words, the look, the camera and the length. Open **Video**, then **Templates**. Templates are for adults.

## Start from one

Press a picture under **Start with a template**. **Create** opens with the words and choices filled in. Nothing starts until you press **Generate**, so you can change the words first.

## Save your own

On any finished clip in **Create**, open the **...** menu and press **Save as a template**. The template is named after the first words of the clip. It is private, so only you see it.

Names of people in your cast become blanks in the words. That way the template never carries a name you did not choose to share.

## Your templates

Your saved templates show under **Your templates**. Press the **...** on a tile for:

- **Share with home.** Everyone at home who can use templates sees it, under **From your household**. A child's profile cannot share. A locked template cannot be shared until you unlock it.
- **Rename.** Type a new name and press **Rename**.
- **Delete.** Asks you to confirm. Videos you made from it stay. Only the template goes.

A shared template moves to **Shared with home**. There the menu says **Stop sharing**, and it goes back to being private.

## Who sees what

A template follows the same rules as the video it came from. A template made from an adults-only clip is adults only. Guests never see shared templates.
