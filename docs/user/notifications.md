MaiPai can let you know about things worth your attention, right in the hub or through Telegram.

## See your notifications

Tap the bell icon in the header to see recent notifications without leaving the page you're on. Open **Notifications** in the sidebar for the full history from the last 30 days.

- Tap a notification to open what it's about.
- Tap **Clear all** to remove everything from the list.

## Get notified on Telegram

To also get notifications on your phone through Telegram:

1. Open **Settings**, then **Notifications** (under Household).
2. An owner or admin sets up the household's Telegram bot token once.
3. Each person then links their own Telegram chat from their own notification settings.
