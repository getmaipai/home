MaiPai can let you know about things worth your attention. You'll see these right in the hub, or through Telegram.

## See your notifications

Tap the bell icon in the header. This shows your recent notifications. You stay on the page you're on. Open **Notifications** in the sidebar for the full history. It goes back 30 days.

- Tap a notification to open what it's about.
- Tap **Clear all** to remove everything from the list.

## Get notified on Telegram

Want notifications on your phone too? Set up Telegram:

1. Open **Settings**, then **Notifications** (under Household).
2. An owner or admin sets this up once for the household.
3. After that, each person links their own Telegram chat. Do this from your own notification settings.
