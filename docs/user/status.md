# Check that Home is working

Open your profile menu and choose **System status**. Anyone can open it.

## Read the top

The banner says **All good** when everything runs. If something is wrong, it names what is affected. An owner or admin can post a note at the top, and everyone sees it.

## Read the apps

Each app shows **Working**, **Degraded**, **Waiting for internet** or **Not working**, with a bar for the last 90 days. Open an app to see what it needs. Needs are sorted into **Needs attention**, **Working** and **No recent use**.

**Degraded** means the app works, with less than it should have. **Waiting for internet** means it needs the internet and cannot reach it yet.

## Owners and admins see more

- **Engine computer** shows **Working**, **Connecting**, **Slow to answer**, **Reconnecting** or **Not reachable**. It appears when you use another computer for the AI.
- **Health checks** shows the last test. Press **Run checks now** to test again.
- **Parts** lists the parts behind the apps. A row is **Fine**, **Slow**, **Down** or **Maintenance**. Engine rows have **Stop** or **Start** and **Restart** buttons.
- **Recent problems** lists what went wrong lately.

## Plan maintenance (owner or admin)

Press **Schedule maintenance**, pick the parts, set the times and press **Schedule**. Everyone in the home sees the plan. Press **Cancel** on a plan to stop it.

If something is down, see [Fix a problem](fix-a-problem.md).
