# Listen to the radio

**Radio** is in the Music app. It plays live stations from the internet. You save the ones you like to **Your stations**.

## Play a station

1. Open **Music**, then **Radio**.
2. Pick a station under **Your stations**, or one from a row such as **Popular now**, **Rock**, **Jazz**, **Chill**, **Classical**, **News**, **Hip hop** or **Around the world**.
3. Press **Play**.

The station plays in the small player at the bottom. It says it is live. You cannot seek ahead in a live station. Pause and play joins the station live again. The station's own song title shows when the station sends one.

You can also pick **Radio from your music** on **Listen now**. That plays songs from your own library and uses no internet.

## Find and save a station

Type in **Search stations**. Press **Add** beside a station to save it to **Your stations**. To remove one, press **Remove** under **Manage stations**, or on the station's page.

Search and the rows of stations need your home to be allowed to look for stations online. See the switch below. If it is off, **Radio** shows only the stations a parent saved.

## Record a station

Teens and adults can record a station.

1. Open a station under **Your stations**.
2. Press **Record**. It records for 15 minutes.
3. To stop early, press **Stop recording**.

The recording appears under **Recordings** on the station's page. You can play it and seek in it. Each person can run one recording at a time, and a home can run four at once. A recording needs room in your storage.

## Kids and teens

Children play only the stations a parent saved for them. A parent can save a station for a child. Children cannot search, add or record. Teens can search and record, but they never see a station marked for adults.

## Choose whether your home can look for stations online

An owner or admin decides this.

1. Open **Settings**, then **Pictures, videos and music**.
2. Open the **Music** tab.
3. Find **Online radio and podcasts**.
4. Turn **Find radio stations online** on or off.

When it is on, people can search a public list of stations and play stations from the internet. The station sees your home's address. When it is off, nothing is looked up and Radio keeps to the saved stations.
