---
title: Let Home close apps for a big job
description: Pick the apps Home may close when a big job needs room, and the apps it must never close.
---

When a big job needs more memory, Home first tries to unload its own models. If that is not enough, it can close other apps on this computer. This is off until you turn it on.

## What you need to get started

- You are an owner or an admin.
- Home runs on a Mac used as a regular computer. The setup wizard tests this by closing and reopening Calculator.

## Turn it on

1. Open **Settings**, then **AI and models**, then **Performance**.
2. In **Close apps for big jobs**, look at **Always close**. Press **Use the starter list**, or **Add an app** one at a time.
3. Look at **Never close**. Add any app that must always stay open.
4. Choose **Automatic**.

You'll know it worked when the card shows **Automatic** and your lists.

## What Home does

- It closes only apps on **Always close**, and only when a big job needs room and nothing else is left to try.
- It asks each app to quit politely. It never forces one.
- It never closes an app on **Never close**, an app in front of you, an app you used in the last two minutes, or a system app.
- When the job ends, it reopens the apps it closed. Each app has a **Reopen when done** switch.

Home keeps only counts of closed apps, never their names, after the job.

## If an app did not close

- If you see **This app is also on the Never close list**, it stays open. Remove it from one list.
- An app with an unsaved document may refuse to quit. Home leaves it and tries the next.
