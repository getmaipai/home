---
title: What MaiPai remembers
description: See, export, or forget what MaiPai has remembered from your conversations.
---

As you talk with MaiPai, it remembers useful details. Things like your favorite foods, or your kid's allergy. This way, you don't have to repeat yourself.

Everything it remembers stays on your hub. It never goes anywhere else.

## What the assistant keeps in mind

A few standing facts about you are always in mind: who you are, and how you want your answers. For example, "Sage is a nurse" or "Sage wants short answers". Up to eight of these are shown to the assistant on every message, each with the date it learned the fact.

Everything else is looked up only when your message is about it. A note about your kid's school, a trip, or your week stays out of the way until you ask about it. The assistant uses a fact only when it changes the answer. It never brings something up just because it knows it.

A note you ask it to keep is always saved. Say "make a note that..." or "remember that..." and it goes in, no matter what. Each saved fact keeps the date it was learned, and a fact about one day (a visit, a game) drops out of the lookup a couple of days after that day.

## See what's remembered

Open **Settings**, then **Your data and apps**. The **Data and privacy** tab lists what MaiPai remembered from your conversations. Each entry is one thing MaiPai learned. A memory a robot keeps about you is listed under the robot's name.

## Forget one memory

Find the memory you want gone. Tap the small archive icon next to it. MaiPai won't use it again.

## Forget several at once

Tap **Select memories** at the top of the list. Check the ones you want gone, then tap **Forget selected**. Confirm with **Yes, forget**, and their words are wiped out. Only a blank marker stays, so a robot or a second hub that was offline knows to forget it too. This cannot be undone.

## Clear everything

Tap **Clear all**. Confirm with **Yes, forget all**, and every one of your memories is wiped out. Only blank markers stay. This cannot be undone.

## Check a child's memories

Owners and admins can see a child's memories. Open **Settings**, then **Household and people**, then the **People** tab, and choose **Users**. Under **Memories for** the child's name you can:

- Press **Export** with their name, like **Export Nova's memories**, to save a file of them.
- Press **Forget everything about** with their name, then confirm with **Yes, forget everything**.
- Tap the archive icon on one memory to forget only that one.

This helps you manage what MaiPai keeps about a child. A teen's memories are not open to an owner or admin.

## Who may hear a household memory

A household memory can be limited to some of the house. An adult can set **Who may hear this** on a memory to Everyone, Teens and adults, or Adults only. The choice, and who made it, are recorded on the memory itself. Children and teens see no control and cannot change it; the memory is not shown to them when it's set above their level.

## Passwords and keys

MaiPai does not keep passwords, PINs, API keys or tokens in memory. If you say one in chat, it answers "Keep passwords and keys in Credentials, not in chat." and stores nothing. It also blanks the password out of the chat history, so it is never saved anywhere.

Saying where a password lives is fine. "The wifi password is on the fridge" gets remembered like any other fact.

One limit to know about: MaiPai spots passwords and keys by their shape, or by words like "password" or "token" right before them. A secret pasted on its own, with no label and no known shape, can look like any other word to it. Real credentials belong in **Settings**, in the fields marked as secret (an account password for an integration, an API key). Those are stored encrypted and never shown back.
