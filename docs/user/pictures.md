Home can make pictures on your own computer. Nothing is sent to anyone.

## Ask in chat

Type "make me a picture of a red fox in the snow" or "draw a lighthouse at dusk". Home answers in a few words and shows a card that says how far along the picture is and how many seconds it has taken. Press **Stop** on the card to cancel.

You can ask for up to 4 pictures at once ("give me three options") and for a shape: square (the default), portrait, landscape, wide or tall ("a tall poster"). Say "another one", "one more" or "the same again" to make a new picture. If you leave the page, the picture keeps going.

When it is done, the picture appears in the chat. Tap it to see it bigger. A Standard picture takes a few seconds on a fast computer and longer on a slower one.

If someone is chatting or talking to Home while a picture is being made, the card says "Paused while chat is working". It carries on after the reply. The wait is not counted against the picture.

## Standard or Boost

Home makes pictures at two levels. **Standard** is the usual one: a few seconds, clean and sharp, and good for words in a picture. **Boost** takes about 15 to 30 seconds and has finer detail, and skin and fur that look real. Boost needs a bigger download and a computer with plenty of memory. See [Levels](levels.md).

- **In chat,** just ask. Say "as detailed as you can" or "photoreal" and chat makes a Boost picture and tells you so in one line.
- **On a picture.** Press **Make it Boost** under a Standard picture, or **Make it Standard** under a Boost one, to make it again the other way. A picture made with a style has no button, because the style decides.
- **For everyone.** An owner or admin can choose the level chat uses when nobody asks (below). It starts as Standard.
- **Edit** always uses Standard.

## Change a picture you have

Ask in chat, in your own words. Attach a picture, or point at one already in the chat, and say what you want:

- "Make the sky clear." "Make the car yellow." "Change the background to a beach." "Fix the lighting, it is too dark." The picture model makes the change. It can redraw small parts of the rest of the picture, so check the result.
- "Add her to this picture." Attach the scene and the other picture (two pictures on one message) and describe the change.
- "Remove the background" and "make it bigger" also work from chat.

The original is never changed. The new picture is the next version of it: press **Edit** on it to see both and go back (**Undo**). Ask for the change you want, including changes to people or their likeness. The model decides whether to fulfill the request; Home blocks generated output only when its CSAM check finds a candidate.

## Change a picture in the editor

- **Edit.** For a picture Home made, press **Edit** below it. For a picture you sent, tap it to show **Edit** on the picture. On a computer, move the pointer over a sent picture to show **Edit**. In the editor, choose **Comment**, tap the part you want to change (or drag a box), and say what you want, like "make the sky pink". Home shows the new picture as a preview. Press **Apply** to keep it as a new version, or discard it. Only the part you pointed at changes.
- **The editor tools.** Press **Edit** to open a picture in the editor: **Remove background** (keeps the subject), **Erase** (paint over something and Home fills the hole), **Upscale** (2x or 4x, bigger and sharper), **Resize**, **Markup** (draw or write on it) and **Comment** (above). The first four run on your computer in a few seconds and need no picture model. A change you apply becomes a new version, and the original stays.
- **Make a variation.** Open the **More** menu under the reply and choose **Make a variation**. You get another picture from the same words.
- **Try again.** If a picture did not work, the card says why and has a **Try again** button.

## Who can make pictures

Adults can, on their own screen, in a written chat. Teens can too, with nothing adult or sexual. They only get the everyday styles. A teen's own photo can be changed in ordinary ways, never into anything sexual. The photo has to look like it could be of a teen, and a parent can see these edits. A teen is never shown a picture that may be adult. Children can make cartoon pictures of safe things, up to 20 a day. Changing one of their own cartoons counts as one. Cartoon pictures are on for every child unless you turn them off. The look is always a cartoon, nothing scary or grown-up is made, and photos and real people are never used. A parent or admin can turn this off for the home or for one child. Spoken answers, the TV and other shared screens never make pictures. A guest cannot.

See [cartoon controls](people.md#cartoon-pictures-and-videos) for the home switch, daily limits and each child's switch.

Home blocks CSAM. The model decides whether to make other adult content, including pictures of real people. Child and teen content filters remain in place. A CSAM candidate is withheld if the check cannot run.

## Choose how it works (owner and admin)

Open **Settings**, **Pictures, videos and music**.

- **Adults can make pictures.** Turn it off to stop all picture making.
- **Adult-only styles.** Choose whether styles marked adults only can be used.
- **Level when nobody says.** Choose **Standard** or **Boost** for the pictures chat makes when nobody names a level. Anyone can pick a level for one picture.
- **Picture models.** Press **Choose models** to open **AI and models**, where you choose which models make Standard and Boost pictures. Installing a model happens there and in **Models and storage**, not on this page.
- **Children and teens.** Choose whether children can make cartoons, and how many a day. See [cartoon controls](people.md#cartoon-pictures-and-videos).

- **Styles.** A style gives pictures and videos a look, like old film or clay. Styles have their own tab, **Styles**. Only admins add them. A new style is for admins only until you choose **All adults** or **Chosen people** for it. Adult-only styles stay behind one **Locked items** row until you enter your own passcode. They lock again when you leave the tab.

### Add a style

1. Open **Settings**, **Pictures, videos and music**, **Styles**.
2. Press **Browse styles** to search Hugging Face, or **Add from a file** to use a file from this computer. Civitai is off until you turn it on under **Where styles come from**, and it asks you to read a short notice first.
3. Open a style to read what it is, its licence and its size. Say if it is for adults only. If a source marks it adult, it stays locked until you unlock the tab.
4. Press **Add**. The progress shows on the strip at the top of the page.
5. Choose who can use it: **Only admins**, **All adults** or **Chosen people**. Children and teens never see styles.

Remove a style and it waits 30 days under **Recently removed**. **Restore** brings it back. **Delete now** frees the space at once.

The picture engine checks that the file is supported and safe to process. Content decisions for adults belong to the model; child protections remain in place.

## Something not working?

| What you see | Why | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Home says it cannot make pictures for you | You are a guest, or you are on a shared screen or a spoken chat, or a parent turned cartoon pictures off for you | Ask an adult, or use your own written chat |
| "No picture model is installed" | The model is not on this computer | An owner or admin chooses and installs a picture model in Settings, AI and models |
| "The picture engine isn't answering", or Home says it cannot reach the engine | The AI engine is restarting or stopped | Wait a minute and ask again. If it stays, open **Repairs** |
| "Waiting for memory to free up" | A video or another big job holds the memory | Wait. It starts by itself |
| "Something went wrong while making it" | The job failed | Press **Try again**, or change the words |
| The reply says it is coming but no picture shows yet | The picture started and the reply did not finish | Wait a minute. The card fills in |
| You asked chat to change a picture and got a new one | The picture tool cannot change an existing picture | Press **Edit** on the picture instead |
| A style file is refused | Over 120 MB in the form, or it was made for another picture model (Pony, Illustrious and SDXL styles do not fit yet) | For a big file, type its path on this computer instead. Otherwise use a different style |
| You cannot find **Edit** | The picture may not be in this chat | Open the picture in this chat and choose **Edit**. If it is in **Files**, open it there |

## Private chats

In an Incognito or temporary chat, the picture lives only in memory and is gone when the chat ends.
