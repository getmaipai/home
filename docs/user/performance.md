---
title: Share the computer during big jobs
description: Choose whether chat or big jobs get the room, free up memory, and see what Home is doing.
---

A big picture, video or song job needs a lot of the computer's memory. This page decides how long Home waits, and what it may lower, when chat and a big job both want the room. Only owners and admins see it.

Only a Max job can make chat simpler, and only when it needs more room than is free. Standard and Boost jobs run beside chat and leave it alone. See [Standard, Boost and Max](levels.md).

## What you need to get started

You are an owner or an admin. Your Home needs the memory to run bigger jobs at all; on a smaller computer some levels are not offered.

## Pick a preset

1. Open **Settings**, then **AI and models**, then **Performance**.
2. Under **Big-job behaviour**, choose one:
   - **Chat first.** Home waits for 60 seconds with no chat before it makes chat simpler. It asks before it lowers a big job.
   - **Balanced.** Home waits for 20 seconds with no chat. It lowers a picture job without asking, and asks before it lowers video or music.
   - **Big jobs first.** Home waits for 20 seconds with no chat. It lowers any big job without asking, so the biggest job that fits runs.

In all three, chat keeps answering.

If you change a detail by hand, the page shows **Custom**.

You'll know it worked when your choice is marked.

## What the presets change

Home waits until nobody has chatted for a while before it makes chat simpler. The least wait is 20 seconds, because swapping the chat model takes 20 to 60 seconds. If people keep chatting, Home waits up to 10 minutes. Then it goes ahead between two chat messages. After chat goes back to normal, Home waits 5 minutes before it simplifies chat again, so chat does not flip back and forth. Under **Customize** you can set these times, put pictures, video and music in order, and say whether Home asks the person first or lowers a level on its own.

## Free up memory

Press **Free up memory** to unload models Home is not using. Chat and anything running are left alone. If nothing is idle, the button says so.

## See what Home is doing

On **AI and models**, a status line shows what each kind of job is doing during a big job, for example chat simpler and music waiting. While a Max job runs, an admin can press **Stop the Max job** on that page. Chat then goes back to normal.

## If chat got simpler

A line under the chat box says chat is simpler and about how many minutes are left. Chat keeps answering, with shorter answers. It goes back by itself when the Max job ends, or when an admin presses **Stop the Max job**. If chat is in the middle of an answer, the swap back waits until that answer is done.
