---
title: Share the computer during big jobs
description: Choose whether chat or big jobs get the room, free up memory, and see what Home is doing.
---

A big picture, video or song job needs a lot of the computer's memory. This page decides who gets the room when chat and a big job both want it. Only owners and admins see it.

## What you need to get started

You are an owner or an admin. Your Home needs the memory to run bigger jobs at all; on a smaller computer some levels are not offered.

## Pick a preset

1. Open **Settings**, then **AI and models**, then **Performance**.
2. Under **Big-job behaviour**, choose one:
   - **Chat first.** Chat always answers, and big jobs wait for a quiet moment.
   - **Balanced.** Big jobs may make chat a little simpler while they run.
   - **Big jobs first.** Big jobs get the best quality, and chat gets simpler while they run.

If you change a detail by hand, the page shows **Custom**.

You'll know it worked when your choice is marked.

## What the presets change

Home waits until nobody has chatted for a while before it makes chat simpler. The least wait is 20 seconds. It also waits a few minutes before simplifying chat again, so chat does not flip back and forth. Under **Customize** you can set these times, put pictures, video and music in order, and say whether Home asks the person first or lowers a level on its own.

## Free up memory

Press **Free up memory** to unload models Home is not using. Chat and anything running are left alone. If nothing is idle, the button says so.

## See what Home is doing

On **AI and models**, a status line shows what each kind of job is doing during a big job, for example chat simpler and music waiting. While a Max job runs, an admin can press **Stop the Max job** on that page. Chat then goes back to normal.

## If chat got simpler

A line under the chat box says chat is simpler for a few minutes. It goes back by itself when the job ends.
