# Make a backup and bring one back

A backup is a saved copy of everyone in your household, what MaiPai remembers, and every conversation. Home also makes one by itself each day and keeps the newest ones.

Owners and admins can see and make backups. Only the owner can bring one back.

## Make a backup now

1. Open **Settings**, then **Backups, storage and updates**.
2. On the **Storage and backups** tab, choose **Backups**.
3. Press **Back up now**.

The new backup shows in the list with its date and size.

## Save your backup key

If the computer breaks, the backup key brings your Home back on a new one. The owner sees **Save your backup key** at the top of the Backups page.

1. Press **Download** to save the file, or **Print** to print it.
2. Keep it somewhere other than this computer.

The page then says when you saved it.

## Bring a backup back

Restoring puts everyone, every memory and every conversation back to how they were on that day. Anything added since is gone.

1. Open **Backups**.
2. Find the backup by its date. Press **Restore**.
3. Read the warning. Press **Yes, restore this backup**, or **Keep things as they are** to stop.
4. Restart MaiPai Home to finish.

Nothing changes until Home starts again. Until then the page says **Ready to restore**. Press **Cancel restore** to leave things as they are.

If a backup came from a newer version of MaiPai Home, Home tells you and does not use it.
