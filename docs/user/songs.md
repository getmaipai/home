title: Songs
description: Ask chat to write and make a song, change it by asking, play it, and choose who can make songs.

Home can write and sing a song on your own computer. Nothing is sent to anyone.

## What you need

A Mac with 64 GB of memory or more, and the music model. An owner or admin gets the model once in **Settings**, **Models and storage**, under **Music** (about 11 GB). On a smaller computer, Home says songs are not available and why.

## Quick and full songs

A song is as long as its words. There are two kinds:

- **Full** sings every part of the song. This is what chat makes unless you ask for quick.
- **Quick** sings the hook: the first parts through the first chorus.

You can ask for a length, such as "make it 60 seconds". If the words are too long, Home sings the first whole parts that fit. The card shows those words and keeps the rest for **Make it full**.

A 64 GB Mac's longest song is 2 minutes. A 128 GB Mac's longest song is 4 minutes. If the words need more time than that, Home sings the first parts that fit and tells you why.

With no words, quick songs are about 45 seconds and full songs are about 3 minutes, up to what your computer can make.

Press **Make it full** or **Make it quick** on the card to make the same song again with every part or just the hook. An owner or admin can change the default in **Settings**.

## Try it in five steps

1. An owner or admin opens **Settings**, **Models and storage**, finds **Music** and presses **Install**. Wait until it says installed.
2. Open a new chat and type "write us a quick song about our dog chasing bubbles".
3. When the card shows the player, press play. Press **Lyrics** to read the words.
4. Type "change the chorus so it is about a red ball". A new version plays. Use the arrows to hear the first one again.
5. Press **Make it full** on the card. The new version sings every part that fits on your computer.

## Ask in chat

Type "write us a cheerful folk song about Saturday pancakes". Chat writes the words and starts the song. A card shows what Home is doing, a clock and a bar. Press **Stop** on the card to cancel.

The first song after a restart takes about a minute more, while the model loads. Picture, video and song jobs share a household queue. Your card shows its place in line, and your song starts when its turn comes.

If someone is chatting or talking to Home while a song is being made, the card says "Paused while chat is working". It carries on after the reply. The wait is not counted against the song.

When it is done, the song plays on the card. **Lyrics** shows the words. The line being sung is lit. Tap a line to jump there. Press **Download** to save it or **Share** to send it.

Press **Edit** to make a quick song full, change the song with a short request, or describe new cover art. Home keeps earlier song versions in the version buttons. Only the person who made the song can change it. A new cover may take a little time to appear.

Tips for a good song:

- Say the style, the mood and the instruments, for example "soft piano and a warm voice".
- Let the length follow the words, or ask for a length you need.
- Say "no singing" for music only.

## Change a song by asking

Ask in the same chat. For example:

- "Make it slower" or "make it in a different key".
- "Change the chorus so it is about waffles."
- "Make it instrumental" or "do a jazz version".
- "Make it 20 seconds longer."
- "Cut the first 5 seconds", "fade it out" or "make it louder".
- "Give me another take."

Each change is a new version. Nothing is lost. The card shows "Version 2 of 2" with arrows to go back. To build on an older version, go back to it and press **Use this version**.

## Split a song into stems

Press **Edit** on a song you made here, open **Stems**, then press **Split into stems**. Home makes four tracks: vocals, drums, bass and other. They show on one card, and you step through them as Track 1 of 4 and so on. Each track is its own song in your Library.

You can split only songs made in Home. Wait for the song to finish first.

## Who can make songs

Adults can, on their own screen, in a written chat. Children and teens cannot make songs. Spoken answers, the TV and other shared screens never make songs. A guest cannot.

Home blocks CSAM. The model decides whether to make other adult content, including music that imitates a real person's voice. Child and teen content filters remain in place. A CSAM candidate is withheld if the check cannot run.

## Using a song for money

Songs are made with a model whose makers say its songs may be used for money, for example in a video you publish. Each song keeps a note of how it was made. If a later model does not allow that, Home will not let you export its songs for money, but you can still download them for yourself.

## Choose how it works (owner and admin)

- **Settings**, **Pictures, videos and music**: **Adults can make music**. Turn it off to stop all song making. **Song length** picks full (the default) or quick songs when nobody says which.
- **Settings**, **Models and storage**, **Music**: get the music model, or remove it to free space.

## Something not working?

- **No song option.** Why: your Mac has less than 64 GB, the music model is not installed, you are not an adult, or music is off. Fix: ask an owner or admin to check Settings.
- **Your song is waiting.** Why: picture, video and song jobs share a household queue. Fix: wait. Its card shows its place in line and it starts when its turn comes.
- **"Not enough free memory for music right now".** Why: another big job has the memory. Fix: wait a few minutes, then ask again.
- **The end of a song has no real words.** Why: the words were shorter than the song. Fix: ask for more words, or a shorter song.
- **My full song leaves out some words.** Why: the words need more time than the length you asked for or your computer can make. Fix: ask for fewer words, or use a computer that can make a longer song.
- **"I can only change a song that was made here".** Why: you asked to change a song Home did not make. Fix: ask for a new song.
- **Home cannot reach the engine.** Why: the AI engine is restarting. Fix: wait a minute and ask again. Still stuck? Open **Repairs**.

## Private chats

In an Incognito or temporary chat, the song lives only in memory and is gone when the chat ends.
