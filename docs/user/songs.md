---
title: Songs
description: Ask chat to write and make a song, change it by asking, play it, and choose who can make songs.
---

Home can write and sing a song on your own computer. Nothing is sent to anyone.

## What you need

A Mac with 64 GB of memory or more, and the music model. An owner or admin gets the model once in **Settings**, **Models and storage**, under **Music** (about 11 GB). On a smaller computer, Home says songs are not available and why.

## Ask in chat

Type "write us a cheerful folk song about Saturday pancakes, about a minute long". Chat writes the words and starts the song. A card shows what Home is doing, a clock and a bar. Press **Stop** on the card to cancel.

A one minute song takes about 15 to 30 seconds. The first song after a restart takes about a minute more, while the model loads. If Home is busy making a picture, a video or another song, the card says so and your song starts after it. You can have one song in the making at a time.

When it is done, the song plays on the card. Press **Lyrics** to see the words. Press **Download** to save it or **Share** to send it.

Tips for a good song:

- Say the style, the mood and the instruments, for example "soft piano and a warm voice".
- Ask for words that fill the length. If the words are too short, the singer fills the rest with sounds that are not words.
- Say "no singing" for music only.

## Change a song by asking

Ask in the same chat. For example:

- "Make it slower" or "make it in a different key".
- "Change the chorus so it is about waffles."
- "Make it instrumental" or "do a jazz version".
- "Make it 20 seconds longer."
- "Cut the first 5 seconds", "fade it out" or "make it louder".
- "Give me another take."

Each change is a new version. Nothing is lost. The card shows "Version 2 of 2" with arrows to go back. To build on an older version, go back to it and press **Use this version**.

## Who can make songs

Adults can, on their own screen, in a written chat. Children and teens cannot make songs. Spoken answers, the TV and other shared screens never make songs. A guest cannot.

Some things are never made, whatever the settings:

- Words that are sexual and about children.
- A song that copies a real person's voice. Home can make a song in a similar style with its own voice.
- A song from a recording you upload. Home only changes songs it made.

If you ask for one of these, Home says it will not make it and what to change.

## Using a song for money

Songs are made with a model whose makers say its songs may be used for money, for example in a video you publish. Each song keeps a note of how it was made. If a later model does not allow that, Home will not let you export its songs for money, but you can still download them for yourself.

## Choose how it works (owner and admin)

- **Settings**, **Home settings**, **Pictures, videos and music**: **Adults can make music**. Turn it off to stop all song making.
- **Settings**, **Models and storage**, **Music**: get the music model, or remove it to free space.

## Something not working?

- **No song option.** Why: your Mac has less than 64 GB, the music model is not installed, you are not an adult, or music is off. Fix: ask an owner or admin to check Settings.
- **"Home is busy making another picture, video or song".** Why: Home does one job at a time. Fix: wait. It starts by itself.
- **"Not enough free memory for music right now".** Why: another big job has the memory. Fix: wait a few minutes, then ask again.
- **The end of a song has no real words.** Why: the words were shorter than the song. Fix: ask for more words, or a shorter song.
- **"I can only change a song that was made here".** Why: you asked to change a song Home did not make. Fix: ask for a new song.
- **Home cannot reach the engine.** Why: the AI engine is restarting. Fix: wait a minute and ask again. Still stuck? Open **Repairs**.

## Private chats

In an Incognito or temporary chat, the song lives only in memory and is gone when the chat ends.
