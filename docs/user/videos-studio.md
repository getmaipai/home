# Edit a video in the Studio

The **Studio** is a page for adults who want to cut and mix a video themselves. Open **Video**, then **Studio**. You can also press **Open in Studio** on a clip in **Create**.

1. **Pick a clip.** Choose one of your own videos under **Pick a clip to open**. It shows in the preview and on the **Video** track.
2. **Cut it.** Drag the handles at each end of a piece to trim it. Press **Split at playhead** to cut a piece in two, and **Delete piece** to take one out.
3. **Add more.** **Add a clip** puts another of your videos after this one. **Add a voice** lets you type a line for Home to say. **Add music** puts one of your songs under the video.
4. **Set the sound.** Each track has a level. Pick a piece to turn it up or down, or fade it in or out. Home can lower a song while someone speaks. Pick a clip that has another clip after it to blend them (a crossfade).
5. **Press Export as a new clip.** Home makes a new video and keeps the old one. A card shows it being made. When it is ready, press **Open in My clips**, or **Keep editing** to change it more.

You end with a new clip in **My clips**. The first clip is not changed.

## Limits

A film in the Studio can have 6 clips, 2 voice lines, 2 songs, and be up to 60 seconds long.

What you hear in the Studio is a preview. It cuts where the export blends, and the final loudness is set when you export. Recorded voice lines sound more natural and may take a little longer. When the computer is busy, Home uses the standard voice.

Export needs the video editor on the AI engine. When it is not ready, the button is off and a line says why.

## Who can see the new clip

The new video is never open to more people than the videos it came from. If any video you use is adults only, the new one is adults only too.

If a video is locked, the Studio shows a lock instead. Unlock it with your passcode and it opens. When it locks again, edits you did not export are gone. See [Adults-only videos and the lock](videos-locked.md).

## Other ways to change a video

You can also ask chat to trim, join, mix or continue a video. See [Make a video](videos.md#change-a-video-in-chat).
