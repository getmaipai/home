## Sign in with a PIN

1. Pick your name on the sign-in screen.
2. Type your PIN. The screen shows one box for each digit of your PIN, and each digit is hidden as you type. If your PIN has 6 digits, you see 6 boxes.
3. Home signs you in as soon as the last box is full. You do not press anything.

If your PIN is a password with letters or symbols, you see one password field and a **Sign in** button instead.

## If you type the wrong code

The boxes shake, turn red and clear, so you can try again. Home says what to do next in plain words. If the network drops while you sign in, the boxes keep what you typed. Try again when the hub is back.

After 5 wrong tries in a row, the account locks for a short time. The wait grows each time it happens again: 30 seconds, then 2 minutes, then 10 minutes, then 1 hour. A countdown shows on screen and the boxes stay off until it ends. A right code clears the count. The lock protects your account if someone guesses your PIN. It also applies to passkeys and passwords, because they share one count.

## The lock screen

If Home locks while you are away from the screen, the lock screen asks for the same PIN in the same boxes. It also has a password option for people whose sign-in is a password.

## Change your PIN or password

Open **Settings**, choose the **Me** tab, then **PIN / password**. A PIN can be 4 to 128 characters. A PIN of only digits shows as boxes at sign-in. If you change from a 4-digit PIN to a 6-digit one, the sign-in screen learns the new length by itself.

## If you forgot it

Ask an owner or admin to reset your PIN under **Settings**, **Users**. See [Fix a problem](fix-a-problem.md).
