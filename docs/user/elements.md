# Elements

Elements are the characters, places and props you reuse. Make one once, then bring it into any video, picture or film. Open **Elements** from the app list.

## Browse

- **Overview** shows everything, with big picture tiles.
- **Characters**, **Places** and **Props** show one kind each.
- **Looks** lists the look sheets you have captured.
- The search box finds an element by name.

Press a tile to open it. The page shows its name, its look and its look sheet, and where it is used.

## Make one

1. Open **Make** and pick **Character**, **Place** or **Prop**.
2. Describe it and press **Make pictures**. You get four pictures to choose from.
3. Open the one you like in your [Library](library.md) and press **Save as an element**.

Making pictures needs the picture engine. If it is not ready, Home says so and nothing is lost.

## Rename and delete

Open an element you saved, then open the **...** menu under **Manage**. **Rename** lets you type a new name. **Delete** asks you to confirm. Pictures and films that used the element keep working.

A companion or a person in your home is changed where that profile lives, so those have no Rename or Delete here.

## Capture the look

A look sheet shows a character, place or prop from several sides, so it looks the same in every video. Press **Capture the look** on an element you saved. If it cannot be made yet, the button is off and a line says why.

## Use one in a video

In **Video**, then **Create**, press **Cast**. The picker lists your characters, places and props together. Pick up to three, write your words and press **Generate**.

## Who sees what

Everyone sees their own elements and the companions made for them. Teens and children do not see the elements of other people, or the people in your home. Making a clip with Cast is for adults.
