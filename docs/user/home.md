---
title: The Home screen
description: What you see when you open MaiPai, and how to use it.
---

Home is the first page after you sign in. It is short on purpose. It says hello, lets you ask a question, and shows a quick look at your day.

## What is on the screen

- **A greeting** with your name.
- **An ask box.** Type a question and press **Ask**. Chat opens with your words already in the message box. Nothing is sent until you press send, so you can change them first. Three starter buttons under the box fill in a common question for you. While the AI is still installing, the box says so.
- **Today.** A few cards from the apps in your house, like the weather. You only see cards you are allowed to see. A card is left out when it has nothing to show. When there are more than four, a **More** button opens the rest.
- **Shelves.** With the **Shelves** layout, rows of what you were watching or listening to (**Continue**), what is new (**New in your library**), and what you made (**Made at home**). A row is left out when it is empty. If you have nothing to show yet, Home uses the **Ask first** layout.

If you are an owner or an admin you also see:

- **Updates** and **Repairs** buttons at the top, to look after the hub.
- **Getting started.** Up to three setup steps that are not done yet, each with a button to open it and a **Skip** button. When more are left, a link such as **Show 2 more** opens the rest in **Settings**. The card goes away when all steps are done.

## Change how Home looks

Open **Settings**, then **Look and ease**, and find **Home layout** on the **Appearance** card. There are three choices:

- **Shelves** puts your shows, music and things you made up front, with the day beside them. Adults and teens start here.
- **Ask first** puts the ask box first, then where you left off. Children start here.
- **Today board** is a grid of your day, with cards and lists.

## Start a conversation

Use the ask box, or open **Chat** in the navigation.

## Something not working?

| What you see | Why | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| The ask box says the AI is installing | The model is still downloading | Wait. You can still type your question |
| No **Today** cards | No app has anything to show you yet | Come back later, or set up an app that has something to show |
| No **Getting started** card | You are not an owner or admin, or all steps are done | Nothing to do |
| The starter buttons open Chat but nothing is sent | That is on purpose | Press send when you are happy with the words |
