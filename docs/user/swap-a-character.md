# Swap a character in a video

You can put one of your cast into a video you already made or added. The person in the clip gets the cast member's face and hair. Everything else stays the same: the room, the voices, the camera.

## How to do it

1. Open Video, then **Swap character** in the Edit list.
2. Pick the clip.
3. Pick one cast member. Home uses up to three of their photos.
4. Press the arrow. Home starts a new video and keeps your first one.

## Good to know

- Swapping works on up to 10 seconds of the clip. Short clips with one clear face work best.
- Only adults can swap a character.
- It needs a lot of free memory. If Home says there is not enough right now, wait until nothing else is being made and try again.
- If your cast member has no photo yet, Home asks you to add one first.
- You can also ask in chat: "put Sage in that video".
