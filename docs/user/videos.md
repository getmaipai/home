Home can make short videos on your own computer. Nothing is sent to anyone.

Open **Videos** or its **Library** to find clips you made under **Made at home**, alongside the videos from your library folders. To watch a video, see [Watch videos](watch-videos.md). To cut and mix clips, see [Edit a video in the Studio](videos-studio.md). To make a longer story, see [Make a short film](videos-film.md). For adults-only videos, see [Adults-only videos and the lock](videos-locked.md).

## Who made a video, and who can see it

Below a video, you can see its title, creator and date. A video made at home uses a short title from its prompt; the full prompt is in the description. Press **Show more** to read it all.

**Private** means only you can see your video. **Shared** means the people shown can watch it. A scanned video follows the audience set for its folder or source.

To share a video you made, press **Private** or **Shared**, then choose people in your home. Press **Make private** to stop sharing. Only the owner or an admin can change sharing. Children can see videos shared with them, but cannot share videos.

## What you need

A Mac with 128 GB of memory and the video model installed. On any other computer, Home says videos are not available and why.

There are three levels: **Standard**, **Boost** and **Max**.

- **Standard** makes a small picture with sound, up to 10 seconds long. It takes under a minute.
- **Boost** makes a bigger picture with better sound, up to 5 seconds long. It takes about a minute.
- **Max** is for the sharpest picture, up to 10 seconds, with sound. An admin has to get it first: it is a big download (about 71 GB) that comes with its maker's licence, and you find it in Settings, Models and storage. Max also needs an admin's OK for you. Until both are done you will not see it, or you will see it greyed out with a line that says what to do. Max makes one video at a time and takes about 4 minutes. A start picture or a style works on Max only when your engine supports it. If it does not, Home says what is missing and makes nothing.

These times come from tests on a Mac with 128 GB. Your computer may be a little slower or faster, and the first clip after a restart takes longer.

Children and teens always get Standard. They do not choose a level, and their clips are short and have no sound.

## Make a video in the Videos app

Open **Videos**, then **Create**. Create is for adults. Its prompt input follows the Chat composer: a growing text field, a start-frame attachment control, compact visible choices and quality levels in one toolbar, and a round Generate button. Shape, Length, Style, Camera, Voices and the available levels stay visible with the prompt. A small **Inspiration** row with four examples sits below it, followed by **Your clips**, newest first. If video generation is unavailable, you can still type and choose settings; Generate shows why it cannot start.

In the maker:

- **Describe a video.** Write what you want to see. Put each person's line in quotes. As you type, Home may show up to two small style suggestions under the box. Leave one on to use it, or press its **x** to drop it.
- **Start frame.** Use the picture attachment control in the composer toolbar to pick a picture from your Library or add a new one as the video’s first frame.
- **Length.** 5 seconds, or the longest the level you picked can make.
- **Style.** A compact control opens the **Look** choices. The same menu includes **Camera** moves. Your **Look** is natural, classic cartoon, anime, 3D animated or film. Camera moves include dolly in, slow orbit, handheld, slow motion, time-lapse, aerial and close-up. **Auto** is the first choice in each, and it is what you get if you pick nothing: Home keeps the look natural and lets the words decide the camera. If an admin has added styles you may use, the same window also has **Styles**, **Characters** and **Strength** (**Less**, **Normal** or **More**). A style works at some levels only, and the window says which.
- **Level.** Choose Standard ("Fast, with sound"), Boost ("Sharper picture") or Max from a compact selector. You see only the levels you may use. If Max is not installed yet, its card is greyed and says what to do.
- **Shape** and **Voices** stay visible beside **Length** and **Style**; no extra settings fold is needed.
- **Generate.** Press it to start. The button says about how long it takes, for example "Boost, about a minute". Your words stay as you typed them. Home adds the wording for the look and camera behind the scenes.

**Inspiration.** Each picture includes a prompt, look and camera move, for example "Paper boat". Press a picture and its prompt and choices fill into the composer above. Nothing starts until you press **Generate**, so you can change the words first. With no clips yet, examples show above the line "Your clips show here. Pick an idea below or describe a video above and press Generate."

On a phone, the maker stacks into the page above the idea row. **Your clips** are below the ideas. The **View** button holds the filters: **Favorites only**, **Group by words** and **Type**. The newest clip is big and the picture comes first. On a wide screen the older clips sit two to a row. The buttons for a clip show when you point at it, and always show on a phone.

A new clip is the top card. It shows where it is in line, then how far along it is, and it turns into the video by itself. You do not need to reload. Press **Stop** to cancel it.

On a finished clip:

- **Download** saves it.
- **Another take** makes the same words again with a new result.
- **Remix** puts the words and choices back on the left, so you can change them and press **Generate**.
- **Make it Max** makes it again at Max. It shows only when Max is ready and allowed for you.
- **Open in Studio** opens it in the [Studio](videos-studio.md).
- The **star** marks a favorite.

If a clip did not finish, press **Try again**.

If Home cannot make videos right now, **Generate** and the buttons that start a clip are switched off, and a line next to them says why and what to do. Your clips still play.

### My clips

Open **Videos**, then **My clips**. It shows every clip you can see, in groups by day. Clips other people shared with you are here too, with their names.

- **All**, **Favorites** and **Uploaded**. Uploaded shows the pictures you added. Press one to use it as a Start frame.
- **Size.** Drag the slider to make the clips bigger or smaller.
- **Heart.** Press the heart on a clip to make it a favorite.
- Press a clip to play it.

## Tips for better clips

- Name and describe each character, for example "a short blue gnome with a white hat". A vague prompt can give you real people instead of cartoons.
- Say the animation style, for example "hand-drawn cartoon from the 1980s". The Look cards do this for you.
- Let one person speak at a time. Write each line as its own turn.
- Spell a hard name the way it sounds. Write "Seer-ah" for "Ciara".
- Do not write stage directions inside a line of speech, such as "(laughs)". The model says them out loud.
- Real, named people and famous voices do not come out right. A reference picture can keep a look, but it will not copy a voice.

## Ask in chat

Type "make a short video of a paper boat in the rain". Home answers in a few words and shows a card. The card shows what Home is doing, how many seconds it has taken, and a bar. Press **Stop** on the card to cancel.

You can ask for a look, a camera move, a length, a level, or "keep it clean" (no music or laugh track, one speaker at a time). For example: "make a 10 second anime video of a fox in the snow, slow orbit". If a picture, video or song is ahead of yours, the card shows your place in the household queue. Your video starts when its turn comes.

If someone is chatting or talking to Home while a video is being made, the video pauses ("Paused while chat is working") and carries on after the reply, so chat is never slowed down. The wait is not counted against the video.

When it is done, the video plays on the card. It repeats. Press **Download** to save it, **Share** to send it, or **Make another** for a new one from the same words.

## Change a video in chat

Ask chat to change a video you made. Home keeps the old video and makes a new one next to it.

- **Another take.** "Do another one." Same words, a new result.
- **Remix.** "Make it anime" or "same but at night." Same video with the change you name. You can change the words, look, camera, sound, length or level.
- **Make it Max.** "Make it Max." It keeps the same words and the same result, and makes the video again at Max. It works only when Max is installed and allowed for you, and only for a video made from words. Otherwise Home says what is missing and makes nothing.
- **Trim.** "Cut the first two seconds." Say where it starts and ends, in seconds.
- **Continue.** "Keep going from where it ended." Home takes the last picture of the video and makes a new video that starts from it. Say what happens next, or leave it out and Home uses your first words. It keeps the same level and look unless you change them. It needs a computer that can start a video from a picture. If yours cannot yet, Home says so and makes nothing. Only one video is made at a time.
- **Join.** "Join these two videos." Pick 2 to 6 of your own videos, in order. Home puts them one after another with a straight cut, or with a blend from one video into the next if you ask for one, such as a fade, a wipe or a slide. Each video has to be longer than twice the blend.
- **Mix.** "Put my voice over it" or "add my song." Home lays a spoken line, one of your songs or a sound over the video. You can ask for it quieter or louder.

Only videos you made can be changed. Changing, continuing, joining and mixing are for adults. A video in Trash cannot be continued: put it back first. A changed video is never open to more people than the videos it came from. If any video you use is adults only, the new one is adults only too.

Trim, join and mix need the video editor on the AI engine. To do the same by hand, use the [Studio](videos-studio.md). You can also ask chat to cut your videos to the beat of one of your songs, choosing how many beats between cuts and how much of the song to use. If beat analysis or the video editor is not ready, Home says so. If your engine does not have the video editor, Home says it cannot change the video.

## Send a video in a text

Press **Share** on the card. On an iPhone, iPad or Mac in Safari, your device's own share sheet opens with the video attached, so you can pick Messages, AirDrop or Mail. On a browser that cannot share a file, **Share** opens the video in your Library instead, where you can share it with people in your home.

**Download** saves a copy made for texting, named after your words (for example `paper-boat-in-the-rain.mp4`). A video with no sound gets a silent sound track, which phones expect from a video. A video with sound keeps it. If you want the exact file the engine made, press **Download original**.

If a video will not send from a file you saved, use **Share** on the card instead of dragging the file into Messages.

To choose who in your home can watch a saved video, open it in the Library and use **Share**. Pick the people who may see it, or **Everyone in the home**. A child cannot share a video. A grown-up can change this later; turning sharing off removes access right away. Videos from a library folder keep the same access as that folder.

## Make a picture move

After chat has made you a picture, say "animate this picture" or open the **More** menu under the reply and choose **Animate this picture**. On a computer that can start a video from a picture, Home makes a video with sound at your level that starts from it. On other computers it makes a 5 second video with no sound.

Home can animate a picture you made or a photo you attach. Describe the motion you want. The model decides whether to fulfill adult requests; Home blocks output only when its CSAM check finds a candidate.

## Who can make videos

Adults can, on their own screen, in a written chat. Teens can too, with nothing adult or sexual and only the everyday styles. Children can make short cartoon videos of safe things, up to 5 a day, switched on for every child unless you turn it off, with the same limits as pictures. An owner or admin can turn this off. Teens and children get Standard only, as short clips with no sound. They never see a half-drawn frame. Spoken answers, the TV and other shared screens never make videos. A guest cannot.

See [cartoon controls](people.md#cartoon-pictures-and-videos) for the home switch, daily limits and each child's switch.

Home blocks CSAM. The model decides whether to make other adult content, including videos of real people. Child and teen content filters remain in place. A CSAM candidate is withheld if the check cannot run.

## Choose how it works (owner and admin)

Open **Settings**, **Pictures, videos and music**.

- **Video making.** Turn it off to stop all video making, for children too.
- **Level when nobody says.** The level chat uses when nobody asks for one: **Standard** or **Boost**. Adults can ask chat for another level for one video. Teens and children always get Standard.
- **Video models.** Press **Choose models** to open **AI and models**, where you pick the models for Standard, Boost and Max videos and download them. Max is for the people you allow.
- **Styles.** Press **Open Styles** to go to the **Styles** tab, the one place to add, share and remove styles for pictures and videos.

### Add a style for videos

1. Open **Settings**, **Pictures, videos and music**, **Styles**.
2. Press **Browse styles** or **Add from a file**. Home reads which video model the file was made for. A style made for the older video maker works at Boost only, and the row says so.
3. Press **Add**, then choose **Only admins**, **All adults** or **Chosen people** for it. Adult-only styles stay locked until you enter your own passcode.

The video engine checks the file and refuses any style that puts a child in an adult scene. It cannot catch everything, so only add styles you trust.

## Something not working?

- **No video option.** Why: your Mac has less than 128 GB, your account cannot make videos, or videos are off. Fix: ask an owner or admin to check Settings.
- **"No video model is installed".** Why: the video model is not downloaded. Fix: an owner or admin installs it in Settings.
- **Chat made a clip with no sound when I asked for sound.** Why: the sound models are not on this computer yet, or you started from a picture on a computer that cannot yet start a video with sound from one. Fix: an owner or admin gets the sound models in **Settings**, **Pictures, videos and music**, and accepts the licence. Or ask for a video from words only.
- **A video is slow.** Why: it makes the sound and the picture together, and the first one after a restart loads a very large model. Fix: wait. Boost takes about a minute, and the first one after a restart a little more.
- **Your video is waiting.** Why: picture, video and song jobs share a household queue. Fix: wait. Its card shows its place in line and it starts when its turn comes.
- **"Waiting for memory to free up".** Why: another big job has the memory. Fix: wait a few minutes.
- **"The computer got too busy, so your video stopped. It will start again by itself."** Why: the computer needed its power for something else, so it stopped your video. It stops a video last, after it has given back everything that was not in use. When no one is chatting, chat may be a bit simpler for a few minutes first, so your video can keep going. Fix: nothing. Home starts the same video again by itself, with the same words, level and picture, and tries up to two more times. If you press **Stop**, it never starts again.
- **"The computer stayed too busy, so your video could not finish."** Why: Home started it again twice and the computer was still busy. Fix: wait a few minutes, then press **Try again**.
- **Home cannot reach the engine.** Why: the AI engine is restarting. Fix: wait a minute and ask again. Still stuck? Open **Repairs**.
- **"Something went wrong while making it".** Why: the job failed. Fix: press **Try again**.
- **No Animate this picture button.** Why: Home did not make that picture, or a clip is running. Fix: ask for a new picture first.

## Private chats

In an Incognito or temporary chat, the video lives only in memory and is gone when the chat ends. In those chats you can still animate a picture chat made or the first frame of a video you just made.
