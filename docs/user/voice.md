---
title: Talking to Home
description: Use your voice with Home, allow the microphone, pick a voice, and fix voice problems.
---

You can talk to Home and hear it talk back.

## Allow the microphone

The first time you tap the microphone or start a voice conversation, your browser asks to use the microphone. Choose **Allow**. Setup does not ask.

If the browser says no, Home does not open the voice conversation and then close it. It shows a short help box instead. The box names your browser and tells you what to change, and it also covers the Mac's own switch in System Settings, **Privacy and Security**, **Microphone**. After you change it, press **Try again**. Home checks again when you come back to the page.

The help box has a different message for each problem:

- **Blocked.** The browser or the Mac has the microphone turned off for this site.
- **No microphone.** Plug one in, or pick another device.
- **In use.** Another app has the microphone. Close that app.
- **Not a secure address.** Browsers only allow the microphone on `localhost` or an `https` address. Open Home from one of those.

If you have more than one microphone, the **Voices** page in Settings shows a **Microphone** choice. It is remembered on this device only. Voice capture works in Safari and Firefox as well as Chrome.

## Talk to Home

Tap the microphone next to the message box to dictate. Home turns your words into text and sends them when you stop talking. To have a back and forth, use the conversation button. It shows when speech listening and speaking are both installed. Talk over Home, or tap Stop, and it stops speaking and listens.

To hear one reply read aloud, tap **Listen** under it. To have every reply read aloud, open the chat menu at the top and choose **Read replies aloud**.

## Choose your voice

Open **Settings**, the **Me** tab, then **Voices**. Choose a voice in the **Voice for you** list and press **Test** to hear it. Press **Stop** to end the sample. Your pick is saved when you choose it.

An owner or admin can choose a voice for a child. A teen or adult chooses their own. If a voice service is not ready, the page says "Voices are still getting ready" (this takes a minute after setup) or "Home can't reach its voice service", with **Try again**.

## The voice itself

On a computer with 64 GB of memory or more, like the Mac Studio, Home speaks with **Chatterbox Turbo**. It was picked by ear. On smaller computers Home uses **Pocket TTS**, a smaller voice that runs on any machine. If Chatterbox cannot start, Home falls back to Pocket TTS.

## Natural speech sounds

An owner or admin can let spoken replies include a small human touch, like "hmm," or a laugh. Open **Settings**, then **Home settings**, then **Engines and AI**, and find **Natural speech sounds**.

- **Off.** Replies are read exactly as written.
- **Light.** A rare "hmm," or laugh, at most once per reply. This is the default.
- **Natural.** A little more often, at most twice per reply.

These sounds only happen in spoken replies, never in the written chat, and never for children or teens. Home never adds one inside a number, a name, a step or a safety message. Chatterbox can make six sounds that were checked by ear: laugh, chuckle, cough, sigh, clear throat and gasp. Pocket TTS can only say "hmm," as a word.
