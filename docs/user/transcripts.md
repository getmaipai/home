# Make a transcript of a video or song

A transcript is the words said in a video or audio file, with the time of each line. Teens and adults can make one for a file in the Library.

## Make one

1. Open the [Library](library.md) and pick a video or audio file.
2. Press **Transcript**.
3. Press **Make transcript**. Wait while Home listens. It does this on your own computer.

You end with a list of lines. Press a line to jump to that moment. The line being said stays lit as the file plays.

## Search it

Open **Search** and type in **Search lines**. Only the matching lines show.

## Fix it

Open **Edit** to change the words, rename the speakers, or fix a part.

- Change a line, then it saves by itself. The page says **Saved**.
- **Detect speakers** guesses who is talking. Rename a speaker in **Speakers**, and use **Filter by speaker** to see one voice.
- **Redo with the speech engine**: type a **From** and **To** time, like 1:05, and press **Redo**. Home listens to that part again.
- **Find and replace** changes a word across the transcript. It shows the lines that would change first.
- **Undo** steps back. Pick a revision to go back to an older version.

## Download it

Open **Export**. Press **Download SRT**, **Download VTT**, **Download Text**, **Download Markdown** or **Download JSON**. SRT and VTT are caption files that other video players read. Under **Caption tracks**, **Make caption track** adds captions you can show over the video. You can also bring in your own SRT, VTT or text file with **Import SRT, VTT or text**.

## Translate the captions

Under **Caption tracks**, pick a language next to **Translate captions to** and press **Translate**. Your home's own language model makes a new caption track in that language. The first track stays as it is, and each language is its own track. If you fix the transcript later, refresh the first track and translate again. The words pass the same age filter as chat, and nothing is saved if any part is held back.

## Burn the captions into the video

Press **Burn into video** next to a caption track. Home makes a copy of the video with those captions drawn on it, so they show in any player. The copy is a new file in your Library and the original is not changed. It works for videos only, up to 2000 captions, and takes about as long as the video is.

Nothing leaves your home.
