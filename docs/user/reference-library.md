# Offline data

Offline data stores books on your Home computer. People in your household can read those books even when the internet is down.

Only an owner or admin can change the library. Open **Settings** and choose **Offline data** in The home group. The Wikipedia app and the Search and web page link straight to it. Children, teens, and other adults cannot open this page.

Books are grouped by topic: Encyclopedia, Dictionaries, Health and medicine, Travel and the world, and Books and how-to. Each Wikipedia choice says what you get. Everything with pictures is the biggest. Everything without pictures is smaller. Short introductions only is the smallest.

## Choose how much to keep

At the top, the **Disk space** bar shows the whole drive: what the offline books use, what everything else uses, and what is free. Under **How much to keep**, choose **Minimum**, **Moderate**, or **Full**. The choice switches every book at once, and the page marks it. Nothing changes yet: Home stages the changes and shows an **Apply** bar at the bottom. It says how much will download, how long that takes, and how much will be freed. If the drive is too full, the message says so and **Apply** stays off.

## Change one book

Each source has one row, grouped by topic: Encyclopedia, Dictionaries, Health and medicine, Travel and the world, and Books and how-to. A row says what the book is, whether it has pictures, its size, its snapshot date, and whether it is installed. A source that comes in one size has a switch. Wikipedia has one row with a menu: Off, everything with pictures (the biggest, about 127 GB), everything without pictures, or short introductions only. Switch a source on, or pick a version, to download it. Pick Off to remove it. Then choose **Apply**. Removing a book asks first and names any books that children or teens may lose.

While a book downloads, its row shows progress. Choose **Pause** to stop for now, **Resume** to keep going, or **Cancel** to stop the download.

## Pictures in answers

When you ask chat to show you something, Home looks in your offline books first. If a book has a page about it with pictures, those pictures come from your own computer and nothing goes online. Home checks every book you can read, not only Wikipedia.

Not every set has pictures. Vikidia and Simple Wikipedia now install with pictures, which adds about 3 GB. English Wikipedia is the exception: the small set has no pictures, and the full set with pictures is about 127 GB. Choose it only if you have the space. A book you installed earlier without pictures still works for text. Install the picture version to get its pictures.

Children see pictures only from the kids' books. Teens and adults see them from every book they can read. If the books have too few pictures, Home fills the rest from the internet as before.

## If something goes wrong

If Home cannot check the catalogue or drive space, check that Home is online and try again. If a download fails, open this page again to check its state. Ask the owner or admin to check the drive if there is not enough space.
