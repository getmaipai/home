# MaiPai Home: the user guide

Short pages for the people in the house. Start with the first one the
day the hub arrives; the rest are for when you need them.

- [Set up your hub](getting-started.md): walk through the one-time setup wizard, screen by screen.
- [Sign in](sign-in.md): the passcode boxes, what happens after wrong tries, and how to get back in.
- [The Home screen](home.md): what you see when you open MaiPai, and how to use it.
- [Chat and talking to MaiPai](chat.md): how to send a message, use voice input, and manage your conversations.
- [Talking to Home](voice.md): allow the microphone, pick a voice, and turn natural speech sounds on or off.
- [Weather](weather.md): ask in chat, open the Weather app, and set your places and units.
- [Turn Home off and on](maipai-command.md): stop Home to free the computer's memory, start it again, and check it is running.
- [All apps](all-apps.md): find any app, and choose which ones sit in the left rail.
- [Media](media.md): open the Videos, Shows and Films, Music, Podcasts and Books apps from one place.
- [The Library](library.md): find, look at, add, share, keep and delete the pictures, videos, songs and files in your home.
- [Pictures](pictures.md): ask chat for a picture, stop it, change it, and choose who can make pictures and which styles are on.
- [Videos](videos.md): ask chat for a short video, stop it, make another, and choose who can make videos and which styles are on.
- [Wikipedia](wikipedia.md): read Wikipedia from your own library, save articles, and keep them on a device.
- [Books](books.md): read EPUB and PDF books, and listen to audiobooks.
- [Shows and Films](shows.md): browse local shows and films, and keep a watchlist.
- [Songs](songs.md): ask chat to write and make a song, change it by asking, play it, and choose who can make songs.
- [Standard, Boost and Max](levels.md): what the levels mean for pictures, video and songs, and who can use each.
- [Share the computer during big jobs](performance.md): choose whether chat or big jobs get the room, and free up memory.
- [Let Home close apps](close-apps.md): pick the apps Home may close when a big job needs room.
- [Watch videos](watch-videos.md): open the Videos app, play a video from your hub, pick up where you stopped, and save videos for later.
- [What MaiPai remembers](memory.md): see, export, or forget what MaiPai has remembered from your conversations.
- [People and parental controls](people.md): see who is in your household, manage accounts, and check in on a child.
- [Settings](settings.md): find your way around MaiPai's settings, for the household and for you.
- [Offline data](reference-library.md): choose and manage offline books (Wikipedia, health and more) for your household.
- [Ease of use](ease-of-use.md): make words bigger, buttons roomier and colors clearer for yourself.

- [Lists, reminders and alarms](lists-reminders-alarms.md): what to say to add to your shopping list, set a reminder, set an alarm or start a timer.
- [Notifications](notifications.md): see MaiPai's alerts, and set up Telegram notifications.
- [Privacy](privacy.md): see exactly what, if anything, leaves your house.
- [What Home looks up for you](what-home-looks-up.md): every outside service a feature can ask, what it sends, and how to clear what it saved.
- [Set up web search](web-search.md): connect MaiPai to your own SearXNG search server, use it away from home, and fix it if it stops.
- [Blocked sites](blocked-sites.md): see which lists of bad sites web search checks, and choose them.
- [The news shelf](news-shelf.md): how MaiPai keeps recent headlines for the news answer, and how to choose which lists each person sees.
- [Sensitive subjects](sensitive-subjects.md): choose how MaiPai talks with a child about bodies, health, risk and violence.
- [Install, upgrade, uninstall and fix the install](install-upgrade-uninstall.md): put MaiPai Home on a Mac, update it, go back a version, remove it, and fix install problems.
- [Fix a problem](fix-a-problem.md): what to check first when something is not working right.
