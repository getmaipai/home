# Media

Tap **Media** in the left rail. It sits right under Chat.

You see a **Media** column next to your page. It has **Home** and each app you can use: Videos, Shows and Films, Music, Podcasts and Books. Tap one to open that app right there.

**Home** shows rows you can tap. A row with nothing in it does not show.

- **Made at home** has the pictures, songs and clips made in your home.
- **Pick up where you left off** has what you started and did not finish. A thin bar shows how far you got.
- **New in your media** has what was added lately.
- **Recently played** has the songs you played lately.

Use **Search Media** in the Media column to find titles across all five apps. It also finds songs and clips made at home. You only see items you can open.

Each app also works on its own. Pin it to the rail and open it from there, with no Media column.

You only see the apps you're allowed to use. Kids see only what a grown-up allowed. See [Kids and media](kids-and-media.md).

News and Weather are separate apps and are not in the Media column.
