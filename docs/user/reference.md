# Read the offline library

MaiPai's Reference page lets you read books saved on your Home hub.

## Open a book

Choose **Reference** in the app rail. The page lists the books you can read.
Each book shows its language, size, date, and licence. Choose **Browse this
book** to search inside it.

## Find an article

Type a title in **Search articles**. Choose a suggested title to open it.
You can also press **Search** to see full search results. Choose a result to
read that article.

## Read and move around

Choose a heading in **Contents** to jump to that part. On a phone, choose
**Contents** to open the list. Use **Back** and **Forward** to revisit pages.
Links to other saved articles open inside Reference. Links outside your
library are shown as plain text.

Each article ends with its licence and snapshot date. A Reference source in
a chat answer opens the same article in the reader.
