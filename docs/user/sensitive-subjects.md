# Sensitive subjects

Children ask about hard things. This page says how you choose what MaiPai says back, and what never changes.

## For a child

Open the child's page from People, then the Topics tab. Only an owner or admin sees it. A child cannot change their own.

- Bodies and relationships, Health, Risk and Violence each have a choice. "Follow child controls (recommended)" is the safe starting point.
- Family stories can be kept or turned off. If you keep them, you can list the names MaiPai may treat as family.

## What never changes for a child

- Feelings always get care first, and MaiPai points the child to a trusted grown-up.
- Questions about people who might hurt them are always handled with protection first.
- Questions about beliefs get a neutral answer.

If a child says they want to hurt themselves, MaiPai tells them to talk to a grown-up they trust right now and shows the help lines.

## For teens

A teen's own settings stay private. You choose two household rows in Settings, Privacy: Bodies and Risk. They apply to every teen in the house. No one can set these two for a single teen from the family page.
