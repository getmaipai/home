---
title: Standard, Boost and Max
description: What the three levels mean for pictures, video and songs, and who can use each one.
---

Pictures, videos and songs can be made at more than one level. A higher level looks or sounds better and takes longer. It also uses more of the computer.

## What the levels mean

- **Standard** is the everyday level. It is on for everyone the age rules allow.
- **Boost** is a bigger, slower maker. Pictures and videos made at Boost are the ones the older pages called Detailed.
- **Max** is the biggest maker, for one big job at a time. Everything else big waits while a Max job runs. Max shows up only when an owner or admin has picked a Max model for that kind of job. Home does not pick one for you. Today there is a Max model for pictures, on a computer with lots of memory. There is none for video or songs, so those two never offer Max.

Home also has a level called **Reduced**. You never pick it. Only a Max job can bring it in, and only when that job needs more room than a Standard job gets. When that happens:

1. Home waits for a quiet moment in chat.
2. Chat swaps to a smaller model. This takes 20 to 60 seconds.
3. The Max job runs. Chat still answers, but answers are shorter and some extras may pause. A line under the box says so and shows about how many minutes are left.
4. When the job ends, chat goes back to its usual model.

A Standard or Boost job never makes chat simpler, even a long one. If the Max job fits beside chat, chat is not touched either.

## Who can use them

- **Standard:** everyone the age rules allow.
- **Boost:** whoever the admin chose under **Who can use it** for that level. The choices are **Admins only**, **Assigned people only** and **Everyone the age gates allow**.
- **Max:** owners and admins. A grown-up can use it when an admin allows it for them. A child never can. A teen can only if an admin allows it for pictures.

If someone asks for a level they cannot use, Home makes the Standard or Boost version and says why.

## Ask for a level

Say it in chat: "make it Boost". Under a finished picture or video, press **Make it Boost** or **Make it Standard** to make it again the other way.

## Change what a level uses (owner and admin)

1. Open **Settings**, then **AI and models**.
2. Press the **Levels** button on the job's row (pictures, video, music, voice or chat).
3. For each level, choose a model, or **Use the recommended model**.
4. Choose **Who can use it**, then save.

Before you save, the dialog says what the change will do. A level can be turned off.

You'll know it worked when the row shows your levels.
