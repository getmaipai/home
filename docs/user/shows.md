# Watch shows and films

Open **Shows and Films** from your apps. These are files on your home hub. The app does not look up posters or show details online.

The first page has a big picture with **Play** and **More info**, then rows: **Continue watching**, **Recently added**, **Films**, and a **Genre** picker to browse by genre. A row with nothing in it does not show.

The app also has **Shows**, **Films** and **Watchlist** pages. Tap a poster for more info.

- Choose a season to see its episodes. Tap an episode to play it.
- A bar shows how much you watched. A glowing edge marks the episode to watch next.
- **Play** starts the next unfinished episode, or resumes your place.

## Keep a watchlist

On a title's page, choose **My list** to save it, or **Mark finished** when you are done. Playing a saved title moves it to **Watching**.

Open **Watchlist** to find these titles. Use **Status** to show one group: **Want to watch**, **Watching** or **Completed**, or pick **All**. Your list and your place belong to you.

## While you watch

- **Skip intro** and **Skip credits** appear when the file has those chapters.
- At the end of an episode, the player can offer the next one.
- Saved files play with the internet off while your hub is within reach.

## Children

Children see only titles cleared for them. Their row says **Keep watching**. On a child's screen, the player never starts the next episode by itself.

## No titles?

If there are no titles, ask a grown-up to add a library folder in **Settings**, **Storage**.
