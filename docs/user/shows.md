# Shows and Films

Open Shows and Films from your apps. These are files on your home hub.
The app does not look up posters or show details online.

Home has a big picture, shows you started, and new titles.
Choose Shows or Films to browse. Tap a poster for more info.
Choose a season to see its episodes. Tap an episode to play it.
A bar shows how much you watched. A glowing edge marks the episode to watch next.
Play starts the next unfinished episode, or resumes your place.

On a title's page, choose My list to save it, or Mark finished when you are done. Playing a saved title moves it to Watching.
Find these titles in Watchlist. Pick a status to show one group, or All.
Your list and your place belong to you.

Skip intro and Skip credits appear when the file has those chapters.
At the end of an episode, the player can offer the next one.
On a child's screen, it never starts the next episode by itself.
Children see only titles cleared for them. Their row says Keep watching.

Saved files play with the internet off while your hub is within reach.
If there are no titles, ask a grown-up to add a library folder.
