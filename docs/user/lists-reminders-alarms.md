---
title: Lists, reminders and alarms
description: What to say to MaiPai to add to your shopping list, set a reminder, set an alarm or start a timer.
---

You can ask MaiPai to keep your shopping list, remind you of things, wake you up and time things. Type it in chat or say it out loud.

## What you need to get started

You need to be signed in as yourself. If MaiPai does not know who is talking, it will not set an alarm.

## Shopping list

Say one of these.

- Say "Add milk to the shopping list."
- Say "Put milk on the shopping list."
- Say "What's on my shopping list?"
- Say "What do I need to buy?"

You'll know it worked when MaiPai says it added the item, or reads your list back.

## Reminders

- Say "Remind me in an hour to check the oven."
- Say "Remind me tomorrow at noon to walk the dog."
- Say "What reminders do I have?"
- Say "Cancel the reminder to call Nadia."

After you set a reminder, the chat shows **Reminder set for** and the time. Tap **Undo** within 10 minutes if it is wrong.

## Alarms and timers

- Say "Set an alarm for 7."
- Say "Wake me at 6:30 on weekdays."
- Say "What alarms do I have?"
- Say "Cancel my alarm."
- Say "Set a timer for 10 minutes."

A timer also shows **Undo** for 10 minutes.

An alarm set by an adult or a teen rings even in quiet hours. A child's alarm waits until quiet hours end.

## When MaiPai asks first

MaiPai asks you to say yes or tap a button before it does these things.

- It deletes a list.
- It clears a list.
- It changes more than 5 items at once.

If you say no, nothing changes.

## Who can do what

Children and teens can add items to the shopping list for the home and tick them off. They can run their own lists, reminders and timers. A parent does the rest. If a child asks for more, MaiPai says a parent can do that.

## Choose how much MaiPai does on its own

An owner or admin can change this. Open **Settings**, then **AI and models**, then **Advanced**. You will see three rows.

- **Reminder actions**
- **Timer actions**
- **List actions**

For each row, pick **Do at once**, **Ask first** or **Off**. If a row is **Off**, MaiPai tells you it can't do that. Change the row to turn it back on.
