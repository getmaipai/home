# News

Open **News** from your apps. It reads the same [news shelf](news-shelf.md) that MaiPai uses when you ask for headlines.

On a wide screen News has a sidebar on the left, like the one in Chat. It holds the search box, **Today**, **My Mix** (teens and adults) and **Saved**, then every **Topic** you can open, then the sources you follow, with **Manage** at the end of that list. Choose a topic or a source to see its stories. On a phone the same choices are in the menu drawer.

Each topic page is laid out for its subject, the way Apple News does it. World, Business, Sports and Kids open on **Top Stories**: three tall cards, three cards with the picture on the right, then the rest as a list. Science, Health and Environment open with two large cards, then two cards with the picture on the right. Local, Technology and Entertainment open on **Today's News**: three tall cards, then **More for you** with one wide card. A source you follow keeps one wide lead card over a list.

**Today** shows recent stories. Open a story to read its text. Teens and adults also see the pictures and captions the article carries, but not ads. **Following** has Topics and Sources tabs: use **Follow** to keep a channel, or open its row to see stories from that channel. **Saved** holds stories you saved. Select saved stories and choose **Remove** to take them out of that list.

Teens and adults also have **My Mix**. It picks stories from what you open and save, and from your More like this and Less like this choices. Open the menu on My Mix and choose **Reset My Mix** to start over, or turn My Mix off in News settings. Children get stories from the Kids' lists, and their reading is never used to learn interests. A child reads inside News, with no links to a publisher's site. Guests do not get News.

## Find a story

Type in the **Search News** box at the top of the sidebar (on a phone, at the top of the page) and press Enter. You get stories that have all of your words in the headline, the short summary, the source name or the topic. Matching topics and sources show first, then your saved stories, then the rest by day. Tap a chip under the box to see only your **Saved only** stories, only **Today**, or one topic. Tap **All** to take the chip off.

On the Today and My Mix pages, the topic chips under the title show only the stories on one topic. The line under the title tells you the day and when Home last checked the news.

Home remembers your last five searches so you can run one again, and **Clear** removes them. Only you can see them, and they stay on the hub. Children can search the Kids' stories, and Home keeps no list of what they searched.

## Share a story or come back to it

Every story, topic, source and search has its own web address. Copy it from the address bar to send it to someone in your home, or bookmark it. Reload the page and you are back on the same story. Someone you send it to needs to be signed in to Home and allowed to see that story.

When you open a story that Home has not read yet, you see its short summary right away and the full text takes its place a moment later.

## Listen, Key points and Discuss

Three buttons sit under a story's headline.

**Listen** reads the story to you in your hub's voice. The player appears at the bottom of the screen with pause, speed and a close button, and it keeps going when you open another page. It shows which paragraph it is on. Nothing leaves your home.

**Key points** shows a short summary written on your hub from the article. If the summary is not ready yet, the button asks the hub to write it. You can read the whole story either way.

**Discuss** opens a chat and sends the story with the question "Read this article and tell me what you think, including anything that looks one-sided or missing." Discuss is for teens and adults. A child sees a note to ask a grown-up.

## News settings

Open the gear in News. Reading choices include My Mix, where stories open and the news digest. My Mix works now. The choice of where stories open and the news digest do nothing yet. Only an admin can change the **News shelf** lists and extra feed boxes. See [the shelf guide](news-shelf.md).

## Without internet

News can use stories already on the hub. A page or picture that has not been saved there needs a connection. If a story is missing, try another story or come back when the hub is online. Your device still needs to reach your hub.

## Your privacy

Your follows, saves and reading history stay on the hub. A picture request goes through the hub when a screen first shows that story. Opening a story may ask its publisher for the full text. See [Privacy](privacy.md#news) for each connection and what it sends.
