---
title: Books
description: Read books and PDFs, listen to audiobooks, and pick up where you stopped, on your own hub.
---

The **Books** app opens the books and audiobooks saved on your hub. Nothing here comes from the internet, so it works with the internet off. Like Videos, Books stays dark, even when the rest of Home is light. The page you read is light, like paper.

## Open it

Open **Books** from the apps list. You see four tabs: **Reading now**, **Books**, **Audiobooks** and **Series**.

- **Reading now** shows the books you are reading and the audiobooks you are listening to. A thin bar under each cover shows how far you got. Under that are your books and your audiobooks.
- **Books** shows every e-book (EPUB) and PDF.
- **Audiobooks** shows every audiobook. An audiobook is one file (m4b) or a folder of parts.
- **Series** groups books that belong together.

A row with nothing in it does not show. An admin adds book folders in **Settings**, **Home**, **Storage**.

## Read a book

Press a cover, then the play button next to **Read**. The book opens full screen.

- Press the arrows, or the left and right keys, to turn the page.
- Press the table of contents button for chapters.
- Press **Menu** at the bottom, or tap the page, to hide or show the top bar.
- Press **Close** to leave. Home saves your place on every page turn.

Open the book again and it opens on the page where you stopped, on any screen in your home.

## Listen to an audiobook

Press a cover, then the play button next to **Listen**. The audiobook player shows the cover, the chapter, a bar to move around, back 15 seconds and forward 15 seconds. The speed button picks a slower or faster speed. Home remembers it (**Settings**, **Voice**).

- **Chapters** lists every chapter. Press one to jump there. A bar shows how far you got in it.
- A folder audiobook plays its parts in order, one after the other.
- Open the audiobook later and it starts in the same place, even in the middle of a chapter.
- **Close** leaves the player. The sound keeps playing in a small bar at the bottom of Books.

### Sleep timer

Press **Sleep** at the top. Pick 15, 30, 45 or 60 minutes, or **End of chapter**. The sound fades out and stops, in any app. **Off** turns the timer off.

## Read it and listen to it

When a book and an audiobook have the same title and author, and sit in the same folder, Home links them. The book's page shows both, **Read** and **Listen**, and each one keeps its own place.

## Kids

A child sees only the books and audiobooks marked for children, from the library folders a grown-up chose. A child gets no new-for-you rows and no autoplay to the next part.

## What is not here yet

Reading aloud, bookmarks and notes are not built yet. You cannot change the font size or the page look (paper, sepia or night) yet. Books open on paper.
