# Add a voice

Open **Settings**, then **Voices**, and choose **+ Add voice**. Pick a tab:

- **Download** to search voices. Choose a gender, country or quality to narrow the list. Press Play to hear a sample, then Add to use that voice. You can change it later.
- **Clone** to make a voice from a 6 to 30 second recording of your own voice, or one you have permission to use. Give it a name and choose a WAV, MP3 or FLAC file.
- **Inspired** to make a new voice that has a similar feel to a recording. It is not a copy. Choose how different it should sound, then add the recording.

Your new voice appears with the other voices. A parent or admin can help choose a voice for a child.
