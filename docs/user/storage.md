# See how much space your home is using

Open **Settings**, then **Your data and apps**. On the **Data and privacy** tab, choose **Storage**. An admin can also open it from **Backups, storage and updates**.

## What everyone sees

Each person sees their own row: the space used, their limit, and the kind of file that uses the most. A child sees only their own row.

Owners and admins also see a **Household total** bar, one row for every person, and a row for files shared by people who are no longer here. Those files stay, and the household keeps them.

Press **Trash** to bring back or delete for good what you deleted. See [Trash](trash.md).

## Set the limits (owner or admin)

The **Limits** card is on the **Storage and backups** tab of **Backups, storage and updates**. It shows the same fields as the Storage page.

- **Household storage cap**: the most all the files in the house may use. Setup fills this in from the disk. Zero means no cap is set.
- **Default per-person storage cap**: how much each person may use. New people start at 20 GB.

Both numbers are in bytes.

## Clear saved online data (owner or admin)

The **Saved online data** card lists copies of weather, news and other things Home looked up online. Your chats, memories and news shelf stay.

1. Find the source. Press **Clear** on its row, or press **Clear all**.
2. Confirm.

Home fetches the data again the next time someone asks.
