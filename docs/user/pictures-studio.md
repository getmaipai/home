---
title: Edit a picture in the Studio
description: Open any of your pictures in the Studio to crop, fix the light, remove a background, erase an object or change it in words.
---

The Studio is where you change a picture you already have. Everything happens on your own computer. Nothing is sent to anyone.

## Open a picture

- In the **Image** app, choose **Studio** under **Edit**, then tap one of your pictures.
- In the **Library**, open a picture and press **Edit**.
- In the **Image** app, tap a picture in **All images** or **Recently made**.

You only see your own pictures. A locked picture shows a lock until you unlock it.

## The tools

- **Comment.** Tell Home what to change, like "make the sky pink". Tap or drag a box on the picture, or choose **Brush** and paint the part to change. Only that part changes.
- **Erase.** Paint over something and Home fills the hole.
- **Remove BG.** Keeps the subject and makes the background clear.
- **Crop.** Drag the box, pick a shape, turn the picture or flip it. Turning and flipping come first, then the crop.
- **Adjust.** Brightness, contrast, color and warmth. **Auto** fixes the light in one press.
- **Resize.** Make it bigger (2x or 4x) or set a new size.
- **Markup.** Draw or write on it.
- **More.** Blur the background.

If a tool needs the picture model and it is not ready, the tool tells you in one line and you can try again later. Crop, Adjust, turn and flip always work.

## Keep or discard

Each tool shows a preview first. Press **Apply** to keep it as a new version, or discard it. The original is never changed.

- **Compare.** Press **Compare** to slide between the first picture and the one you have now.
- **Undo.** Open the **Versions** menu and choose the version before. Nothing is deleted.
- Each version is also a picture in your [Library](library.md).

## Make a video from it

In the [Library](library.md), open the picture and press **Make a video from this**. The Video app opens with the picture as the first frame. Nothing starts until you press the button there.

See [Pictures](pictures.md) for making pictures and who can make them.
