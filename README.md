<h3 align="center">Free AI that runs on your own hardware, in one private home for your whole family.</h3>

<p align="center"><a href="#install">Quick start</a> · <a href="docs/site-fumadocs/content/docs/user/index.mdx">Documentation</a> · <a href="CHANGELOG.md">Releases</a></p>

Your own private AI at home, with chat, voice, pictures and videos, on your own hardware - for protection, privacy, and independence.

Music, pictures, video, films, podcasts, chat, voice and your smart home share one memory, one Library and one set of family profiles. Nothing is sent to a cloud service to make any of it. *Preview* means it works with rough edges. *Planned* means it is not built yet. *Max* needs the Max resource level. *Adults* means adult profiles only.

## What you can do

### Music

- **Create a song.** Describe it, choose lyrics or instrumental, Quick or Full. Lyrics are timed and light up word by word.
- **Create a band.** A name, a look and a sound, with pictures drawn for you.
- **Create an album.** A title, a cover and the songs, in chat or in Create.
- **Create a song style.** Save a sound once, reuse it for many songs.
- **Make a new song with an existing band.** Press **Song like this** or **Band like this** on any playing song.
- **Use an existing singer.** Your companion or a household voice sings the song in its own voice.
- **Make a cover or remix.** Bring your own audio file, or pick a song you made. Change the style, words, tempo or voice.
- **Split any song into stems.** Vocals, drums, bass and other. Standard, finer and guitar-and-piano splits.
- **Clean up a song.** Remove vocals, keep or drop parts, trim, fade, set loudness, **cut to the beat**.
- **Mix in the Studio.** Waveform tracks, mute, solo, volume, section flags, EQ and compressor.
- **Make sound effects.** Type a sound, get three takes, loop it with no click, add it to a song.
- **Add music to a video.** Lay a new track under a clip.
- **Stream your own library.** Albums, artists, playlists, search, radio from your music, internet radio, and an AI DJ that speaks between songs.
- **Learn about what plays.** Song facts, artist photos and bios, album covers, lyric translation.
- **Create a singer.** *Planned.*

### Pictures

- **Make a picture** from words, with variations, 18 launch templates and styles (LoRAs) you can browse and install.
- **Replace clothes, background or face, and keep the original faces.** A face guard checks the likeness before an edit is kept.
- **Edit in chat.** Crop, adjust, blur the background, find and erase people, animals or objects.
- **Edit in the editor.** Remove background, Erase, Upscale, Resize, Markup, or describe a change in words.
- **Save subjects.** Characters, props and places you can use again, private to you unless you share them.
- **Ask about a picture.** Attach a photo and chat about it.

### Video

- **Make a video** from words or a start picture, with sound, 5 or 10 seconds, with Look and camera moves (eight camera recipes with loop previews).
- **Choose a film look with LoRA styles.** Night film look, Characters and Strength sliders, and suggested styles.
- **Continue, extend or retake a clip.** Set an end frame. *Max* for extend and retake.
- **Join clips with 19 transitions.** Add a title card, rolling credits and cast credits.
- **Edit in chat or the Studio.** Trim, mix, reframe, grade, change speed, add effects (speed ramp, reverse, freeze, loop, flip, rotate, sharpen, denoise, picture-in-picture, Ken Burns) and a letterbox.
- **Remove a video background.** Color, blur or see-through.
- **Upscale and sharpen** a clip with progress.
- **Restyle a clip.** Pull a colour look from another clip. *Max.*
- **Hear it in surround sound.** Add a 5.1 file to a stereo clip and export it with side channels.
- **Cut to the beat** of a track.
- **Add voice-over, music and captions** in the Studio, then export.

### Movies and shows

- **Make a film from a prompt.** Home plans the shots, casts your characters, draws stills, makes the clips, adds music and assembles it. Resume at any step, or redo one shot.
- **Keep Productions.** A private home for each film or show: cast, places, props, script, source clips and finished cuts, with sharing you control.
- **Cast from saved subjects.** Each character gets model sheets from four angles so they look the same in every shot.
- **Make a film with no faces.** Narration, Ken Burns moves and music.
- **Make your own AI podcast.** Pick a format and a topic and synths write and speak the episode, with overlap, backchannels, chapters and stingers. Formats: news briefing, kids' news, round table, debate, discussion, film review, radio announcer, DJ links and narration. *Preview: the finished audio mix waits on the next engine update.*
- **Get the news reported to you.** An anchor reads a daily briefing built from the news shelf you chose, your weather and household life, such as a birthday or a trip. A kids' news host is warm and loose. Failed episodes retry.
- **Schedule a show.** Due shows appear on Today and under **New show** in Podcasts.
- **Hear a DJ.** The AI DJ speaks between songs in your companion's voice, off, minimal or full, with a fact when Home has one. Held for parents until they turn it on for children and teens.

### Synths

- **Make a synth.** An AI being with a name, personality, voice, look and a life that carries on from day to day.
- **Make one your companion.** It chats with you, speaks, and shows its face on every reply. Companions also live on robots.
- **Cast one in your podcast or show.** Put Abraham Lincoln on a round table, or make a friend the host. Hosts remember last week and mention your birthday. *Preview.*
- **Cast one in your movie.** Link a synth to a saved subject and the film uses its look and voice. Spoken dialogue between synths in a film is *Planned.*
- **Base a synth on a real person, living or historical.** Give it the real name and Home's researcher builds a sourced history that it keeps current. *Preview.*
- **Talk with a synth of Abraham Lincoln, or anyone else.** Ask a question in chat, or hear them debate on a round table. It answers from the sourced history and says when it does not know. *Preview.*
- **Give a synth a voice.** Pick a household voice, a voice cloned from a clip, or an inspired voice.

### Voice

- **Talk and listen.** Natural speech with small laughs and sighs, and a voice call button.
- **Clone a voice from a clip.** Every adult can. Your robot or a synth can then speak in it, and a child's robot speaks only a clip voice chosen for it. Or make an inspired voice that is based on the clip and is not a copy.
- **Change the voice** in any audio or video file.
- **Add a voice-over** from a script.
- **Wake Home with a word.** *Preview.*

### Chat

- **Chat with memory.** See, fix or forget what Home remembers.
- **Pick an answer style.** Instant, Thinking or Max, and every reply says which one answered.
- **Research a topic.** A plan, up to 12 searches, claims marked "not confirmed by the sources".
- **Search the web** with DuckDuckGo or your own SearXNG.
- **Code in a private workspace** with personal keys.
- **Set reminders, lists, alarms and timers.** Undo works.

### Documents and Library

- **Write a document** in chat and work on it beside the conversation.
- **Keep everything in one Library.** Pictures, videos, songs and files, sharing and a trash you can restore.
- **Get captions and transcripts.** Detect speakers, fix a range, find and replace, translate, burn them in. Export SRT, VTT, text, Markdown or JSON.
- **Search your reference library.** Wikipedia and books, with pictures in answers.
- **Talk through your documents** as a spoken discussion. *Planned.*

### Integrations

- **Home Assistant.** Control lights, locks and scenes by room or name, with a confirm step and Undo. Schedule actions and read automations. See rooms and devices on the House page. Owners can restart Home Assistant itself, always after a confirm.
- **Uptime Kuma.** Ask what is up or down.
- **Telegram.** Get your notifications on your phone in your language.
- **MCP servers.** Admins can connect them. Several connections of one kind work at once.
- **MusicBrainz, Cover Art Archive and Wikimedia.** Match songs, fetch covers, artist photos and bios. Each has an off switch.
- **Hugging Face and Civitai.** Install styles. Civitai is off by default.

### Family, safety and language

- **Give everyone a profile.** Children are restricted by default, teens get more privacy, guests get the teen ceiling. The safety gates cannot be switched off.
- **Lock adult pictures and clips** behind a fresh passcode, with Lock now and Mark as NSFW. *Adults.*
- **Sign in carefully.** Passcode boxes, two-step codes, a lockout that cannot lock out the owner, and a check on the address Home opens at.
- **Speak your language.** English (US or UK) or Spanish per person, in every screen, notice and spoken time.
- **Get clear errors.** Each says what went wrong and what to do.

### Resource levels

- **Reduced, Standard, Boost and Max.** Pick how much of the computer a job may use. A held job says why, and offers **Make it now at a lower level** or **Wait for room**. One heavy job per person at a time, in fair order.

## Private by design

Nothing leaves your home unless you ask for it. The things that do go out are ones you start: web searches, weather lookups, a word or place lookup, downloads of models and updates, and a daily check for a new release. The privacy page in Settings lists every one and what it sends. There is no tracking, no crash reporting and no training on your family's words.

## Install

On the Mac that will run Home, open Terminal and run:

```bash
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/getmaipai/home/main/scripts/install.sh | sudo bash
```

Home is up in about a minute. Open the address it prints and the setup wizard installs the AI engine. Steps, upgrade, rollback and uninstall: [Install, upgrade, uninstall and fix](docs/user/install-upgrade-uninstall.md).

**What you need.** A Mac for chat, voice and music. Reading pictures needs 32 GB of memory or more. Making video needs a Mac with 128 GB. Each feature's guide page lists what it needs. This is an early release (0.2.x), first tested on a Mac Studio, so read the [changelog](CHANGELOG.md) before you upgrade.
