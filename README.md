<h3 align="center">Free AI that runs on your own hardware, in one private home for your whole family.</h3>

<p align="center"><a href="#install">Quick start</a> · <a href="docs/site-fumadocs/content/docs/user/index.mdx">Documentation</a> · <a href="CHANGELOG.md">Releases</a></p>

Your own private AI at home, with chat, voice, pictures and videos, on your own hardware - for protection, privacy, and independence.

Music, pictures, video, films, podcasts, chat, voice and your smart home share one memory, one Library and one set of family profiles. Nothing is sent to a cloud service to make any of it, and the AI is uncensored for adults. *Preview* means it works with rough edges. *Planned* means it is not built yet. *Max* needs the Max quality level. *Adults* means adult profiles only.

## What you can do

### An assistant that does things

- **Ask once, and it gets done.** In one chat Home makes pictures, songs, videos and films, edits them, writes documents, researches questions, sets reminders and controls your house. You never switch apps.
- **It asks before anything that matters.** Locking a door, restarting a server or cancelling a schedule always shows a confirm step, and house actions and reminders offer **Undo**.
- **It acts on your house.** Lights, locks and scenes through Home Assistant, scheduled actions, and a check on what is up or down with Uptime Kuma.
- **It connects to more.** Admins can plug other tools into chat with MCP servers, and decide which tools each person gets.
- **A failed tool never breaks the answer.** If one step fails, Home says so and carries on with the rest.

### A companion that is yours

- **One companion, everywhere.** Pick a synth as your companion and it is the voice and face of your chat, your music DJ and your robot.
- **It has a personality and a voice.** Warm or dry, short or chatty, with its own look. Make one, copy one, or base one on a person.
- **It knows you.** It remembers what you tell it, and carries a life of its own from day to day.
- **It lives on your robot.** The robot speaks in its voice, shows its face and brings back what it was told.
- **Ask another one.** Type @ and a name to ask a different companion a single question.

### Music

- **Create a song.** Describe it or write the lyrics. Choose a short hook or a full three-minute song. The lyrics light up word by word as it plays.
- **Create a band.** Name it, describe the sound and the look, and Home draws the members.
- **Create an album.** A title, cover art and a set of songs that belong together.
- **Make a new song with an existing band.** Press **Song like this** or **Band like this** on anything that is playing.
- **Use an existing singer.** Have your companion or a voice from your household sing a song.
- **Make a cover or a remix.** Bring your own audio file or pick a song you made, then change its style, words, tempo or voice.
- **Split a song into parts.** Vocals, drums, bass and everything else, as separate tracks. Remove the vocals for karaoke.
- **Mix in the Studio.** See each track as a waveform, then mute, solo, set volume and add EQ.
- **Make sound effects.** Type "a wooden door creaking open" and pick from three takes. Loop one with no click, or drop it into a song.
- **Add music to a video.** Lay a new track under any clip.
- **Play your own library.** Albums, artists, playlists, internet radio and an AI DJ that talks between songs. See the story behind a song, the artist's photo and bio, and translated lyrics.
- **Create a singer.** *Planned.*

### Pictures

- **Make a picture.** Describe it, make variations, or start from one of 18 templates.
- **Change clothes, background or face and keep the original faces.** Home checks that the person still looks like themselves before it keeps the edit.
- **Edit in chat.** Crop, adjust, blur the background, or find and erase a person, animal or object.
- **Edit in the editor.** Remove background, Erase, Upscale, Resize, Markup, or describe the change in words.
- **Save characters, props and places.** Use them again in later pictures, videos and films. They are private unless you share them.
- **Ask about a picture.** Attach a photo and chat about it.

### Video

- **Make a video.** Describe it or start from a picture. You get picture and sound together, up to 10 seconds a clip, with a camera move such as slow orbit, dolly in, aerial or handheld.
- **Pick a style in plain words.** Say "claymation", "anime", "classic cartoon", "3D animated" or "1980s hand-drawn", or choose a Look card. Install more styles from Hugging Face.
- **Make it longer.** Press **Continue** and the next clip starts where the last one ended. Join up to 6 clips with transitions. Or ask for a film and Home plans, shoots and assembles 40 seconds or more.
- **Join clips with 19 transitions.** Add a title card, rolling credits and cast credits.
- **Edit in chat or the Studio.** Trim, mix, reframe, change speed, add a slow pan and zoom, reverse, freeze, loop, flip, sharpen, denoise, or put one clip inside another. Add cinema bars and a film look.
- **Cut to the beat.** Cut your clips to the beat of a song, so every cut lands on the music.
- **Hear it in surround sound.** Add a 5.1 surround file to a stereo clip and export it.
- **Remove a video background.** Replace it with a color, a blur or see-through.
- **Upscale and sharpen** a clip.
- **Add voice-over, music and captions** in the Studio, then export.
- **Extend, retake or restyle a clip.** Restyle borrows the colors of another clip. These need the *Max* quality level.

Try these in chat:

- *Make a 10 second claymation video of a fox in the snow, slow orbit.*
- *Keep going from where it ended.*
- *Make it anime.*
- *Join these three clips with a fade.*

### Movies and shows

- **Make a film from one sentence.** Say "make a 40 second claymation film of a paper boat in the rain". Home writes the shots, casts your characters, draws each scene, makes the clips, adds music and puts it together. Redo one shot, or change the music, without starting over.
- **Keep Productions.** A private folder for each film or show, with its cast, places, props, script, clips and finished cuts. You decide who can see it.
- **Keep characters looking the same.** Each character is drawn from four angles first, so they match from shot to shot.
- **Make a film with no faces.** Narration over slowly moving pictures, with captions and music.
- **Make your own AI podcast.** Pick a format and a topic, and synths write and speak the episode, talking over each other like real people. Formats: news briefing, kids' news, round table, debate, discussion, film review, radio announcer, DJ links and narration. *Preview: the finished audio mix waits on the next engine update.*
- **Get the news reported to you.** An anchor reads a daily briefing built from the news you chose, your weather, and things from your household such as a birthday or a trip. A kids' news host is warm and loose.
- **Schedule a show.** New episodes show up on Today and in Podcasts.
- **Hear a DJ.** The AI DJ talks between songs in your companion's voice. Turn it off, keep it short, or let it share a fact about the song.

### Synths

- **Make a synth.** An AI being with a name, personality, voice, look and a life that carries on from day to day.
- **Make one your companion.** It chats with you, speaks, and shows its face on every reply. Companions also live on robots.
- **Cast one in your podcast or show.** Put a historical figure on a round table, or make a friend the host. Hosts have lives that carry from one episode to the next, and can work in your birthday or a trip. *Preview.*
- **Cast one in your movie.** Link a synth to a saved character and the film uses its look and voice. Spoken dialogue between synths in a film is *Planned.*
- **Base a synth on a real person, living or historical.** Give it the real name and Home builds a sourced history and keeps it current. *Preview.*
- **Talk with a figure from history, or anyone else.** Ask a question in chat, or hear two of them debate. It answers from the sourced history and says when it does not know. *Preview.*
- **Give a synth a voice.** Pick a household voice, a voice cloned from a clip, or an inspired voice.

### Memory

- **Never repeat yourself.** Home remembers your tastes, your routines, your kid's allergy and the trip you are planning, and brings a fact up only when it changes the answer.
- **Go Incognito.** Turn it on from your profile menu and nothing from that chat is saved or remembered. It is forgotten when you turn it off, after two idle hours, or when the server restarts.
- **Say "remember that...".** A note you ask for is always saved, with the date.
- **See, fix or forget.** Look at every memory, forget one, forget several or clear them all. Forgotten means wiped, not hidden.
- **Share with the house.** An adult chooses who may hear a household memory: everyone, teens and adults, or adults only. Parents can look at a child's memories and export them.
- **Keep secrets out.** Home never stores passwords, PINs or keys, and blanks them from the chat history.
- **Give a synth a memory of its own.** Its life, with dated events, carries across chats and shows.

### Voice

- **Talk like you would to a person.** Speak, and Home answers in a natural voice. Talk over it and it stops and listens, with no button to press.
- **Hear emotion, not a reading.** Replies can include a laugh, a chuckle, a sigh, a gasp or a clear throat, chosen to fit what was said. You set how often: off, light or natural.
- **Get the right tone.** On an emotional message Home decides whether you want advice or someone to listen.
- **Clone a voice from a clip.** Every adult can. Your robot or a synth can then speak in it. Or make an inspired voice that is based on the clip and is not a copy.
- **Change the voice** in any audio or video file.
- **Add a voice-over** from a script.
- **Wake Home with a word.** *Preview.*

### Chat

- **Choose how hard it thinks.** Instant for quick answers, Thinking for harder ones, Max for the hardest. Every reply shows which one answered.
- **Research a question.** Ask something big and Home searches the web in several rounds and reads the pages. The answer shows its sources and says which claims it could not confirm.
- **Search the web** with DuckDuckGo or your own SearXNG.
- **Write code** in a private workspace with your own keys.
- **Set reminders, lists, alarms and timers.** Undo any of them.

### Documents and Library

- **Write a document** in chat and keep working on it beside the conversation.
- **Keep everything in one Library.** Pictures, videos, songs and files, with sharing and a trash you can restore from.
- **Get captions and transcripts.** Home finds who is speaking. Fix a line, find and replace, translate, burn the captions into the video, or export them as SRT, VTT, text, Markdown or JSON.
- **Look things up in your own books.** Wikipedia and other reference books on your computer, with their pictures in the answers.
- **Talk through your documents** as a spoken discussion. *Planned.*

### Apps

- **News.** Read the topics and sources you follow, laid out like Apple News, with Today, My Mix and Saved. Each story has **Key points**, an AI summary written on your own computer, **Listen**, which reads it to you, and **Discuss**, which opens a chat about the story.
- **Wikipedia, kept on your computer.** Search and read English Wikipedia, with or without pictures, save articles, and keep reading with the internet off. Add dictionaries, health, travel and how-to books the same way.
- **Weather.** An animated sky card with your places, your current location and Fahrenheit or Celsius. Ask in chat and get the same card.
- **Podcasts.** Follow shows, play new episodes at once, save them to listen offline, and pick up where you stopped.
- **Videos, Shows and Films.** Browse your own files, keep a watchlist, play the next episode.
- **Books.** Read EPUB and PDF books and listen to audiobooks. Your place follows you from screen to screen.
- **Radio.** Internet stations you can save and record.
- **Clock.** Alarms, timers, world clocks and a stopwatch.
- **House.** See your rooms and devices from Home Assistant, and control them with one tap.

### Integrations

- **Home Assistant.** Control lights, locks and scenes by room or by name in chat, with a confirm step and Undo. Schedule actions and read your automations.
- **Uptime Kuma.** Ask what is up or down on your network.
- **Telegram.** Get your notifications on your phone, in your language.
- **MCP servers.** Admins can connect other tools to chat.
- **MusicBrainz, Cover Art Archive and Wikimedia.** Match your songs, fetch album covers and artist photos. Each has an off switch.
- **Hugging Face and Civitai.** Install new picture and video styles. Civitai is off until you turn it on.

### Adults, family and safety

- **Give everyone a profile.** Children are filtered by default, teens get more privacy, guests get the teen limits. Parents set the rest, and the safety gates for children and teens cannot be switched off.
- **Uncensored for adults.** Adult profiles get straight answers and the full creative range: frank talk on health, law and politics, dark and mature fiction, horror, war and romance, with no content filter and no lectures.
- **Private when you want it.** Mark any picture or clip as adult and it sits behind a locked tile until you enter a fresh passcode. Lock everything at once, and let the lock close itself again.
- **Speak your language.** English (US or UK) or Spanish per person, on every screen, notice and spoken time.
- **Sign in safely.** Passcode boxes, two-step codes, and a lockout that never locks out the owner.
- **Get clear errors.** Each says what went wrong and what to do.

### For the person running the server

- **Four quality levels.** Standard is the everyday level. Boost is bigger and slower. Max is the biggest, one job at a time, and needs a big download and your OK. Reduced is never picked: a Max job brings it in only when it needs more room.
- **Chat keeps answering during big jobs.** Standard and Boost run beside chat. A Max job may switch chat to a smaller model for a while, a line under the box says so, and chat goes back afterward. Stop a Max job at any time.
- **A fair queue.** One heavy job per person at a time, in order. A job that has to wait says why and starts by itself when there is room, or runs now at a lower level if you choose.
- **Three presets for sharing the computer.** Chat first, Balanced or Big jobs first. Free up memory in one press, and tune the Mac with changes you can revert.
- **See what Home is doing.** A System status page shows each app as working, degraded or down with a 90-day record, and Repairs offers one-press fixes.
- **Use a second computer for the AI.** Pair an Ubuntu machine on your network to run the engines while Home keeps your family settings.
- **Update with a way back.** Home checks for new versions each day. Update an engine part or Home itself, and go back one version if something breaks.
- **Back up and restore.** Make a backup, keep the backup key, and bring an older backup back.
- **Set storage limits per person,** with a Trash, and choose how much offline reference data to keep.
- **Choose the models.** Pick the model for each job, add styles, and see what every outside connection sends on the Privacy page.
- **Install in one line** on a Mac, with a setup wizard in your browser.

## Private by design

Nothing leaves your home unless you ask for it. The things that do go out are ones you start: web searches, weather lookups, a word or place lookup, downloads of models and updates, and a daily check for a new release. The privacy page in Settings lists every one and what it sends. There is no tracking, no crash reporting and no training on your family's words.

## Install

On the Mac that will run Home, open Terminal and run:

```bash
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/getmaipai/home/main/scripts/install.sh | sudo bash
```

Home is up in about a minute. Open the address it prints and the setup wizard installs the AI engine. Steps, upgrade, rollback and uninstall: [Install, upgrade, uninstall and fix](docs/user/install-upgrade-uninstall.md).

**What you need.** A Mac for chat, voice and music. Reading pictures needs 32 GB of memory or more. Making video needs a Mac with 128 GB. Each feature's guide page lists what it needs. This is an early release (0.2.x), first tested on a Mac Studio, so read the [changelog](CHANGELOG.md) before you upgrade.
